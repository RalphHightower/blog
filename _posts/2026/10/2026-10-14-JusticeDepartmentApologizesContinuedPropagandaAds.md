## [On taxpayer-funded midterm ads, Trump administration issues rare apology](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-apology-propaganda-midterm-elections)

_With the election commercials finally paused, the focus shifts to the need for reimbursements._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Oct. 13, 2026, 3:27 PM EDT

[Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) began last week with an unexpected announcement: Going forward, [the president said](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-super-pac-taxpayer-money-election-midterms-propaganda), he would stop using taxpayer money to finance the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s propagandistic campaign ads. Going forward, he said, his well-funded [super PAC](https://www.magapac.com/) would start paying for the commercials.

It seemed as if [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) didn’t have much choice: The ad campaign had generated [bipartisan criticism](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-propaganda-taxpayer-republicans-bipartisan-criticisms), and polls showed [overwhelming public opposition](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-super-pac-taxpayer-money-election-midterms-propaganda) to the misappropriation of public funds.

Except the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s vow proved meaningless, and evidence quickly emerged that the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) was [still using public funds](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/dnc-white-house-lawsuit-campaign-ad-midterms-propaganda) for the midterm propaganda days after [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had publicly committed to stop. This sparked [a pair of lawsuits](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/dnc-white-house-lawsuit-campaign-ad-midterms-propaganda) from the [Democratic National Committee](https://democrats.org/leadership/) and a coalition of groups, including the nonpartisan watchdog [Common Cause](https://www.commoncause.org/).

[U.S. District](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/) [Judge Jennifer L. Rochon](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-jennifer-l-rochon), responding to the coalition’s case, soon granted [a temporary restraining order](https://www.ms.now/news/judge-trump-administration-cancel-taxpayer-tv-ads) and directed the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) to cancel any remaining television commercials that had been paid for with taxpayer money.

Except that didn’t work out, either: Though the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) faced a deadline of late Sunday afternoon, publicly financed campaign ads [continued into Monday morning](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-court-order-propaganda-midterm-elections).

Was the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) deliberately ignoring a court order? Evidently not. Hours after the problem was exposed, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) did something it rarely does: It apologized, not to the public, but to the federal judge.

In a court filing, a [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) attorney expressed contrition and cited the Columbus Day holiday as a reason for the procedural confusion. The filing added:

> Defendants understand that they are enjoined from using, disbursing, or transferring funds appropriated to the [Department of Homeland Security](https://www.dhs.gov/) to develop, create, disseminate, place, distribute, publish, or air the challenged advertisements; Defendants understand that they shall cancel or cause to be cancelled any slot, placement, reservation, or arrangement for the airing or dissemination of the challenged advertisements.

At least for now, it would appear the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) understands it cannot use taxpayer money for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s campaign ads.

That said, there’s still the matter of the millions in public funds the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has already spent, and whether Americans [might expect to be reimbursed](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-white-house-reimburse-propaganda-campaign-ads-midterm-elections) for [Team Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s recent abuses.

For his part, [Homeland Security](https://www.dhs.gov/) [Secretary Markwayne Mullin](https://www.dhs.gov/markwayne-mullin) appeared on [“Face the Nation”](https://www.cbsnews.com/face-the-nation/) over the weekend and suggested to [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/)’ [Margaret Brennan](https://www.cbsnews.com/team/margaret-brennan/) that taxpayers would never get their money back.

“Any time we can do a public service announcement and brag about America, I’m all about it,” the [Oklahoma](https://oklahoma.gov/) [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) said, despite the obvious fact that the campaign ads [weren’t PSAs](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-propaganda-fcc-carr-taxpayer-money-midterm-elections) — one of the ads literally recycled one of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s campaign ads from two years ago — and the fact that the commercials bragged about [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), not the U.S.

This post updates our [related earlier coverage](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-court-order-propaganda-midterm-elections).
