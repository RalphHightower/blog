### Senator Darline Graham Interrogating Jack Smith

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/yc3HZUIkDZo?si=f-Oyhc0zgLVinaMe" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

[WASHINGTON](https://dc.gov/) – U.S. [Senator](https://www.senate.gov/) [Darline Graham (T-South Carolina)](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) today questioned former [Special Counsel Jack Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) at a [Senate Judiciary Committee](http://www.judiciary.senate.gov/) hearing. [Senator](https://www.senate.gov/) [Darline Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) grilled [Jack Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) for weaponizing the legal system and leading the politically motivated [Arctic Frost investigation](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith), where he subpoenaed private phone records of sitting members of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/).

[GRAHAM](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/): “The legal term for your behavior would be obstruction of justice. But in [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) we just call it a lie. This was undeniably a fishing expedition by you and your team to intimidate the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s allies in [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/).”  https://youtu.be/V5eBVtV1Dek?si=NvqbBj9Suk7i4ema&t=276

[GRAHAM](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/): “You should be ashamed of yourself. It’s also a damn shame that you weaponized our legal system to do this. To go after a political opponent and his allies, including [Lindsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1). What a disgrace to our justice system and the [Constitution](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/) that you would stoop this low. Shame on you.” https://youtu.be/V5eBVtV1Dek?si=sQN3v9As_VV-dlIt&t=425

[GRAHAM](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/): “Do you really believe that [Lindsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1) was going to destroy evidence that was not even in his possession?”

[FORMER SPECIAL COUNSEL JACK SMITH](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith): “No, I believe your brother was a patriotic public servant. He was not a target of our investigation…”

[GRAHAM](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/): “Then why does the gag order say that disclosure of the subpoena would result in destruction of or tampering with evidence?”

[FORMER SPECIAL COUNSEL JACK SMITH](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith): “Because disclosure of that subpoena could result in [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) attempting to intimidate witnesses as he had before and after. We had an ample basis.”

[GRAHAM](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/): “The evidence in question belonged to [Lindsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1) and was in the possession of [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/). It makes absolutely no sense that anyone would be able to destroy the evidence that you were seeking.” https://youtu.be/V5eBVtV1Dek?si=Zy5uIaRIJP1glop_&t=339

> Down here, we’ve got a saying: if you see hoofprints, you don’t go looking for zebras. You look for the horse that’s been running through your garden for years. And Lord knows, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s been trampling institutions like they were tomato plants.

> Folks around here know better than to pretend January 6 was just a misunderstanding. We watched the same footage as everybody else. We saw the attempt to overturn an election. We saw the intimidation. We saw the pressure campaigns. You can’t sweet‑tea your way out of that.

> In the South, we’ve got a long memory. When someone goes after law firms, universities, newspapers, comedians, and the [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/) — all because they didn’t kiss the ring — we don’t call that ‘weaponization.’ We call that throwing a fit and breaking the furniture.

> My granddaddy used to say, ‘If a man fires every person who tells him the truth, don’t be surprised when he starts hiring folks who only nod.’ That’s what [Patel](https://www.fbi.gov/about/leadership-and-structure/director-patel)’s purge was — clearing out anyone who dared investigate the boss.

> We’ve all met that one person who thinks every rule is optional if it inconveniences them. Turning the [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) into a personal law firm isn’t ‘defending yourself.’ It’s using the courthouse like it’s your cousin’s garage.

> Bless her heart, [Darline](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/)’s acting like [Jack Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) woke up one morning and decided to subpoena [Lindsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1) for sport. But down here, we know better: you don’t get an investigation without a reason, and you sure don’t get a gag order unless somebody’s been tampering with the pie before it cools.

> Down here, we know politics has always had sharp elbows. But we also remember when folks could fight all morning and still sit down to hash out a deal by supper. What we’re seeing now isn’t disagreement — it’s tribalism. And tribalism doesn’t just divide us; it convinces us that the other side isn’t wrong, but wicked. [Pogo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pogo_%28comic_strip%29?wprov=sfla1) had it right: the enemy isn’t out there somewhere. The enemy is what we’ve let ourselves become.
