## [Trump puts new Russia sanctions that he recently signed into law on hold, citing national interest](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-new-russia-sanctions)

_[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s unexpected decision comes a week after he said the U.S. had agreed to purchase diesel from [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) to try to bring down global energy prices._

By [The Associated Press](https://www.ms.now/author/the-associated-press)

Oct. 16, 2026, 11:13 PM EDT

[WASHINGTON](https://dc.gov/) ([AP](https://apnews.com/)) — [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://apnews.com/hub/donald-trump) on Friday signed an executive order that puts on hold a [sweeping new sanctions package](https://apnews.com/article/donald-trump-russia-sanctions-ukraine-lindsey-graham-d1715ad5e8feccba76bfae51c5f28c45) targeting the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) economy, telling [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) the pause was in the national interest.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) personally [signed the legislation](https://apnews.com/article/donald-trump-russia-sanctions-ukraine-lindsey-graham-d1715ad5e8feccba76bfae51c5f28c45) just last month and his move came just two days before a deadline set in the law itself for its implementation. It drew shift condemnation from congressional [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/).

Named after late [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Lindsey Graham](https://apnews.com/hub/lindsey-graham), the legislation had sought to clamp down more quickly on the flow of financial resources fueling the [war against Ukraine](https://apnews.com/hub/russia-ukraine).

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s unexpected decision comes a week after he stunningly said the U.S. had agreed to purchase diesel from [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) to try to bring down global energy prices, despite that potentially hurting [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/) in its war with [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/).

An [An AP-NORC Center for Public Affairs Research poll](https://apnorc.org/projects/most-blame-president-trump-for-higher-prices-and-are-unhappy-with-how-he-is-handling-the-economy/) poll published Oct. 1 found that Americans disapprove of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s handling of the [Iran](https://iran,gov/) war and blame him for higher prices. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s approval ratings have sunk to near-historic lows and pressure is mounting as [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) try to hold control of both chambers of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) after [midterm elections](https://apnews.com/projects/elections-2026/) on Nov. 3.

### Sanctions were seen as part of Graham’s legacy

[Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1), a close ally and friend of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), had [just returned from Ukraine when he unexpectedly](https://apnews.com/article/lindsey-graham-death-reactions-30c9758bfc124c30e8e4db0e4dd719e2) died in July. He had advocated for stricter U.S. sanctions against [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) as a way of pressuring it to end its war in [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/).

His colleagues in [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) responded with bipartisan support after his death and [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) attached his signature to the legislation — then changed his mind Friday.

The package had been meant to starve [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) [President](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) [Vladimir Putin](https://apnews.com/hub/vladimir-putin) of the resources [Moscow](https://www.mos.ru/) needs to continue its war in [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/) after invading its neighbor more than four years ago. The bill, also drafted by [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Richard Blumenthal (D-CT)(https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/), had sought to sanction [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/)n officials, banks and a shadow fleet of tankers that keeps [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) energy moving.

It also directed [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to impose up to 100% tariffs on the top five importers of [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oil or natural gas, with an exception for countries that import less than 15% of [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/)’s natural gas exports and have taken significant steps to reduce those imports.

[Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) said in a statement that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) was turning his back on “the majority of Americans, their representatives in both houses of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/), and our European allies.”

“These waivers have nothing to do with our national security, and everything to do with sucking up to [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/),” [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) said.

[Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Don Beyer (D-VA8)](https://beyer.house.gov/) noted on social media shortly after the announcement of the sanctions being suspended that he and others had “warned” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) might make such a move. The veteran congressman, who spoke out against the bill as it was debated, called on [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) to “fix this flawed law to stop [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) from capitulating to [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) and ensure that it truly supports [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/).”

The [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) did not immediately explain how pausing the sanctions was in the national interest. The decision was posted on the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) website late in the evening, and the notification noted that the report explaining the decision had been submitted to [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/).

Still, it comes as [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has increasingly lashed out at [Ukrainian](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/) [President](https://www.president.gov.ua/) [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://apnews.com/hub/volodymyr-zelenskyy), blaming him for failing to make a deal with [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) to end the fighting.

The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), meanwhile, had remained mostly quiet since the passage of the bill with large majorities in the [House](https://www.house.gov/) and [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) following [Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1)’s sudden death — with lawmakers urged ahead by [Zelenskyy](https://www.president.gov.ua/).

### Trump’s action follows announced diesel deal with Putin

Under last week’s agreement, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) would immediately supply more than 300,000 tons of diesel, followed by an additional 500,000 tons in November and 1 million tons “immediately thereafter.”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s striking a diesel deal with [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) has been [pilloried by Republicans and Democrats alike](https://apnews.com/article/trump-russian-diesel-putin-congress-ukraine-963624ed2ed30ae4a0259ffd1880fefa) alike who say it will help [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) execute its war against [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/) — without making a different to global fuel prices, which are elevated mostly due to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s war on [Iran](https://iran,gov/).

The sanctions that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has now suspended, meanwhile, also had become a campaign centerpiece for [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Darline Graham](https://apnews.com/article/south-carolina-election-lindsey-graham-03899983c3702842039e99d62e16ddad), the late senator’s sister who was appointed to serve out the remaining months in his current term. She is now running to replace him in the [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) more permanently.

[Darline Graham (T-SC)](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) said in a statement after [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) signed the bill, “I wish [Lindsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1) were here to celebrate this historic day.” In a debate earlier this week with [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) nominee [Annie Andrews](https://drannieandrews.com/), [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) — who has [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s endorsement and is expected to campaign with him in [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) next week — said she had been “so proud” to help get the measure passed.

She acknowledged, however, that it was the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s purview as to how he implemented it.

“It gives the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) tools that he can use if he so chooses, to end the war through economic sanctions,” [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) said Sunday during a debate in [Columbia](https://www.columbiasc.gov/). “So that’s up to him as to whether or not he wants to use it, or whether he wants to use something else, but I think the purpose of it is to bring down the gas prices and ultimately end the war. And when the war ends, gas prices will fall.”
