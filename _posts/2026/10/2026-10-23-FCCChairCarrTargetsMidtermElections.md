## [Trump's anti-free speech warrior Brendan Carr is coming for elections](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-polls-midterms-censorship-fcc-brendan-carr)

_Citing “fake polls,” [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) is helping lay the groundwork for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to — once again — falsely claim fraud in the event of a [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) midterms wipeout._

By [Anthony L. Fisher](https://www.ms.now/author/anthony-l-fisher)

Oct. 23, 2026, 6:00 AM EDT

[Federal Communications Commission](https://www.fcc.gov/)) [Chair Brendan Carr](https://www.ms.now/opinion/msnbc-opinion/trump-jimmy-kimmel-fcc-chair-brendan-carr-censorship-rcna232154) issued a threat this week that adds a sinister new dimension to the [Trump Censorship Industrial Complex](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-war-free-speech-abc-news).

Without citing specifics, [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) decried what he called “a significant surge in entirely Fake Polls and Suppression Polls designed to do nothing more than interfere with the regular functioning of the electoral process and alter the normal patterns of voter turnout and enthusiasm.”

“Broadcasters are advised to avoid engaging in News Distortions or running Broadcast Hoaxes by airing Polls that would violate their regulatory obligations,” [Carr warned Wednesday](https://x.com/BrendanCarrFCC/status/2112913475705934326).

It’s possible [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) was referring to news this week that an obscure group called Wedgewood Polls had propagated fraudulent polling data that, despite news outlets’ due diligence, made its way into polling aggregations at several outlets, including [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/21/us/midterm-election-fake-polling-wedgewood.html) and [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/). The problem of apparently malevolent actors fudging poll numbers before an election is not something to take lightly, but responsible news organizations have been transparent with their audiences, appending corrections, editor’s notes and updating their aggregations as needed. (The commission’s lone [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) appointee, [Anna Gomez](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/anna-gomez), [posted to X](https://x.com/AGomezFCC/status/2112934159609946512) on Wednesday, “[FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/) rules on news distortion or hoaxes do not apply to a station that reports this data in good faith.”)

> This would hardly be the first time [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) has used his office to threaten broadcasters from airing bad news about his boss.

But [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr)’s [track record as an anti-free-speech warrior for Trump](https://www.ms.now/opinion/brendan-carr-fcc-cpac-trump-viktor-orban) makes it hard to see his comments as an earnest effort to prevent the distribution of incorrect information before an election. In nearly two years as [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/) chair, [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) has demonstrated that he’s an [unprincipled, hyperpartisan hack](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-polls-hegseth-miller-praise) who cosplays as an advocate of free speech and limited government. Examples abound of him using his office to try to [silence Trump critics](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/09/18/arts/television/jimmy-kimmel.html) and [strong-arm](https://www.ms.now/opinion/brendan-carr-fcc-licenses-media-threats-first-amendment) risk-averse media corporations that prioritize nothing more than their bottom lines.

More likely, [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) is helping lay the groundwork for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to — [once again](https://www.ms.now/opinion/jan-6-trump-pardons-oath-keepers) — falsely claim fraud in the event of a [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) wipeout in the midterm elections, lest [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s [historic unpopularity](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-polls-unpopular-midterms-undecided-voters) be blamed for [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)s losing control of one or both houses of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) or any number of statehouses.

In March, as it became evident that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s war on [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) was destined to become an unwinnable quagmire, [Carr threatened broadcasters](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-fcc-chair-news-networks-iran-war-coverage) from airing “hoaxes and news distortions” in war coverage. Those words, in [MAGA](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)-speak, typically translate to “reports that accurately reflect the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s incompetence.”

[Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) also effectively censored [Jimmy Kimmel](https://abc.com/show/9bfe2f4f-41ad-4492-a6dd-0b67db180543) after the September 2025 assassination of [Charlie Kirk](https://www.charliekirk.com/) [by jawboning](https://www.ms.now/opinion/msnbc-opinion/trump-fcc-brendan-carr-disney-abc-bob-iger-letter-rcna185685) [ABC](https://www.abc.com/)’s corporate parent, [The Walt Disney Company](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/), into suspending the late-night comic, [even boasting](https://www.ms.now/opinion/msnbc-opinion/trump-jimmy-kimmel-fcc-chair-brendan-carr-censorship-rcna232154), “When we see stuff like this, look, we can do this the easy way or the hard way.” So egregious was this government gangsterism that no less a [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) lickspittle than [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Ted Cruz (T-TX)](https://www.cruz.senate.gov/) [condemned it as “mafioso” behavior](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/09/19/us/politics/ted-cruz-fcc-abc-jimmy-kimmel.html). When [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) wasn’t acting as the comedy police, he went after [ABC](https://www.abc.com/)’s daytime talk show [“The View,”](https://abc.com/show/70207ea9-cb32-454b-a519-b40106227e32) [claiming the program violated](https://firstamendment.mtsu.edu/post/abc-battles-fcc-regulators-in-dispute-over-the-view-equal-time-rules/) the [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/)’s antiquated “equal time” rules regarding political candidate interviews, despite the fact that talk shows had [historically been considered exempt from that regulation](https://www.mediainstitute.org/2026/01/22/fcc-late-night-shows-may-not-be-exempt-from-equal-time-rule/).

Sadly, the [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/)’s censorship campaign has been such a success that [ABC](https://www.abc.com/)’s lawyers claimed in court recently that the network’s news division has been tempering its election coverage to avoid further harassment by the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/).

This spring, [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) himself bragged about how effective [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s authoritarian attacks on the free press have been: “Look at the results so far. [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/) defunded. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/) defunded…\[[Stephen\] Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Colbert?wprov=sfla1) is leaving. [CBS](https://www.cbs.com/) is under new ownership. And soon enough, [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/) is going to have new ownership as well.” ([Skydance](https://www.skydance.com/), the company controlled by the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)-friendly billionaire trust fund baby [David Ellison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ellison?wprov=sfla1), now owns [CBS](https://www.cbs.com/) and [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/).)

> The [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/)’s censorship campaign has been such a success that [ABC](https://www.abc.com/)’s lawyers claimed in court recently that the network’s news division has been tempering its election coverage to avoid further harassment by the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/).

Thankfully for [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr), his efforts earned him a good-boy pat on the head from [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [at an Alaska campaign rally](https://www.thebulwark.com/p/brendan-carr-the-hackiest-hack-in-hackville-nathan-fielder-elizabeth-holmes-whalefall-haunted-hotel) this month, when the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) praised his [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/) attack poodle as “the most-feared man in government.” If you were wondering, yes, it is bizarre that the head of the historically bipartisan [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/) would be attending a partisan political rally. But then, [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) — who’s known for [wearing a lapel pin in the shape of Trump’s head](https://x.com/bennyjohnson/status/1907845931141087667) — broke decades of precedent when he told a [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) committee in December 2025 that [the FCC is not an independent agency](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/media/senate-testimony-fcc-chairman-says-agency-isnt-independent-rcna249724). References to “independent” have also been removed from the [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/)’s website.

Twelve former [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/) officials — who served in both [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) and [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/)s — [issued a joint statement](https://techfreedom.org/former-fcc-officials-criticize-brendan-carrs-threat-against-broadcasters-reporting-on-political-polls/) Wednesday condemning [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr)’s “dangerous and unconstitutional” attempt to “censor and control the information available to Americans as they prepare to vote.” They added, “Make no mistake: this is a direct attack on free and fair elections, which is especially concerning in the days and weeks when Americans are heading to the polls.”

In an X post Wednesday, [The Bulwark](https://www.thebulwark.com/)’s [Will Saletan](https://substack.com/@saletan/) [aptly summed up Carr’s pathology](https://x.com/saletan/status/2112957601315188964): “[Stephen Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Miller?wprov=sfla1) might look the part, but [Brendan Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) is actually [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s most dedicated authoritarian apparatchik. In a fascist regime, he would do anything the boss ordered.”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s recent claim that [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) is the “most feared” person in government is in keeping with the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s flattery of thugs, and [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) no doubt appreciated the ego-stroking. That’s probably overstating the case a bit — the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) does have high-ranking officials who [brag about committing likely war crimes](https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/Article/4434484/secretary-of-war-pete-hegseth-and-chairman-of-the-joint-chiefs-air-force-gen-da/), after all — but [Carr](https://www.fcc.gov/about/leadership/brendan-carr) is most certainly a giddy soldier in [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s war on the [Constitution](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/).
