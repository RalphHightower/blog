## [BBC lawyers accuse Trump of backing out of a deposition](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-bbc-defamation-lawsuit-deposition)

_The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s defamation case can’t advance until he sits down for a deposition, but he appears reluctant to do so._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Oct. 14, 2026, 1:59 PM EDT

[Donald Trump](https://www.ms.now/white-house)’s defamation case [against the BBC](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-defamation-lawsuits-bbc-cbs-new-york-times) is ongoing, though the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) doesn’t seem especially eager to sit down for a deposition in his own lawsuit.

Over the summer, attorneys for both sides negotiated a schedule, which included plans for an Oct. 16 deposition. Evidently, that plan has fallen apart. [Politico reported](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/13/Trump-deposition-BBC-lawsuit):

> [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) was set to be deposed Friday in a defamation lawsuit he filed against the [British Broadcasting Corporation](https://www.bbc.co.uk/), but his lawyers recently canceled the session and said he is unavailable through at least mid-December, according to a court filing.

> “The [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) is no longer available for his deposition on October 16 due to other matters of national importance,” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s attorneys told lawyers for the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) late last month, the network’s attorneys said in motion Tuesday. The [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) is asking a judge to force [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to set a firm date to submit to questioning in the case.

“Nearly eight months into discovery, after seven depositions of [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) witnesses, and with only two months of discovery remaining, the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) still has not been able to depose, and does not even have a schedule to depose, the most central figure in this case: Plaintiff himself,” [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) lawyer Charles Tobin wrote. “There is hardly a more crucial and relevant piece of discovery in a defamation lawsuit than the deposition of the Plaintiff.”

While [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has earned a reputation as a [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) who loves to file outlandish lawsuits against major news organizations, his gambit against the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) was weird even for him.

Late last year, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [sued the British broadcaster for \$10 billion](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-washington-post-defamation-lawsuit-news-organizations) over a news program that focused on the [Jan. 6 attack](https://youtu.be/WUB814y5ns0?is=-2OVQdmeSHAaQO2D). Defending the litigation, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [told reporters](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-sues-bbc-for-10-billion-over-edited-clips-of-his-jan-6-speech) that the network “literally put words in my mouth,” adding, “I guess they used AI. … They actually put terrible words in my mouth having to do with [Jan. 6](https://youtu.be/WUB814y5ns0?is=-2OVQdmeSHAaQO2D) that I didn’t say.”

That wasn’t quite right. For one thing, the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) didn’t use artificial intelligence. For another, the network did air a program that edited [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s Jan. 6 speech, connecting two clips that were delivered roughly 50 minutes apart, but the news outlet didn’t manufacture words or phrases out of whole cloth.

Nevertheless, the editing sparked a controversy that [led to an internal investigation](https://www.ms.now/top-stories/latest/bbc-apology-trump-jan-6-speech-editing-rcna243820), and the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) ultimately fired some senior officials and issued a written apology.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), however, apparently doesn’t want an apology. He wants \$10 billion.

That said, the case can’t and won’t proceed until the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) gives a deposition, and as things stand, he seems reluctant to help advance his own case. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s attorneys’ latest position is that he might be available [in the second or third week of January](https://www.washingtonsun.com/legal/trump-backed-out-of-bbc-deposition-jan-6-defamation-case).

The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has filed a great many dubious lawsuits against independent news organizations, [nearly all of which ended in failure](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-msnow-cnn-politico-white-house-free-press-court-order-ruling). There’s no reason to assume his case against the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) will fare any better.

This post updates our [related earlier coverage](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/bbc-trump-financial-records-defamation-lawsuit).
