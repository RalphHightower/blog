## [Trump targets Fed’s Lisa Cook with new inquiry panel](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-lisa-cook-federal-reserve-committee-inquiry-white-house)

_After the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s crusade against the [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) governor was blocked, he settled on a radical new course._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Oct. 9, 2026, 11:34 AM EDT

It was 14 months ago when Donald [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), exercising a power the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) didn’t appear to have, said he had [fired Federal Reserve governor Lisa Cook](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trumps-offensive-feds-lisa-cook-five-alarm-fire-rcna227188), effective immediately. His motivations were hardly subtle: He wanted to seize greater control over the [Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/), in part by ousting a [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) appointee and replacing her with a choice of his own.

To date, those efforts have come up short: As recently as June, the [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/) [blocked the White House’s efforts](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house/deadline-legal-blog/supreme-court-federal-trade-commission-rebecca-slaughter-humphreys) to remove [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) from office immediately, allowing her to remain on the job while the underlying case continued to be adjudicated.

Days later, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [indicated to CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/cnbc-transcript-president-donald-trump-speaks-with-cnbcs-joe-kernen-today-.html) that he would continue to try to remove [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm). Evidently, he was serious. [Politico reported](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/09/donald-trump-lisa-cook-fed-investigation-01113667):

> [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) established a committee of inquiry to investigate whether [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) [Gov. Lisa Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) made “false statements in connection with one or more mortgage instruments,” his latest effort to remove her from the central bank.

“As [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), it is my job to ensure the laws are faithfully executed, including by firing subordinates who cannot be trusted to tell the truth and follow the law,” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) wrote in a memo.

To be sure, it’s awfully tempting to spend the next several paragraphs relentlessly mocking [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) for suggesting that he cares about honesty and the importance of strict adherence to the law. It’s also tempting to once again make clear that the allegations surrounding [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) and her mortgage documents remain [awfully difficult to take seriously](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/mortgage-fraud-allegations-generate-even-political-headaches-team-trum-rcna231929).

But while that might be satisfying, what matters most in this instance is the breathtaking abuse that’s unfolding before our eyes: The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) set out to fire a [Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) member without cause; he faced institutional and legal resistance; and now he’s exploring new ways to advance his crusade, regardless of the evidence or possible concerns about propriety.

The [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/), which characterized [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s gambit as “extraordinary,” [reported](https://www.washingtonsun.com/whitehouse/trump-committee-of-inquiry-federal-reserves-lisa-cook) that the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s [new memo](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/establishment-of-a-committee-of-inquiry-to-investigate-allegations-of-false-statements-by-lisa-denell-cook/) directs [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) to testify early next month before this newly established committee. A [CNBC report](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/09/fed-lisa-cook-trump.html) added that the panel will include [Kevin Hassett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Hassett?wprov=sfla1), a brazen [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) loyalist who leads the [National Economic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Economic_Council_%28United_States%29?wprov=sfla1).

There were already concerns about the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) trying to undermine the [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/)’s independence. Those concerns are about to reach unprecedented heights.
