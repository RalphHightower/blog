## [Why the BBC wants to depose Jack Smith, and why Team Trump wants to stop them](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-bbc-lawsuit-jack-smith-deposition)

_“It is difficult to imagine a more authoritative source on these issues than Mr. [Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith),” the network’s lawyers explained in a court filing._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Oct. 20, 2026, 11:27 AM EDT

In hindsight, it seems like [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) might not have fully thought through the consequences of [his bizarre $10 billion lawsuit against the BBC](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-defamation-lawsuits-bbc-cbs-new-york-times), which continues to move forward in ways the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)ial plaintiff doesn’t seem to like.

Attorneys for the British broadcaster, for example, recently subpoenaed former [special counsel Jack Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) to testify.

“It is difficult to imagine a more authoritative source on these issues than Mr. [Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith), who spent years gathering evidence on those topics with the force of the [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) and the power of the grand jury behind him,” the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) said in a [court filing](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.flsd.703382/gov.uscourts.flsd.703382.189.0_1.pdf).

The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) and his lawyers apparently don’t see it that way. [The New Republic reported](https://newrepublic.com/post/216805/trump-jack-smith-testimony-bbc-defamation-lawsuit-implodes):

> [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) doesn’t want former special counsel [Jack Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) to testify in a deposition as part of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s \$10 billion defamation lawsuit against the [British Broadcasting Company](https://www.bbc.co.uk/).

The [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) has subpoenaed [Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) to defend itself from [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s accusations that the network defamed him with deceptive editing in a 2024 documentary about the [January 6, 2021, Capitol insurrection](https://youtu.be/WUB814y5ns0?is=-2OVQdmeSHAaQO2D). In a court filing Friday, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s lawyers tried to fight the subpoena by claiming that “[Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) lacks any personal knowledge regarding the issues in this matter.”

In response, the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/)’s attorneys wrote, “No one forced Plaintiff to file this lawsuit and to dispute that his speech on [January 6](https://youtu.be/WUB814y5ns0?is=-2OVQdmeSHAaQO2D) incited the violence that followed at the [Capitol](https://www.congress.gov/). But as long as he chooses to maintain the case, he cannot commit the fundamental injustice of preventing the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) from obtaining the evidence it needs to robustly defend itself on every element of his claim, including Plaintiff’s burden to prove falsity.”

To recap briefly, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [sued the British broadcaster for \$10 billion](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-washington-post-defamation-lawsuit-news-organizations) late last year over a news program that focused on the [Jan. 6 attack](https://youtu.be/WUB814y5ns0?is=-2OVQdmeSHAaQO2D). Defending the litigation, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [told reporters](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-sues-bbc-for-10-billion-over-edited-clips-of-his-jan-6-speech) that the network “literally put words in my mouth,” adding, “I guess they used AI. … They actually put terrible words in my mouth having to do with Jan. 6 that I didn’t say.”

That wasn’t quite right. For one thing, the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) didn’t use artificial intelligence. For another, the network did air a program that edited [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s [Jan. 6 speech](https://youtu.be/WUB814y5ns0?is=-2OVQdmeSHAaQO2D), connecting two clips delivered roughly 50 minutes apart; but the news outlet didn’t manufacture words or phrases out of whole cloth.

The editing nevertheless sparked a controversy that [led to an internal investigation](https://www.ms.now/top-stories/latest/bbc-apology-trump-jan-6-speech-editing-rcna243820), and the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) ultimately fired some senior officials and issued a written apology.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), however, apparently didn’t want to settle for an apology. He wants \$10 billion.

The case, however, has proven rather inconvenient for the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) who filed it. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/) lawyers want to depose the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) as part of the proceedings, but [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has already [backed out of a scheduled deposition](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-bbc-defamation-lawsuit-deposition) and appears reluctant to answer questions about his own case.

As [The New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/) report added, “His attorneys are also fighting attempts to … [children to testify](https://newrepublic.com/post/214418/trump-kids-hide-subpoenas-bbc-defamation-lawsuit-backfires) and to dive into [his finances](https://newrepublic.com/post/214034/trump-tries-pause-bbc-lawsuit-hide-finances).”

Now those same lawyers want to prevent the former special counsel, who has plenty to say about [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s [Jan. 6](https://youtu.be/WUB814y5ns0?is=-2OVQdmeSHAaQO2D) efforts, from answering related questions, further delaying the case that the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) ostensibly wants to proceed.

The judge has scheduled a hearing for later this week to consider questions about the [Smith](https://www.justice.gov/archives/sco-smith) deposition and discovery related to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s [Mar-a-Lago](https://www.maralagoclub.com/) revenue, which would help shed light on whether the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has suffered damages as the result of the alleged defamation. Watch this space.
