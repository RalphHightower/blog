## [Fiery golf course hearing ends with a bang: Judge calls for a probe into government lawyers](https://www.ms.now/news/fiery-golf-course-hearing-ends-with-a-bang-judge-calls-for-a-probe-into-government-lawyers)

_[U.S. District](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/) [Judge Ana Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) says [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) lawyers have given her false information in the case deciding the fate of a D.C. golf course._

By [Fallon Gallagher](https://www.ms.now/author/fallon-gallagher) and [Selena Kuznikov](https://www.ms.now/author/selena-kuznikov)

Oct. 30, 2026, 2:55 PM EDT

A federal judge who has openly sparred with government attorneys throughout the case of a D.C. golf course is launching a formal inquiry into their conduct to determine if they misrepresented [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s plans, which he has publicly announced on social media.

At the end of a two-and-a-half-hour hearing Friday, [U.S. District](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/) [Judge Ana Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) said the court could conduct the investigation itself or appoint a special master to do so.

She said a probe would focus on whether [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) lawyers have committed any ethical violations during proceedings, including if they have made false statements, withheld testimony or interfered with witness testimony.

[Associate Attorney General](https://www.justice.gov/) [Stanley Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/) and other lawyers representing the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) have repeatedly argued in court that there is no final plan to close and renovate [East Potomac](https://www.playdcgolf.com/east-potomac-golf-links/) — which [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) intends to transform into a championship-style golf course — despite the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s [Truth Social posts](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/117462306089931035) earlier this month that have announced it would close on Nov. 15.

Two days ago, [Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/) [informed](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.289401/gov.uscourts.dcd.289401.107.0_2.pdf) the court that the [Department of Interior](https://www.doi.gov/) was “working to develop a plan” to close the course — on or around Nov. 15.

The disconnect between the statements in her courtroom and [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s statements on social media frustrated [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes), who repeatedly asked [Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/) if the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s post meant that he was directing the [DOI](https://www.doi.gov/) and [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/index.htm) to close the course on that date.

[Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/) responded that we could not know what the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) meant by the post and could not opine on his choice of words. [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) was not convinced.

“You are telling me, as the third-highest-ranking official at the [Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/) … you are saying to me, ‘do not believe the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/).’”

In response to today’s hearing, a [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) spokesperson told [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/), “The [Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/) will not be deterred from executing on the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s agenda.”

Throughout the fiery hearing, [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) questioned [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) attorneys on if they misled the court about plans for the golf course. When asked when work began on a submission of a renovation proposal to the [National Capital Planning Commission](http://www.ncpc.gov/), [Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/) said there has been consideration of various plans for renovation “for some time now.”

[Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) asked if [Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/) was telling her that no [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) counsel decided to inform the court of plans that had been ongoing for weeks to send a proposal to the [NCPC](http://www.ncpc.gov/). [Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/) responded that he did not recall the court asking a question about whether they were coming up with a plan to send.

“Are you kidding me right now?” [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) erupted. “Seriously [Mr. Woodward](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stanleywoodward/), come on.”

While [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) concluded the hearing with her announcement of an investigation into the [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) lawyers’ conduct, she emphasized the court has not yet determined they committed any ethical violations.

[Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) has made no secret of her disappointment with how the [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) lawyers have conducted themselves in the case, in one previous hearing telling them, “[You did your absolute worst.”](https://www.ms.now/news/you-did-your-absolute-worst-judge-blasts-government-after-she-says-it-defied-her-on-dc-golf-course-case)”

On Monday, [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) [indefinitely blocked](https://www.ms.now/news/judge-blocks-d-c-golf-course-tree-removal-orders-government-to-give-notice-before-more-changes) the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) from removing even a single tree from the course in [Washington, D.C.](https://dc.gov/) She has ordered the government to provide notice of at least 10 business days before any closure or “irreversible modification” to [East Potomac Park](https://www.playdcgolf.com/east-potomac-golf-links/).

In the scathing [70-page ruling](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.289401/gov.uscourts.dcd.289401.103.0_1.pdf), [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) scolded the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) for the disparity between [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s statements and those of his lawyers.

“Rarely, if ever, has the [United States Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/) urged a federal court not to believe the sitting [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/). Not to believe him that a cabinet secretary has ‘developed’ a federal land project. Not to believe him that the secretary’s agency will destroy a historic golf course already on the land. Not to believe him that ‘Restoration work’ will begin ‘IMMEDIATELY!’ Not to believe him that the course ‘will be closed’ for over a year. Not to believe what the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has repeatedly told the American public,” [Reyes](https://www.dcd.uscourts.gov/content/district-judge-ana-c-reyes) wrote.

“And yet, here we are.”

The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) appealed that ruling and the case remains pending.
