## [Trump secretly swapped planes at NATO summit due to security threat](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-plane-swap-air-force-one-catering-truck-turkey)

_The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) boarded [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) but quickly swapped planes following an [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) threat. To arrive in [Turkey](https://global.tbmm.gov.tr/), [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had used a new plane gifted to him by the [Qatari](https://hukoomi.gov.qa/) government with fewer security features._

Aug. 11, 2026, 1:36 PM EDT
By [Jake Traylor](https://www.ms.now/author/jake-traylor) and [Erum Salam](https://www.ms.now/author/erum-salam)

After boarding [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) last month in [Turkey](https://global.tbmm.gov.tr/), [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) exited that airplane and embarked on another one in an extraordinary act of subterfuge aimed at avoiding a threat from [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), according to a [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) official.

To pull off the maneuver, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) **secretly boarded a catering truck** after entering [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) in view of press cameras. The catering truck was positioned so that the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) could not be seen exiting the [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) plane, which contained some [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) staff and media traveling with [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to a [NATO](https://www.nato.int/) [summit](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/events/event-programmes/2026/07/nato-summit-2026?selectedLocale=).

The [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/) [first reported](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/08/10/trump-flew-secrecy-amid-iran-threat-air-force-one-became-decoy/) the plane swap, which a [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) official confirmed to [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/). The official was granted anonymity to share details of the clandestine mission.

[Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) on the [House Intelligence Committee](https://intelligence.house.gov/), who said they were not informed about the threat that required the switch, have requested an immediate briefing from the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) on the matter.

“We were not briefed,” ranking member [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Jim Himes (D-CT4)](https://himes.house.gov/). said. “There has been a lot of threat reporting ever since the killing of [Soleimani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasem_Soleimani?wprov=sfla1). But nothing specific to this moment.”

A spokesperson for [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Mark Warner (D-VA)](https://www.warner.senate.gov/), the ranking [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/) on the [Senate Intelligence committee](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/), said he had also not been briefed.

> _Only a small circle of people was aware that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) boarded one plane and then secretly transferred to another, according to a [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) official._

Only a small circle of people were aware that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) boarded one plane and then secretly transferred to another, according to the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) official. Journalists traveling as part of the press pool on the initial plane were also not told, and the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) did not disclose the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) was leaving [Turkey](https://global.tbmm.gov.tr/) on a different plane.

In addition to journalists on board the decoy aircraft, there were also several [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) staffers. According to the official, “most staffers on the plane did not know” that the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) was not on board.

The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) arrived for the [NATO](https://www.nato.int/) [summit](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/events/event-programmes/2026/07/nato-summit-2026?selectedLocale=) on the new [Qatari](https://hukoomi.gov.qa/)-gifted [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/), but for the first leg of his return trip home, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) flew on the older version of [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/).

After the plane switch, [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) [reported](https://www.ms.now/news/officials-say-trump-switched-out-new-air-force-one-over-security-concerns) that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) swapped out his new [Qatar-gifted Air Force One](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-new-air-force-one-qatar-jet) for his flight from the [NATO](https://www.nato.int/) [summit](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/events/event-programmes/2026/07/nato-summit-2026?selectedLocale=) to the [United Kingdom](https://www.gov.uk/) over concerns that the gifted aircraft lacks necessary secure communications systems and military defenses that the original 1990s-era [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) has.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) initially denied that the plane switch was related to security concerns when asked during his closing press conference in [Ankara](https://www.ankara.bel.tr/seffaflik-ve-hesapverebilirlik). He said the reason was to allow the new plane to be seen by [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) troops at a [U.K.](https://www.gov.uk/) military base.

The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) also acknowledged that he was a primary target for [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) —and later reporting from [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/) confirmed that there was a credible threat from [Iran to Trump involving an airplane](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/24/us/politics/trump-plane-iran-threat.html?eafs_enabled=false). The [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) and [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) are engaged in a war that is coming up on the six-month mark.

[White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) communications director Steven Cheung on Tuesday defended the security features of the [Qatari](https://hukoomi.gov.qa/)-gifted plane.

“The new [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) is a state-of-the-art aircraft that has been fitted with high-level security protocols that ensure the safety of the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) and his staff,” Cheung said in a statement to [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) when asked about the reported plane swap. “As the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has said recently, there are many enemies of [America](https://www.usa.gov/) who have their sights on him, and we use every tool at our disposal to address those threats.”

Once on board the original [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) in [Ankara](https://www.ankara.bel.tr/seffaflik-ve-hesapverebilirlik), journalists were advised to keep their window shades in the press cabin closed, according to notes sent on behalf of the traveling press pool. In a subsequent [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) pool note, one journalist reported that a photographer opened their shade but was quickly instructed to close it again, at the request of [Secret Service](https://www.secretservice.gov/).

During the return trip from the [U.K.](https://www.gov.uk/) to the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) on the new [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/), the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) was asked about those security precautions, according to the traveling press. He responded, “Well, yeah, because you’re probably on a dangerous flight because of the sleazebags we have to deal with.”

Referring to threats from [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) added, “I’m number one on their list, before you. But if I go, you go. Right? Perhaps someday you want to change professions.”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [debuted the nearly $400 million jet](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-new-air-force-one-qatar-jet) last month ahead of the Fourth of July weekend for a trip to [North Dakota](https://www.nd.gov/). He called it “the world’s most luxurious plane,” and he has repeatedly bashed the older [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/).
