## [‘Mission Accomplished’: 6 months into the war with Iran, Trump embraces the wrong phrase](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-mission-accomplished-iran-war-six-months-bush-iraq)

_For a generation, the two-word phrase has served as a warning to [presidents](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), tempted by hubris. It’s a lesson [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has chosen not to learn._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)
Aug. 27, 2026, 9:04 AM EDT

On Feb. 28, [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) launched the war with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), a conflict that the American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [expected to last about four weeks](https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-war-goals-six-months-bb91832b61fa98fe12790d0a887719e0). Six months later, there’s no end in sight, and the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) who carelessly started an unnecessary military offensive appears to have no idea how to end the debacle of his own making.

As a new [Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/six-consequences-six-months-war-iran-2026-08-27/) summarized, “Six months after the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) and [Israel](https://www.gov.il/) launched strikes on [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), the war that [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has called a ‘little excursion’ has become a deeply unpopular stalemate that threatens to derail his presidency.”

With this in mind, he’s apparently embraced the one phrase he should go out of his way to avoid. [The New York Times reported](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/08/26/us/trump-news/82ba712d-b462-54d5-b465-18fc2207ed0b?smid=url-share):

> _[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) shared on [Liars Club](https://truthsocial.com/) what appeared to be an A.I.-rendered image of him standing over two signs in a war zone. One sign, next to former [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [George W. Bush](https://georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov/), stated, “MISSION NOT ACCOMPLISHED 2001-2021.” Another sign, next to what appeared to be [Iran](https://irangov.ir/)’s former [supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khameneii](https://www.leader.ir/), read, “MISSION ACCOMPLISHED 2026.” It was unclear what [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) meant by the post._

One of the things that stood out from [the message](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/117162623815523911) was the specific time frame, 2001 through 2021, that the incumbent [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) derided as a failure. It was a curious choice, since it included [Trump](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/)’s first term but not [Joe Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov)’s term.

More important, however, was that the online item was also plainly wrong. A litany of words come to mind when describing the U.S. mission in [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), but “accomplished” isn’t among them.

The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) launched the war convinced that he could simply bomb [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) until it agreed to an [“unconditional surrender.”](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/the-problems-with-trumps-demand-for-irans-unconditional-surrender) He similarly set out to achieve five specific objectives, nearly all of which [have gone unmet](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-iran-deal-strait-hormuz-already-toll-free). As the aforementioned [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/) report added, “[Iran](https://irangov.ir/) has not surrendered, shipping through the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1) remains severely disrupted, and there is no clear path to ending the conflict that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) once predicted would be over in four weeks.”

But let’s also take a moment to consider [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s adoption of a phrase he really ought to remember.

Whether he understands this or not, “Mission Accomplished” was the text on the banner above [George W. Bush](https://georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov/)’s head in 2003 when the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) declared the end of “major combat operations” in [Iraq](https://ur.gov.iq/). [Bush](https://georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov/) never literally spoke the words “mission accomplished” in his remarks, but their prevalence in his carefully choreographed event was unsubtle — and the phrase haunted the rest of his presidency.

Conditions in [Iraq](https://ur.gov.iq/) deteriorated, the death toll mounted, and the arguments in support of the invasion evaporated, and that two-word banner became a byword for the premature celebration of a conflict that was at its beginning, not its end.

“Mission Accomplished” became a warning to future [presidents](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) tempted by hubris. (Even [Bush](https://georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov/) himself later conceded the message was a “mistake.”) It’s a warning [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has either failed to understand or chosen to ignore.

As for the political and electoral context, with 10 weeks remaining before the midterm elections, I’d love to know how many [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) incumbents and candidates are willing to endorse the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s absurd boast about the deadly, destabilizing war.
