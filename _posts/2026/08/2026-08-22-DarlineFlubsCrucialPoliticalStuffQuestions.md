## [With time running out, Darline Graham’s attempt at damage control falls far short](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/darline-graham-south-carolina-debate-senate-election-taiwan-hannity)

_If her [Faux News](https://www.foxnews.com/) interview was meant to allay concerns about whether she’s up to the job, it was the [senator](https://www.senate.gov/)’s second missed opportunity in as many days._

Aug. 20, 2026, 9:53 AM EDT
By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Ever since [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Darline Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) decided to seek a full term of her own on [Capitol Hill](https://www.congress.gov/), the [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) [Republican](https://sc.gop/) has confronted unavoidable questions about her qualifications. She was appointed to fill her brother’s vacancy on [Capitol Hill](https://www.congress.gov/), but as an actual [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) candidate, [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) has had to prove that she is up to the task of being more than just the late [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Lindsey Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1)’s sister.

This week she faced a crucial test. With one week remaining before [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) [Republicans](https://sc.gop/) chose a new U.S. [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) candidate for this year’s ballot, [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) participated in a debate against her intraparty rival, [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Ralph Norman](https://norman.house.gov/). It was a unique opportunity to silence critics and prove she’s ready to serve in the institution known as the world’s most deliberative body.

With the pressure on, [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) flubbed the test.

Asked a straightforward question about U.S. policy toward [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/) and the [South China Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_China_Sea?wprov=sfla1), [the appointed senator conceded](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/darline-graham-debate-south-carolina-norman), “I’m not on national security,” adding, “National security is not my thing.” (As part of the same debate, [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) [also said](https://x.com/RonFilipkowski/status/2089893090219663770), of her own home state, that [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) has a population of “50 million people.” She was off by about 45 million.)

@RalphHightower: Hon, you betta get up to speed on national security. [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/) is a free republic. Communist [China](https://www.gov.cn/) claims that it’s theirs. [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/) is a major manufacturer of advanced computer chips.

@RalphHightower: 50 million people! That would make us bigger than [California](https://www.ca.gov/) with a population of 39,355,309 people. Your imagination is on scale with [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).

The morning after the debate, [Norman](https://norman.house.gov/), [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov)’s rival, told [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/)’s [Mychael Schnell](https://www.ms.now/author/mychael-schnell) that he “couldn’t believe it” when he heard her onstage comments. “It’s a little bit shocking,” the [congressman](https://norman.house.gov/) added.

As other prominent [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) [joined the pile-on](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/08/19/darline-graham-taiwan-flub-south-carolina-senate-01042395), making the case that [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) had effectively [proved her critics right](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/darline-graham-questions-experience-shaky-debate-answer-china-rcna593354) about her lack of preparation, the appointed [senator](https://www.senate.gov/) went into damage control, seeking safe harbor. Naturally, this led her to [Sean Hannity](https://www.foxnews.com/person/h/sean-hannity)’s [Faux News](https://www.foxnews.com/) program.

At the beginning of the relatively brief segment, the host, whose close association with the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) is hardly a secret, did his best to downplay what had happened the night before.

“So this issue of [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/) came up last night, and I know people have made a big deal about it,” [Hannity](https://www.foxnews.com/person/h/sean-hannity) said. “And I’m like, ‘I wonder how many people around the country, if I gave them a map, could pick out [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/)?’ I don’t think many. And not just, I mean, the whole country — never mind [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/).”

“Right,” [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) replied. “I think you’re 100% right, [Sean](https://www.foxnews.com/person/h/sean-hannity).”

That’s certainly one way to look at this. The other is to realize that while many Americans may not be able to find [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/) on a map, this is why we have a representative form of government in the first place. We hire people to powerful federal offices who are supposed to be qualified and knowledgeable, with the expectation that they can address the complex issues that regular folks aren’t familiar with.

The [senator](https://www.senate.gov/) added, “You know, I tripped up on one question last night regarding [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/). But you know, I’ve said many times, I’m not a politician, certainly not a career politician. I don’t speak politician. You know, I was just honest. And I think that’s kind of a foreign concept.”

Except [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) is a politician — she’s currently a sitting [senator](https://www.senate.gov/) who is currently running to continue in that powerful political office — and her answer wasn’t just unpolished; it was ignorant.

As the short interview continued, the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) said, “I think people in [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) are more worried about making ends meet. They’re concerned about boys in girls’ sports. One lady talked to me extensively about that today.”

Amid tensions between a U.S. ally and our most important global rival, and as much of the world questions whether the war in [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) has weakened our ability to extend security assistance to our global partners, [Lindsey Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1)’s sister apparently thinks what really matters is [transgender kids playing sports](https://www.ms.now/11th-hour/watch/part-1-a-special-panel-discussion-on-transgender-youth-in-athletics-194634309980).

@RalphHightower: Darline, Darline, we've got more pressing issues facing America than transgender kids playing kickball and marbles. 

If the interview was intended to calm concerns about whether [Darline Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov) is up to the job, it was the second missed opportunity in as many days.
