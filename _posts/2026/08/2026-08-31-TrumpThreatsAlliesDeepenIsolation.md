## [6 months into Iran war, Trump’s threats to allies deepen U.S. isolation](https://www.ms.now/news/news-analysis/iran-war-diplomacy-global-alliances-trump-canada-strait-hormuz)

_Despite requests from the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) for support, many traditional U.S. allies have stayed on the sidelines. [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has responded with threats and humiliation._

By [Vaughn Hillyard](https://www.ms.now/author/vaughn-hillyard)
Aug. 31, 2026, 5:00 AM

One month before the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) launched its military offensive against [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/)’ most vital global partners convened at [World Economic Forum](https://www.weforum.org/) in [Davos](https://www.gemeindedavos.ch/), [Switzerland](https://www.admin.ch/gov/), and [openly scrutinized](https://www.ms.now/news/news-analysis/trump-davos-allies-appeasement-greenland) the reliability of the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) as a trusted ally. [Canadian](https://www.canada.ca/) [Prime Minister](https://www.pm.gc.ca/) [Mark Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about) warned the other world leaders and prominent business executives that they should resist the urge to “go along to get along, to accommodate, to avoid trouble, to hope that compliance will buy safety.”

[Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about) stated clearly: “It won’t.”

Those diplomatic cracks have only deepened over the course of the six-month war as [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) uses the same tone of threats as leverage against allies and adversaries alike.

When [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) started the war, [Israel](https://www.gov.il/) was the only nation to join the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) in battle. Since then, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has scoffed at the unwillingness of [America](https://www.usa.gov/)’s traditional global allies to contribute to both military and logistics support against [Iran](https://irangov.ir/).

Instead, [America](https://www.usa.gov/)’s longtime military, diplomatic and trading partners have only further solidified their postures shunning the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) — heeding  the urgings of [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/)’s [Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about). In response, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has abruptly shifted the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) further toward isolationism rather than reassuring weary allies.

Last week, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) placed new 50% tariffs on a broad range of [Canadian](https://www.canada.ca/) goods after the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) accused [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/), one of its top trading partners, of discriminating against [American](https://www.usa.gov/) products.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) called its northern neighbor “among the worst nations in the world to deal with,” adding on [Liars Club](https://truthsocial.com/), “WE DON’T NEED [CANADA](https://www.canada.ca/), THEY NEED US!”

[Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/) [Secretary Sean Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) justified the move by mocking [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) and  falsely asserting that [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) security forces “provide their security for them.”

“It’s a country that doesn’t have a military,” [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) told [Faux News](https://www.foxnews.com/). In reality, [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/)’s military joined the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) effort in [Afghanistan](https://moi.gov.af/) after the 2001 terrorist attacks, leading to [158 [Canadian](https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/remembrance/memorials/canada/national-monument-canadas-mission-afghanistan/fallen-canadian-armed-forces-members) soldiers dying in defense of the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/)

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), facing further headwinds in his war effort, also sought to spite ally [South Korea](https://plus.gov.kr/) for its lack of assistance to the war effort.

“I said, ‘Would you like to give us a little hand? We don’t need help with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), but if you’d like, give us a hand with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/).’ He said, ‘No thanks,’” recounted [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) about a phone call with [South Korean](https://plus.gov.kr/) [President](https://www.president.go.kr/) [Lee Jae Myung](https://en.president.go.kr/president).

The [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) subsequently announced that it would cut its joint military exercises with [South Korea](https://plus.gov.kr/), held annually to deter [North Korea](http://www.naenara.com.kp/). The cancellation came even as [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) publicly revealed that [North Korea](http://www.naenara.com.kp/) “has 57 very powerful nuclear weapons.”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s criticism of [South Korea](https://plus.gov.kr/) came less than a year after the country offered lucrative concessions to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) — \$300 billion in [American](https://www.usa.gov/) investments and cash — in exchange for slightly lowering the tariff rate on the country, from 25% to 15%.

The expansiveness of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s hostility to allies has extended to its key Gulf partners as well, including [Oman](https://www.oman.om/), whose diplomatic mediators were integral to the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s nuclear negotiations with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) in the year prior to the Feb. 28 attack.

After [Oman](https://www.oman.om/) announced a temporary plan with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) to open part of the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1) to traffic, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) responded, “[Oman](https://www.oman.om/) will behave just like everybody else, or we’ll have to blow them up. … They understand that.”

Notably, [Oman](https://www.oman.om/) tried to steer the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) away from an all-out military campaign against [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) in the summer of 2025, and again before its six-month engagement.

In the 72 hours prior to the Feb. 28 strikes, [Omani](https://www.oman.om/) [Foreign Minister](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/) [Badr Al Busaidi](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/ministry/about-us/key-figures/#badr-albusaidi) met with special envoy [Steve Witkoff](https://www.witkoff.com/) and [Jared Kushner](), [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s son-in-law, in [Geneva](https://www.geneve.ch/) to help the indirect talks with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) progress toward technical negotiations. [Al Busaidi](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/ministry/about-us/key-figures/#badr-albusaidi) also flew overnight to [Washington, D.C.](https://dc.gov/), for [a last-hour appeal](https://www.ms.now/news/vice-president-jd-vance-set-to-host-high-stakes-iran-meeting) to [Vice President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/jd-vance/) [JD Vance](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jd-vance-770a9047/).

Now, the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) strategy — and lack of diplomatic coordination on [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) — is having a direct impact.

[Retired Gen. Barry McCaffrey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/general-barry-mccaffrey/), a retired four-star [Army](https://www.army.mil/) general and an [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) national security analyst, suggested in a [social media post](https://x.com/mccaffreyr3/status/2091313067200266407?s=20) that the war with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) has “probably” cost the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) access “to 15 Persian Gulf bases.”

“[Iran](https://irangov.ir/) [is] now poised to control access [of] the Gulf States,” [McCaffrey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/general-barry-mccaffrey/) said. “US will have to negotiate new access in western [Saudi Arabia](https://my.gov.sa/), [Israel](https://www.gov.il/), southern Europe.”

Among the other partners facing intensified pressures from [Washington](https://dc.gov/) is [Turkey](https://global.tbmm.gov.tr/), a [NATO](https://www.nato.int/) ally that [Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/) [Secretary Scott Bessent](https://home.treasury.gov/about/general-information/officials/scott-bessent) [threatened](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/us-iran-secondary-sanctions-china-india-uae-hormuz-trade-.html) with hefty tariffs if it, along with other countries, maintained trading relations worth billions with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/).

The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s threats and economic attacks on these partners come as the global oil market reels from months of high prices. At home in the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/), gas prices for consumers across the country currently sit at an average of more than \$4 a gallon — a price point that has become a gas station norm.

At the same forum in [Davos](https://www.gemeindedavos.ch/) last January, [French](https://www.info.gouv.fr/) [President](https://www.elysee.fr/) [Emmanuel Macron](https://www.elysee.fr/emmanuel-macron) laid bare his direct assessment that the old global order of the last 80 years is “shifting to a world without rules.” “Imperial ambitions are resurfacing,” [Macron](https://www.elysee.fr/emmanuel-macron) said, a nod to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s go-it-alone expansionist motivations.

Just over five weeks after [Macron](https://www.elysee.fr/emmanuel-macron)’s and [Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about)’s speeches warning of an [American](https://www.usa.gov/) interventionist future, the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/) — without informing its European or Gulf allies — launched its military assault on [Iran](https://irangov.ir/). Six months later, it remains to be seen how vestiges of that global alliance can endure given the absence of the [U.S.](https://www.usa.gov/), and its attacks on allies.

[Vaughn Hillyard](https://www.ms.now/author/vaughn-hillyard) is a senior White House reporter for MS NOW.
