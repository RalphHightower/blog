## [Pentagon picks the wrong person to punish for Trump’s Air Force One debacle](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/kendall-air-force-one-pentagon-defense-department-qatar-security-clearance-revoke)

_Those looking for the actual scandal should look at what the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) did, not at what the former [Air Force](https://www.af.mil/) secretary said._

Aug. 10, 2026, 1:01 PM EDT
By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

By any objective measure, the [Qatari](https://hukoomi.gov.qa/)-donated [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) was already a legal, political and ethical mess, [which grew vastly worse last month](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/air-force-one-qatar-plane-security-updates).

In early July, [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) took the new plane to [Turkey](https://global.tbmm.gov.tr/) for a [NATO](https;//www.nato.int/) summit, but he chose not to fly home on it. The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) said by way of social media that he was taking the older [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) “for old times’ sake” and [and denied that the switch](https://www.notus.org/trump-white-house/trump-qatar-donated-af1-air-force-one-jet-plane-out-of-service-security-concerns) had been made because of security concerns.


The [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/) published [a related report](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/08/us/politics/trump-air-force-one-security.html) three weeks ago that highlighted the plane’s deficiencies, which [were visible to the naked eye](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow/watch/trump-embarrassed-by-another-leak-freaks-out-on-new-york-times-for-sources-2506469955610).

[Frank Kendall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frank-kendall-84483a5/), the former [Air Force](https://www.af.mil/) secretary who supervised the [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) plane modernization project, [told the Times soon after](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/09/us/politics/new-air-force-one-defensive-countermeasures.html), “Time didn’t permit all the normal [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) modifications, so some mix of security, communications and support is missing.” He added, “Frankly, I’m surprised to see this plane used outside the U.S.”

Weeks later, [Kendall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frank-kendall-84483a5/) [also told the newspaper](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/25/us/politics/trump-air-force-one-security.html), “People need to be aware it has a lot of shortfalls compared to [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/); they do add a measure of risk.”

The good news is, the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) finally found someone to punish for the [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) debacle. The bad news is, officials decided to punish [Kendall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frank-kendall-84483a5/). [The Wall Street Journal reported](https://www.wsj.com/politics/hegseth-strips-security-clearance-from-bidens-air-force-secretary-565eb26f?mod=lead_feature_below_a_pos1):

> _[Defense](https://www.defense.gov/) [Secretary Pete Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/) revoked the security clearance of a former high-ranking [Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) defense official for allegedly leaking classified information about [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/). […]_
>
> _“Safeguarding classified information is a non-negotiable duty,” [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) spokesman Sean Parnell wrote in a post on X. “Those who violate that trust forfeit the privilege of access and any role requiring it.” Parnell’s post didn’t specifically say what classified information [Kendall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frank-kendall-84483a5/) released, but referenced “classified information regarding [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/)’s capabilities.”_

“I’m mystified,” [Kendall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frank-kendall-84483a5/) told the Journal soon after. “I’ve been extremely careful to not say anything that could be classified, and as far as I know I have not said anything classified. … So far no one has told me what I’m supposed to have revealed.”

For now, let’s skip the irony of [Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/)’s [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) stressing the importance of “safeguarding classified information,” given [his unfortunate track record](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/protecting-classified-information-pete-hegseth-picks-fight-cant-win-rcna207909) of [failing to properly safeguard classified information](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/2025/12/pete-hegseth-signalgate-unqualified/685145/).

Instead, let’s consider the [Defense Department](https://www.defense.gov/)’s decision to punish someone who doesn’t appear to have done anything wrong.

To be sure, the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) has gone to [outlandish lengths](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/authoritarian-fashion-trump-orders-probes-two-ex-officials-defied-rcna200574) to [weaponize security clearances](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/09/08/us/politics/security-clearances-trump-gabbard-democrats.html) over the course [of Trump’s second term](https://apnews.com/article/trump-security-clearances-russia-262fcd2766a22ea39968abda9ecb0d50). The [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has routinely treated security clearances as a treat that only the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s political allies are entitled to enjoy.

But punishing [Kendall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frank-kendall-84483a5/), who exited public office early last year, is especially bizarre. The former [Air Force](https://www.af.mil/) secretary offered on-the-record quotes to journalists, sharing uncontroversial observations rooted in his extensive experience and expertise.

[Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/)’s [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) may have found [Kendall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frank-kendall-84483a5/)’s assessments insulting, but it’s not the former secretary’s fault that the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) screwed this up so badly.

We’re talking about a controversy in which a sitting American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) accepted a legally dubious “gift,” and, like a child who wanted immediate access to his new toy, [rushed its approval process](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/air-force-one-qatar-plane-security-updates), apparently indifferent to the fact that many of the security and safety features that were included in the previous [Air Force One](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104588/vc-25-air-force-one/) [were left off the Qatari-donated plane](https://www.nytimes.com/video/us/100000011047754/president-trumps-new-air-force-one-poses-potential-security-risks.html).

Those looking for the actual scandal should look at what the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) did, not at what the former [Air Force](https://www.af.mil/) secretary said.

This post updates our [related earlier coverage](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/air-force-one-qatar-plane-security-updates).
