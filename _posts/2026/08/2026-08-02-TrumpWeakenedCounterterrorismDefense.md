## [Trump has been dismantling post-9/11 counterterrorism protections, Senate report says](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-has-been-dismantling-post-9-11-counterterrorism-protections-senate-report-says)

_[Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) [senators](https://www.senate.gov/) are accusing the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) of weakening U.S. defenses that were developed and refined in the 25 years since the 9/11 attacks._

Jul. 30, 2026, 5:00 AM EDT
By [Ken Dilanian](https://www.ms.now/author/ken-dilanian-2)

The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) has weakened America’s counterterrorism defenses by downsizing staff, politicizing intelligence, cutting grants and forcing federal agents to focus on immigration enforcement, [according to a new report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) by [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) lawmakers on the [Senate Homeland Security Committee](http://www.hsgac.senate.gov/) that was obtained by [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/).

The [37-page report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) also accuses the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) of skewing its focus on domestic terrorism almost exclusively to left-wing groups, ignoring significant threats from far-right and white supremacist extremists.

“Since the first day of his second term, [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and his [Administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) have dismantled critical national security agencies and programs and diverted significant resources and attention away from the fight against terrorism,” the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says. 

“These actions are the result of a combination of ideologically motivated firings and realignment of personnel and the wielding of government resources — from grants to counterterrorism authorities — as a weapon against political opponents. The [Administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/)’s actions have left Americans far more vulnerable to a potential terrorist attack, and left law enforcement officers and agents without the tools to protect our nation,” it continues.

The [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says the second [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) has significantly degraded the vast counterterrorism infrastructure that was built after the 9/11 attacks and refined over the 25 years since. 

[MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) has reached out to the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) for comment on the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/).

The [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) examines a series of moves by the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) that have been covered by the news media, including [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/), and argues that, taken together, they have made America less safe.

For example, the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says the [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) and the [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/) have lost more than 15,000 employees, including many experienced national security professionals. Others have been reassigned, [including the entire staff](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/fbi-scales-back-staffing-tracking-domestic-terrorism-probes-sources-say-2025-03-21/) of the [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/)’s [Domestic Terrorism Operations Section](https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/terrorism), which provided operational support to all 55 [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/) field offices in investigating domestic terrorism threats, the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says. 

The [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/)’s [National Security Division](https://www.justice.gov/nsd), which includes the counterterrorism and intelligence sections, [has lost](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2025/03/08/trumps-justice-dept-ousts-national-security-officials-latest-purge/) nearly 38% of its personnel, the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) adds. As [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) has reported, those departures included some of the government’s most experienced national security lawyers.

“These cuts basically mean there are fewer experienced professionals put there doing what they have to do, which is track threats,” said [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Gary Peters (D-MI)](https://www.peters.senate.gov/), the ranking [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/) on the committee.

The [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) has eviscerated key [Department of Homeland Security](https://www.dhs.gov/) components by removing more than 1,300 personnel involved in preventing, countering and responding to terrorism.

[MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) has reached out to the [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/), [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) and [DHS](https://www.dhs.gov/) for comment on the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/)’s claims.

The [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) [has also gutted CISA](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-gutted-cisa-election-interference), the agency responsible for protecting critical infrastructure against both cyber and physical attacks. [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/) runs cyber hygiene vulnerability scans and penetration testing for utilities, hospitals and election systems, identifying exploitable weaknesses before American adversaries, including terrorists, can exploit them. But now it’s doing much less of that work, the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says.

The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) also cut almost \$1 billion in national security and public safety grants to state and local government agencies, the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says.

And it has required law enforcement officers and agents, intelligence analysts and lawyers from across the government to focus on immigration enforcement “to the detriment of the national security and homeland security missions from which they were pulled,” according to the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/).

The [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) also criticizes how the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) has defined terrorists and terrorism, saying it has politicized and undermined counterterrorism efforts.

“The designation of cartels and gangs as foreign terrorist organizations has redefined terrorism and stretched the capacity of the country’s counterterrorism personnel and resources,” the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says.

It adds that the [designation of antifa](https://www.whitehouse.gov/ial-actions/2025/09/designating-antifa-as-a-domestic-terrorist-organization/) as a domestic terrorist organization was illegal and politically motivated.

And it says [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has downplayed certain kinds of terrorism and redirected counterterrorism resources away from lethal threats to the nation in favor of persecuting the his political opponents.

For example, the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) quietly removed a [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) study from its website showing that since 1990, far-right extremist attacks have outpaced all other types of terrorism and domestic violent extremism.

The most recent [Annual Threats Assessment of the Intelligence Community](https://archive.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/ATA-2026-Unclassified-Report.pdf), released March 17, “also bears out fears of politicized intelligence, particularly with regards to terrorism and Iran,” the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says. “It makes no mention of domestic terrorism including lone actors, white supremacists, or threats to U.S. elections.”

The [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/)’s [May 2026 counterterrorism strategy](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-USCT-Strategy-1.pdf) focuses on cartels, gangs and “legacy Islamist terrorists,” the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says, along with a third priority: “violent leftwing extremists, including anarchists and anti-Fascists.”

That strategy report notes, “For the 25th Anniversary of the 9/11 terror attacks, America has returned to a common sense and reality-based Counterterrorism Strategy.”

“[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has affected a complete revision of how we defeat threats to America predicated on national sovereignty and civilizational confidence and the objective of destroying the groups who would kill Americans or hurt our interests as a free nation. This applies to cartels, Jihadists, left-wing violent extremists, state actors and state sponsors, or any future terror threat,” the strategy reads.

But the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) from the [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) [senators](https://www.senate.gov/) notes that the strategy “excludes mention of any domestic terrorists who are not left-wing.”

“The new strategy also completely ignores the threat of white supremacists — despite the most recent attack on the [Islamic Center of San Diego](https://www.icsd.org/) carried out by two teenagers who were inspired by hate and referenced the shooter in the [Christchurch](https://www.christchurchnz.com/), [New Zealand](https://www.govt.nz/), mosque attack in 2019,” the [report](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-report-finds-trump-administration-has-dismantled-critical-counterterrorism-infrastructure-and-weaponized-what-remains-against-political-opponents-leaving-americans-vulnerable-to-terror-threats/) says.

[Ken Dilanian](https://www.ms.now/author/ken-dilanian-2) is the justice and intelligence correspondent for [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/).
