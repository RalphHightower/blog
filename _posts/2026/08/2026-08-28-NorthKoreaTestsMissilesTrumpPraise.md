## [North Korea launches ballistic missiles after Trump praises Kim Jong Un](https://www.ms.now/news/north-korea-launches-ballistic-missiles-trump-kim-jong-un-well-behaved)

_News of the missiles comes after [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) announced plans to meet with the [North Korea](http://www.naenara.com.kp/)n leader._ 

By [Erum Salam](https://www.ms.now/author/erum-salam)

Aug. 20, 2026, 4:51 PM EDT

[North Korea](http://www.naenara.com.kp/) launched several ballistic missiles toward the sea on Thursday, according to [South Korea](https://plus.gov.kr/)’s military, one day after [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) praised [Kim Jong Un as “well-behaved.”](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-plans-to-meet-kim-jong-un-north-korea)”

The 10 short-range missiles ​flew roughly 185 miles toward the sea, [South Korea](https://plus.gov.kr/)’s [Joint Chiefs of Staff](https://jcs.mil.kr/jcs2/) said, according to [The Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/south-north-korea-projectile-launch-military-exercises-b3af05067e4258f90f67023c7b2b3e9a).

“The ballistic missile launch ​is a grave act that violates [U.N.](https://www.un.org/) [Security Council](https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en) resolutions,” the statement said. The nation’s leaders called on [North Korea](http://www.naenara.com.kp/) to immediately cease fire.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has criticized the country’s possession of “57 very powerful nuclear weapons,” but his posture toward [Pyongyang](http://www.vok.rep.kp/) has remained soft in his second term. When asked on Wednesday about his directive to the [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) to [significantly scale back the U.S.’ joint military drills](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-instructs-pentagon-reduce-military-exercises-with-south-korea-2026-08-16/) with [South Korea](https://plus.gov.kr/), a longtime U.S. ally, earlier this week, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) doubled down.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) called the drills “very insulting to somebody that, frankly, has been during my term, at least, very well behaved,” referring to the [North Korean](http://www.naenara.com.kp/) leader, whom he plans to meet with later this year.

[South Korean](https://plus.gov.kr/) [President](https://en.president.go.kr/eng/index.do) [Lee Jae Myung](https://en.president.go.kr/president) on Wednesday downplayed the reduced joined military exercises, and [expressed hope for peace](https://x.com/Jaemyung_Lee/status/2090089417751036398) on the Korean peninsula

[Kim Yo Jong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Yo_Jong?wprov=sfla1), [Kim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Un?wprov=sfla1)’s sister and top adviser, denied knowledge of any conversations between [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and her brother, and rejected the diplomatic gesture of reducing the military drills with [South Korea](https://plus.gov.kr/).

“The reduction of the scale and duration of the drill does not mean the change of the nature of the military drill with a provocative and offensive feature,” she said in a [statement](https://kcnawatch.org/newstream/1787155486-781126445/kim-yo-jong-department-director-of-c-c-wpk-clarifies-her-stand-on-major-international-affairs/) shared by state media on Thursday.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) became the first sitting U.S. [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) to [step foot into North Korea](https://www.cnbc.com/2019/06/30/rtrs-190630-trump-kim-quotes-dmz-eu.html) in 2019 during his first term. No date or location has been set for this year’s meeting with [Kim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Un?wprov=sfla1), and the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) declined further comment.

The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) is expected to travel to [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia?wprov=sfla1) for the [Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation](https://www.apec.org/) summit in [Shenzhen](https://www.sz.gov.cn/en/), [China](https://www.gov.cn/), in November.
