## [Trump doubles down on idea of making Strait of Hormuz a U.S. territory](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-strait-hormuz-iran-war-control-territory-foreign-policy)

_If you voted for the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) ticket because it promised a restrained foreign policy, I have some bad news for you._

Aug. 17, 2026, 3:50 PM EDT
By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

[Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has invested an enormous amount of time and energy lately [trying to convince people](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/strait-hormuz-trump-open-iran-war-president) that the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) controls the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1). In reality, the U.S. most certainly [does not control](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/trump-says-hormuz-is-under-u-s-control-few-ships-are-listening-485ba60e?mod=hp_lead_pos6) the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1), but the [American president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) spent much of last week [insisting on the delusion](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/strait-hormuz-trump-open-iran-war-president) for reasons he never explained.

At one point last week, the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) added a twist to the truly bizarre rhetorical campaign, [posting an item](https://trumpstruth.org/statuses/40758) to his social media platform that read in part, “The U.S.A. has total control over the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1). I THINK WE WILL KEEP IT! … Praise be to Allah!”

The idea that the U.S. will somehow “keep” the body of water didn’t make a lot of sense, but as last week neared its end, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) elaborated on the proposition during an event in [New York](https://www.nyc.gov/).

“[A]fter we finish defeating [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), which is being very badly defeated, pretty soon I’ll be declaring the Hormuz Strait a territory of the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/),” [Trump said](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-speech-crime-statistics-prevention-enforcement-garden-city-new-york-august-14-2026/).

During an unrelated [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) event this week, a reporter asked the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) to elaborate on this apparent plan. He said making the strait a U.S. territory is “a great idea,” before again falsely claiming that the waterway is currently “open” and controlled by U.S. forces. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) added, “I like the idea of declaring it a territory.”

There’s no reason to believe the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) will seriously pursue such a goal. For that matter, no one seems to have any idea how the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) would even try to pursue such a goal.

But [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s rhetoric stood out in large part because of the scope of his imperialistic ambitions. He’s [already treating Venezuela like a colony](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-venezuela-colony-billions-oil-resources-military-rubio) like a colony, and the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) has announced his desire to annex [Canada, Greenland, the Panama Canal and the Gaza Strip](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/multiple-fronts-trump-ups-ante-territorial-ambitions-rcna196403). For good measure, let’s not forget the [American president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has even spoken publicly about possibly [returning U.S. troops to Afghanistan](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-saber-rattles-two-countries-apparently-giving-nobel-peace-prize-rcna232935).

If you voted for the [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) ticket because you were moved by [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s vows of a foreign policy rooted in restraint and non-interventionism, I have some bad news for you.
