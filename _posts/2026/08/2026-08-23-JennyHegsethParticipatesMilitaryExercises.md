## [Former Pentagon spokesperson describes Jennifer Hegseth’s expansive role as ‘bonkers’](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/jennifer-pete-hegseth-defense-department-pentagon)

_“It’s bonkers that a [defense](https://www.def/) [secretary](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/) would involve his wife in his sensitive official duties,” [John Ullyot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-ullyot-a9561510b/) said in reference to [Pete Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/)’s spouse._

Aug. 20, 2026, 1:00 PM EDT
By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Throughout American history, plenty of defense secretaries have been married, but none have taken on roles similar to [Secretary Pete Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/)’s third wife, [Jennifer Rauchet Hegseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hegseth?wprov=sfla1), whose expansive portfolio continues to raise eyebrows at the [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/).

The first indication that we were dealing with an unusual dynamic emerged just two months into [Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/)’s tenure, when [The Wall Street Journal reported](https://www.wsj.com/politics/national-security/hegseth-brought-his-wife-to-sensitive-meetings-with-foreign-military-officials-c16db0ea?mod=hp_lead_pos1) that the [defense secretary](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/) had brought his wife “to two meetings with foreign military counterparts where sensitive information was discussed.” A month later, the public learned that [Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/) used his personal phone to send highly specific details about an imminent U.S. military strike to a 13-person [Signal](https://signal.org/) group chat, [which also included his wife](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/intensifying-scandals-pete-hegseth-keeps-trumps-backing-long-rcna202315).

A week later, [The Washington Post reported](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2025/04/30/jennifer-hegseth-pentagon-trump/) that [Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/)’s [wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hegseth?wprov=sfla1) had supplanted media-engagement professionals employed by the [Defense Department](https://www.defense.gov/) and started informing the secretary’s staff about his scheduled media interviews.

Earlier this month, [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) announced that [Jennifer Hegseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hegseth?wprov=sfla1) — who, like her husband, used to work at [Faux News](https://www.foxnews.com/) — would also [lead a new commission](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/jennifer-rauchet-hegseth-pentagon-defense-commission-military-portfolio) focused on improving the lives of military spouses and families.

As [The New York Times reported](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/19/style/jennifer-hegseth.html) this week, “There has never been a [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) spouse quite like [Jennifer Hegseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hegseth?wprov=sfla1).” From the article:

![Jenny & Pete Hegseth Exercise with Navy & Marines on Deck of USS Boxer](/assets/images/JennyHegsethAircraftCarrierExercise.jpg)

> _In May she was in camouflage shorts and a sweat-drenched T-shirt that said “This Is War” working out with [sailors](https://www.navy.mil/), [Marines](https://www.marines.mil/) and her husband, [Defense](https://www.defense.gov/) [Secretary Pete Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/), on the deck of the [U.S.S. Boxer](https://www.surfpac.navy.mil/lhd4/) in [Singapore](https://www.gov.sg/). In June she and six of the couple’s seven children accompanied [Mr. Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/) to the D-Day commemoration in [Normandy](https://www.abmc.gov/cemeteries-memorials/about-normandy-american-cemetery/), where a [Defense Department](https://www.defense.gov/) promotional video with a swelling, cinematic soundtrack captured husband and wife walking arm in arm on [Omaha Beach](https://en.normandie-tourisme.fr/discover/d-day-and-the-battle-of-normandy/dday-landing-beaches/omaha-beach/). The imagery seemed to cast her as the first lady of the [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/)._

The article added that [Jennifer Hegseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hegseth?wprov=sfla1/) is [Pete Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/)’s “most influential adviser and his de facto producer at the [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/). She has prepped him for appearances, sat in on his meetings with journalists, interviewed potential members of his staff and brainstormed ideas for the [Defense Department](https://www.defense.gov/) videos he — and sometimes she — appears in.”

[John Ullyot](https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-ullyot-a9561510b/), a former [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) chief spokesperson who stepped down last year after three months of working under [Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/), told the [Times](https://www.nytimes.com/), “It’s bonkers that a defense secretary would involve his wife in his sensitive official duties, including sharing details of an impending military operation, and face zero consequences.”

While the secretary’s wife didn’t respond to the [Times](https://www.nytimes.com/)’ request for comment, a [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) spokesperson described [Jennifer Hegseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hegseth?wprov=sfla1) as “a vital asset and a true force multiplier for our mission.” The [Defense Department](https://www.defense.gov/), the spokesperson added, “not only welcomes her current leadership but actively plans to involve her even more deeply in the future.”

In other words, those with concerns about her role at the [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/) should expect matters to get considerably worse.
