## [Anti-aircraft missiles while golfing: Trump security hardens as threats multiply](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-anti-aircraft-missiles-bedminster-iran)

A senior official said the agency is investigating an unprecedented number of threats against the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/).

Aug. 14, 2026, 11:28 AM EDT
By [Priya Sridhar](https://www.ms.now/author/priya-sridhar), [David Rohde](https://www.ms.now/author/david-rohde) and [Marc Santia](https://www.ms.now/author/marc-santia-2)

As [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) prepares to head out this weekend to [his golf course](https://www.trumpnationalbedminster.com/) in [Bedminster](https://www.bedminster.us/), [New Jersey](https://www.nj.gov/), he will face course features quite different from the usual sand traps: anti-aircraft installations.

The hardware on the course was visible when [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) shook hands with a dozen U.S. [Army](https://www.army.mil/) soldiers while playing golf on a sunny afternoon earlier this month.

“Not worried about missiles or drones,” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) told the soldiers as the Jackson 5’s “I’ll Be There” played in the background, as seen in a [video posted online](https://x.com/margomartin47/status/2083578489920184542?s=46) by a [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) staffer on Aug. 1.

“We got it covered,” one of the soldiers replied, gesturing at a vehicle behind the group.

The vehicle behind them was a [Humvee](https://www.amgeneral.com/what-we-do/entities-brands/humvee-brand-licensing/) armed with an [Avenger Air Defense System](https://odin.t2com.army.mil/WEG/Asset/AN::TWQ-1_Avenger_American_4x4_Mobile_Air_Defense_Missile_System). An [Army](https://www.army.mil/) surface-to-air missile system, the [Avenger](https://odin.t2com.army.mil/WEG/Asset/AN::TWQ-1_Avenger_American_4x4_Mobile_Air_Defense_Missile_System) can fire multiple short-range missiles at incoming cruise missiles, low-flying aircraft, helicopters and drones. The missiles are mounted on the back of a [Humvee](https://www.amgeneral.com/what-we-do/entities-brands/humvee-brand-licensing/), on a turret that can spin 360 degrees and fire in any direction.

“At the request of the U.S. [Secret Service](https://www.secretservice.gov/), ground-based air defense equipment was operating,” [Lt. Col. Andrew Scott](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-scott-8b348838/), an [Air Force](https://www.af.mil/) spokesperson, said in a statement at the time.

The following weekend, [F-16](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/f-16.html) jets from the [North American Aerospace Defense Command](https://www.norad.mil/), or [NORAD](https://www.norad.mil/), which monitors and protects U.S. airspace, also [intercepted “multiple general aviation aircraft”](https://x.com/NORADCommand/status/2086513190935154824?s=20) that violated the temporary flight restrictions over the golf course. Officials said both aircraft were escorted safely out of the airspace.

Days later, another [NORAD](https://www.norad.mil/) [F-16](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/f-16.html) used flares to intercept a general aviation aircraft over [Geneva](https://genevaohio.gov/), [Ohio](https://ohio.gov/), where [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) was attending the [Patriot Games](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-patriot-games-youth-sports), a youth sports competition, on a Tuesday evening.

In July, after the [NATO](https://www.nato.int/) summit in [Turkey](https://global.tbmm.gov.tr/), [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) used a decoy plane to make his way back to the U.S. [after a reportedly credible threat on his life](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-plane-swap-air-force-one-catering-truck-turkey) from Iran. Warnings of a possible shoulder-fired missile led to [the plane swap](https://www.ms.now/news/rubio-bessent-decoy-flight-trump-air-force-one-turkey), prompting questions about what security measures are needed to ensure the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s safety against low-altitude missiles and drones.

The decoy plane and advanced weaponry reflect the heightened security around [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) as the Iran war continues and as the [Secret Service](https://www.secretservice.gov/) responds to what one source described as an unprecedented number of threats against the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s life.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is now the focus of more threats than any modern American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has been, according to one senior [Secret Service](https://www.secretservice.gov/) official, speaking on the condition of anonymity to discuss internal matters. The official said the agency was investigating the highest number of threat cases in its history, with agents examining 10,000 threat cases, 40% more than last year.

Agents have conducted some 3,000 protective visits to people who have threatened to kill [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) or other federal officials, and many of the people making the threats have mental health issues, the official said. The [Secret Service](https://www.secretservice.gov/) has also seen a tenfold increase in mental health commitments as a result of its investigations.

“What we are seeing now is something I’ve never seen before,” the official told [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) earlier this week. “Threat environment is the highest we have seen.”
