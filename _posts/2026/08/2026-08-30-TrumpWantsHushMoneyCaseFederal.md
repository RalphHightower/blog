## [Trump’s appeal to move criminal case to federal court denied again](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-criminal-case-appeal-federal-court)

_The decision comes as [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is directly challenging his conviction on [34 felony counts](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2024/attorney-general-james-wins-landmark-victory-case-against-donald-trump) before a state appeals court._

By [Lisa Rubin](https://www.ms.now/author/lisa-rubin)
Aug. 28, 2026, 1:24 PM EDT

A [Manhattan district attorney’s criminal case](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-sentence-new-york-hush-money-case-rcna187107) has again declined [President](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s efforts to transfer, or “remove,” the [Manhattan](https://www.manhattan.nyc.gov/) [district attorney](https://manhattanda.org/)’s criminal case against him to federal court in an effort to erase his [2024 conviction](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2024/attorney-general-james-wins-landmark-victory-case-against-donald-trump) in the so-called hush money case.

In considering whether [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had shown the requisite “good cause” for delaying his second attempt to remove the case to federal court, [Judge Alvin Hellerstein](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-alvin-k-hellerstein), a 92-year-old judge in the [Southern District of New York](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/), noted that even after the [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/)’s [presidential](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) immunity decision, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) first tried to litigate that issue in [New York](https://www.ny.gov/) state court.

That, according to [Hellerstein](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-alvin-k-hellerstein), is antithetical to good cause: A litigant “cannot seek victory in one court, and then, when it becomes apparent that the court will rule against him, seek to remove his case to another court,” he wrote.

[Hellerstein](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-alvin-k-hellerstein) also found that none of the evidence [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) pointed to as evidence of his official acts is, in fact, [presidential](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) or official conduct that should have been excluded from the trial on account of the [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/)’s ruling.

“[P]aying hush money to an adult film star or engaging in a cover-up of an embarrassment are not subsumed in [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) immunity,” [Hellerstein](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-alvin-k-hellerstein) wrote.

The decision marks the third time [Hellerstein](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-alvin-k-hellerstein) has ruled on [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s efforts to move the district attorney’s case to federal court.

In response to the ruling, a spokesperson for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s legal team issued a statement that read in part, “[President](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has already prevailed in this case at the [Second Circuit Court of Appeals](https://www.ca2.uscourts.gov/), and today’s baseless and lawless decision contradicts that correct ruling. [President](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) will be filing a powerful appeal, and will continue defeating Democrat weaponization at every turn.”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [first sought to remove](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311.1.0_2.pdf) [Manhattan](https://www.manhattan.nyc.gov/) [District Attorney](https://manhattanda.org/) [Alvin Bragg](https://manhattanda.org/meet-alvin-bragg/)’s case against him in spring 2023, shortly after [Bragg](https://manhattanda.org/meet-alvin-bragg/) unveiled his indictment of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/). By July of that year, [Hellerstein](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-alvin-k-hellerstein) ruled the case [should remain in state court](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311.43.0_1.pdf), on the grounds that it was unrelated to any of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s actions as [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) and that neither of his intended federal defenses — [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) immunity and preemption of the relevant state law — were plausible.

Although [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [initially indicated](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311.44.0_1.pdf) he would challenge that ruling, he [withdrew his appeal](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311.45.0.pdf) months later in November 2023.

Yet nearly a year later, following his May 2024 trial, at which he was convicted on all [34 counts of falsifying business records in the first degree](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2024/attorney-general-james-wins-landmark-victory-case-against-donald-trump), [Trump again sought](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311.49.0.pdf) to move his case to federal court. He argued that the [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/)’s [presidential immunity decision](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/23-939_e2pg.pdf), issued weeks after the trial’s conclusion, as well as the trial court’s alleged bias against him, constituted “good cause” for a renewed push to remove the case.

Within days, [Hellerstein denied Trump’s motion](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311.50.0.pdf) in a four-page order. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) appealed, and in November 2025, a three-judge panel of the [U.S. Court of Appeals for the 2nd Circuit](https://www.ca2.uscourts.gov/) [vacated Hellerstein’s decision](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.ca2.c95c060a-7c1c-465e-ad55-c66a4c162941/gov.uscourts.ca2.c95c060a-7c1c-465e-ad55-c66a4c162941.104.1_1.pdf), instructing him to examine more closely the effect of the [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/)’s [presidential](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) immunity decision on removal and to consider whether a removal motion can be raised during or after a trial if new grounds arise. 

[Hellerstein](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/hon-alvin-k-hellerstein)’s newest order comes as [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) simultaneously appeals his conviction in [New York](https://www.ny.gov/) state courts. While [Judge Juan Merchan](https://iapps.courts.state.ny.us/judicialdirectory/Bio?judge_id=Jvv5ZfC8zLC57yZndmF4ow%3D%3D), who oversaw the trial, ruled in January 2025 that [Trump would receive an “unconditional discharge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s7WCr35-Bno) — meaning no jail time, fines or probation in connection with his conviction — he left [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s conviction in place. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s final brief in that appeal is not due until mid-October. It likely will be months before the appeals court, known as the First Department, renders its decision.

However, even as he pursues his state court appeal, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) apparently would prefer to move the case to federal court. In particular, [Trump’s submissions before Hellerstein](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311/gov.uscourts.nysd.598311.53.0.pdf) reflect the belief that a federal court would be more receptive to his argument that under the [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/)’s [presidential](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) immunity decision, the [Manhattan](https://www.manhattan.nyc.gov/) [DA](https://manhattanda.org/)’s office improperly introduced evidence of his official acts as [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), so his conviction therefore must be vacated. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has also suggested that a federal court could be persuaded that [Merchan](https://iapps.courts.state.ny.us/judicialdirectory/Bio?judge_id=Jvv5ZfC8zLC57yZndmF4ow%3D%3D) should have been recused from the case because of alleged conflicts of interest and the appearance of impropriety.
