## [Darline Graham refuses to say if she would’ve certified 2020 results](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/darline-graham-certify-2020-election-biden-trump-south-carolina-senate)

_[Lindsey Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1) voted to certify the results of the [2020 election](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/federalelections2020.pdf). Would his sister? The appointed senator apparently doesn’t want to talk about it._

Aug. 24, 2026, 2:58 PM EDT
By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

By the evening of [Jan. 6, 2021](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/federalelections2020.pdf), all of the rioters who had attacked the [U.S. Capitol](https://www.congress.gov/) in [Donald Trump](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-military-bond-market-yields-economy-ignorance-illiteracy)’s name had been cleared from the building, and it was time for members of [Congress](https://www.ms.now/opinion/congress-omb-spending-vought-block) to do their duty and certify the results of the [2020 presidential election](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/federalelections2020.pdf).

[Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) leaders urged their members to do the right and responsible thing, but in the [very](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1171/vote_117_1_00001.htm) first [votes](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1171/vote_117_1_00002.htm) of the 117th Congress, held just hours after one of the most serious examples of political violence in American history, eight [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) senators — [Ted Cruz (T-TX)](https://www.cruz.senate.gov/), [Josh Hawley (T-MO)](https://www.hawley.senate.gov/), [Cindy Hyde-Smith (T-MS)](https://www.hydesmith.senate.gov/), [John Kennedy (T-LA)](https://www.kennedy.senate.gov/), [Cynthia Lummis (T-W)](https://www.lummis.senate.gov/), [Roger Marshall (T-KS)](https://www.marshall.senate.gov/), [Rick Scott (T-FL)](https://www.rickscott.senate.gov/) and [Tommy Tuberville (T-AL](https://www.tuberville.senate.gov/) — voted at least once not to certify the rightful winner of the national election held two months earlier.

Then-[Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Lindsey Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1), a [South Carolina Republican](https://www.sc.gop/) who briefly suggested he was done with [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) in the aftermath of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s defeat and the insurrectionist violence on [Capitol Hill](https://www.congress.gov/), was one of the 92 senators who voted to certify [Joe Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/)’s victory. That wouldn’t ordinarily be especially notable, were it not for the unusual circumstances unfolding in the [Palmetto State](https;//www.sc.gov/).

Now that the late senator’s sister, [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Darline Graham (T-SC)](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/), is running to fill his seat for the next six years, would she have done the same thing as her brother the evening of Jan. 6?

[MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/)’s [Mychael Schnell](https://www.ms.now/author/mychael-schnell) asked the appointed senator about this during a Monday press conference. She apparently didn’t want to talk about it.

“[Senator](https://www.senate.gov/), you just mentioned that you agree with your brother on everything except for one matter, that being illegal immigration, you’d go farther than him on that,” [Schnell](https://www.ms.now/author/mychael-schnell) noted. “That being said, I’m curious, do you agree with him about the [2020 election](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/federalelections2020.pdf), that [Joe Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/) was certified the winner of that election, was lawfully elected, and that you would’ve voted to certify the election, as your brother did?”

[Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) replied, “I think our election system is broken,” pushing a line that was both outrageously false and irrelevant to the underlying question. She then quickly shifted to emphasizing her support for the voter suppression package the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has spent much of the year pushing.

“You won’t commit to having certified the results of the [2020 election](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/federalelections2020.pdf)?” [Schnell](https://www.ms.now/author/mychael-schnell) added in a follow-up question.

At that point, [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) refused to engage and moved on.

This was hardly a trick question — or, for that matter, a question about national security, which [she said last week](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/darline-graham-south-carolina-debate-senate-election-taiwan-hannity) is “not my thing.” Either she would have voted to certify the results of a free and fair American election, or she would not have.

The fact that [Graham](https://www.dgraham.senate.gov/) didn’t want to answer directly suggests 1) she’s terrified of upsetting [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), who’s championing her candidacy; 2) she’s reluctant to advertise her role as an election denier, which would put her at odds with her late brother’s legacy; 3) the [2020 election](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/federalelections2020.pdf) is another one of those issues that is not her “thing”; or 4) some combination therein.

[South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/)’s special [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) runoff election is [one day away](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/darline-graham-south-carolina-primary-trump-oklahoma-governor-runoff-election), and it’s far from clear whether the appointed incumbent will prevail over her intraparty rival, [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Ralph Norman (T-SC5)](https://norman.house.gov/). Watch this space.
