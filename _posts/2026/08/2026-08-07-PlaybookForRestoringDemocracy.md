title: Ring the Alarm Bells: The Oath We Swore and the Authoritarian Playbook We’re Now Living Through"
---

Democracies rarely collapse in a single dramatic moment. They erode slowly — through fear, loyalty tests, and the quiet normalization of power that was never meant to exist. Senator Jeff Merkley’s *Ten Rules of Trump’s Authoritarian Playbook* lays out that erosion with unsettling clarity, and it matches what many of us have been seeing with our own eyes.

The document states plainly:

> “Authoritarianism isn’t down the street or around the corner – it is here right now.”

That line isn’t hyperbole. It’s a diagnosis.

## The Constitution Has No “Invisible Ink”

One of the most striking passages in Merkley’s report describes the Supreme Court “finding ‘invisible ink’ in the Constitution” to expand presidential power. That metaphor captures a dangerous trend: reading into the Constitution powers that were never written, debated, or ratified.

The Constitution is a text — not a Rorschach test.
Its meaning is not supposed to shift because a president demands it or a court fears the consequences of denying him.

This is where the oath of office matters.

Every federal employee swears an oath to **support and defend the Constitution of the United States** — *not* to a president, *not* to a party, and certainly not to a strongman. The oath is the firewall between constitutional governance and personal loyalty.

Merkley’s document shows what happens when that firewall is attacked.

## The Ten Rules — and Why They Matter

Merkley outlines ten authoritarian tactics, each backed by concrete examples. A few stand out:

- **Rule One: Fire the Referees**
  Trump removed 17 Inspectors General in four days — the very officials responsible for exposing corruption.

- **Rule Two: Pack the Government with Loyalists**
  The Senate confirmed 107 nominees in a single vote, many lacking qualifications but not loyalty.

- **Rule Four: Disregard Due Process**
  The report recounts ICE detaining legal residents and even U.S. citizens, including Kilmar Abrego Garcia, who was deported without due process despite legal status.

- **Rule Eight: Weaponize the Justice System**
  The Supreme Court found “invisible ink” granting immunity for crimes committed “as acts of the federal government.”

- **Rule Ten: Rig the Next Election**
  Consolidating voter files, gerrymandering, and undermining vote‑by‑mail are all described as active strategies.

These aren’t theoretical concerns. They are documented actions.

## The Crypto Thread Fits the Same Pattern

While Merkley’s report doesn’t explicitly discuss crypto, it *does* describe a pattern of:

- manipulating administrative approvals
- punishing institutions through regulatory leverage
- centralizing executive control over financial systems

Any attempt to fold crypto into federal banking without congressional oversight would fit squarely under **Rule Seven: Abuse Government Services** and **Rule Six: Steal Congress’ Power of the Purse**.

The pattern is the point.

## Why I’m Posting This

Because silence is complicity.

Because the oath we swear — *support and defend the Constitution* — is meaningless if we ignore the moments when that Constitution is bent, stretched, or overwritten by “invisible ink.”

Because Merkley’s document is not just a warning; it is a map of what is happening right now.

And because citizens have power — real power — when they act together.

Merkley writes:

> “Being angry and frustrated alone is depressing and discouraging, but being angry and organized is energizing and effective.”

He’s right.
The alarm bells are ringing.
We should not pretend we don’t hear them.

# Call to Action

If this concerns you, don’t sit with that feeling alone.
Channel it.

- Read the document yourself.
- Share it with someone who hasn’t seen it.
- Talk openly about the constitutional stakes.
- Support institutions and organizations that defend civil liberties.
- Hold your elected officials accountable — firmly, peacefully, and consistently.

Democracy is not self‑maintaining.
It survives only when citizens insist on it..

## [Merkley Leads Sweeping Legislation to Counter Trump’s Authoritarian Takeover of U.S. and Defend Our Democracy - Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/merkley-leads-sweeping-legislation-to-counter-trumps-authoritarian-takeover-of-u-s-and-defend-our-democracy/)

August 4, 2026

[Washington, D.C.](https://dc.gov/) – Today, [Oregon](https://www..oregon.gov/)’s [U.S. Senator](https://www.senate.gov/) [Jeff Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/) unveiled the [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf), a comprehensive legislative package to fight back against [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s aggressive authoritarian takeover of American democracy.

The brand-new [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) includes provisions to directly oppose [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s authoritarian playbook, including defending independent government watchdogs, protecting non-partisan civil servants, reasserting [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ power of the purse, fighting government corruption, strengthening free speech and civil liberties, and safeguarding free and fair elections.

“Authoritarianism isn’t down the street or around the corner—it is here right now,” said [Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/). “We have all three ingredients of a strongman state in the United States: a rubber-stamp [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) doing the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s bidding; a deferential [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/) finding ‘invisible ink’ in the [Constitution](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/) to enhance the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s powers; and a power-hungry [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) behaving like a king, breaking the law and violating the checks and balances of our [Constitution](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/).

[Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/) continued, “The [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) lays out a clear roadmap of the actions [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) can take to fight back against [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s authoritarian takeover and reassert our authority. Together, let’s fight back and save our republic!”

The [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) is co-sponsored by U.S. [Senators](https://www.senate.gov/) [Tammy Duckworth (D-IL)](https://www.duckworth.senate.gov/) and [Richard Blumenthal (D-CT)](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/).

“In a clear conflict of interest and gross abuse of power, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) fired 17 Inspectors General within his first days in office so he could tap political allies to conduct oversight of his own [Afministration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/)—leaving these watchdogs to answer to the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) before the American people,” said [Duckworth](https://www.duckworth.senate.gov/). “First and foremost, [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/)’ [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) includes my legislation that would prevent stone-cold corruption like this, which have too often become the norm in the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [Afministration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/). It’s clear that the current checks on the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) are not enough, and our legislation would not only help us restore good government—it will also empower [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) to prevent any more wanna-be kings from bending our Republic to its breaking point.”

“[Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has led a brazen, unprecedented campaign of corruption—growing his power while ignoring the needs of the American people. The [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) installs critical guardrails against [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s relentless attacks on our democracy, promoting government transparency and accountability while preserving Americans’ rights and liberties. This legislation fights against [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s slide towards tyranny and protects our country’s democratic values,” said Blumenthal.

To fight back against the known playbook of authoritarian governments, the [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) includes new provisions to counter the rise of authoritarianism and erosion of our democratic government under the second [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [Afministration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/), as well as existing legislation from [Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/) and U.S. [Senators](https://www.senate.gov/) [Richard Blumenthal (D-CT)](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/), [Tammy Duckworth (D-IL)](https://www.duckworth.senate.gov/), [Kirsten Gillibrand (D-NY)](https://www.gillibrand.senate.gov/), [Ben Ray Luján (D-NM)](https://www.lujan.senate.gov/), [Edward J. Markey (D-MA)](https://www.markey.senate.gov/), and [Adam Schiff (D-CA)](https://www.schiff.senate.gov/).

The [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) outlines ten principles to protect our democracy:

1. Defend the Referees
2. Protect the Independent, Non-Partisan Civil Service
3. Celebrate One Nation, Indivisible
4. Reaffirm Equal Justice Under Law
5. Strengthen Free Speech
6. Reassert [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ Power of the Purse
7. Fight Government Corruption
8. Ensure Liberty and Justice for All
9. Prevent Authoritarian Policing Tactics on America’s Streets
10. Safeguard Free and Fair Elections

A detailed outline of the [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) can be found by clicking [here](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act-Outline.pdf).

The [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) is supported by [Public Citizen, Project on Government Oversight (POGO)](https://www.pogo.org/), [Refugees International](https://www.refugeesinternational.org/), and [Common Cause](https://www.commoncause.org/).

“It is stunning how the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [Afministration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/), empowered by an obedient congressional [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) caucus, has empowered the federal government to intrude and impose upon our daily lives. The separation of powers, and the checks and balances against over-reach by the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), have largely been vanquished. America has fallen into dangerous times. [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/)’s [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) seeks to restore those critical checks and balances and re-affirm the democratic principles upon which America was founded. [Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/) heartily endorses this very necessary measure,” said [Craig Holman, Ph.D.](https://www.citizen.org/about/person/craig-holman/), [Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/).

“With so many attacks on our country’s core values, the [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) is a critical step in restoring our democratic principles,” said [Carol Evans](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carolaevanswashingtondc/), [Common Cause](https://www.commoncause.org/)’s Vice President of Policy. “This legislation takes the power back for the people. It ensures that our government serves the public rather than answering to the whims of one person in charge.”

[Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/) has led the charge to ring the alarm bells about the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [Afministration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/)’s authoritarian takeover of America. On October 21, 2025, [Merkley](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/) held the [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) floor for 22 hours 37 minutes—the third longest speech in [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) history—to speak directly to the American people about [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s authoritarianism. He later published an analysis, titled [“Ring the Alarm Bells: The Ten Rules of Trump’s Authoritarian Playbook,”](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/connect/ring-the-alarm-bells-the-ten-rules-of-trumps-authoritarian-playbook/) to ensure all Americans understand what our country is facing.

Full text of the [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) can be found by clicking [here](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf).

A section-by-section summary of the [For Our Republic Act](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act.pdf) can be found by clicking [here](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/For-Our-Republic-Act-Section-by-Section.pdf).
