## [EU chief Ursula von der Leyen says she wants Canada to become bloc’s 1st associate member](https://www.ms.now/news/eu-chief-ursula-von-der-leyen-canada-blocs-1st-associate-member)

_The overture comes as [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s tariffs and repeated talk of making [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) the 51st state have pushed [Ottawa](https://ottawa.ca/en) to seek closer economic and security ties with [Europe](https://european-union.europa.eu/index_en)._

By [The Associated Press](https://www.ms.now/author/the-associated-press)

Sept. 16, 2026, 4:21 AM EDT

[STRASBOURG](https://www.strasbourg.eu/), [France](https://www.info.gouv.fr/) [AP](https://apnews.com/) — [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/) [President Ursula von der Leyen](https://commission.europa.eu/about/organisation/president_en) told [Canadian](https://www.canada.ca/) [Prime Minister](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en) [Mark Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about) on Wednesday that she wants to open the door for [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) to become the [European Union](https://european-union.europa.eu/index_en)’s first associate member.

The overture comes as [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s tariffs and repeated talk of making [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) the 51st state have pushed [Ottawa](https://ottawa.ca/en) to seek closer economic and security ties with [Europe](https://european-union.europa.eu/index_en). [Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about) has called for a “unique alliance” with the [EU](https://european-union.europa.eu/index_en) as [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) seeks to reduce its heavy dependence on U.S. trade.

“I would like to work with you on opening the door for [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) being the first associate member of the [EU](https://european-union.europa.eu/index_en),” [von der Leyen](https://commission.europa.eu/about/organisation/president_en) told [Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about) during her annual [State of the European Union](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/state-union_en) address. [Carney](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about) received a standing ovation from [European lawmakers](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/portal/en). He is due to address the [European Parliament](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/portal/en) on Thursday.

[Von der Leyen](https://commission.europa.eu/about/organisation/president_en) said [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) and the [EU](https://european-union.europa.eu/index_en) would build on their existing free trade agreement, known as [CETA](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-trade-relationships-country-and-region/countries-and-regions/canada/eu-canada-agreements_en), with a broader “Alliance for the Future” aimed at creating “a common prosperity and economic security space.”

She cast that closer relationship as rooted not just in trade but in shared democratic values.

“Above all, dear [Mark](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/about), [Europe](https://european-union.europa.eu/index_en) and [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) believe in democracy,” [von der Leyen](https://commission.europa.eu/about/organisation/president_en) said. “We believe that power does not belong to the strongest, the richest or the loudest, but to all of us. That democracies have the freedom to choose with whom to work.”

[Von der Leyen](https://commission.europa.eu/about/organisation/president_en) said the expanded partnership would cover advanced manufacturing, technology, defense production, the Arctic, energy, critical minerals, batteries, artificial intelligence, quantum computing, cybersecurity and economic security.

“This is a partnership not against anyone else, but for our common strength,” she said. “In short, we want to bring the relationship with [Canada](https://www.canada.ca/) to the highest level possible.”
