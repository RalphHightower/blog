## [As oil prices keep climbing, Trump doesn’t expect relief until after the midterms](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/oil-prices-gas-diesel-trump-iran-war-midterm-election-november)

_There’s a striking contradiction at the heart of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s line on gas prices. He hasn’t tried to reconcile the contradiction because he can’t._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Sept. 10, 2026, 3:22 PM EDT

Consumers looking for relief at the gas pump will have to wait a while longer. Oil prices [spiked yet again](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/10/business/oil-middle-east-iran-war.html) on Thursday, and [according to AAA](https://gasprices.aaa.com/), per-gallon averages climbed another 5 cents over the course of 24 hours (and 13 cents over the past seven days). Diesel prices, meanwhile, have climbed to nearly \$6 per gallon nationally, which is an all-time high.

With this in mind, a reporter asked [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) during a brief Q&A, “Oil is now back above \$100 a barrel. How do you explain this to Americans who are wondering when prices are going to come down?”

“Well, I think it’s very easy to explain to [America](https://www.usa.gov/),” the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) said. “All you have to do is say, ‘Will you let [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) have a nuclear weapon?’ And the answer is no.”

This might make more sense if [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) had, or was even close to having, nuclear weapons; but since that isn’t the case, the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)’s talking points continue to be worthless.

But then the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) kept going.

After insisting that [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) “is in shambles right now,” [Trump added](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-press-gaggle-before-air-force-one-departure-september-9-2026/), “We control the [\[Strait of Hormuz\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1) and lots of other things. So for a little while. I would say this: Shortly, but right after the election, because they’d like to have it that way, but right after the election, oil prices are going to be tumbling downward. They’re going to be tumbling down, and we’ll get ‘em down.”

The Q&A moved on, but I found myself stuck on the tension between the first part of the answer and the second.

On the one hand, we’re supposed to believe that [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) is in “shambles” and the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) enjoys control over the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1). On the other hand, we’re also supposed to believe that [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) is continuing to fight the war and has the capacity to keep oil prices high for the next eight weeks.

But which is it? Is [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) a defeated force or not? Do we control the strait or not? If so, why doesn’t the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) simply open it up to international traffic now and lower prices?

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) hasn’t tried to reconcile the contradiction because he can’t. Instead, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) apparently just wants to keep peddling desperate rhetoric in the hope the public won’t notice how little sense he’s making.
