## A Moment of Constitutional Clarity for America’s Media

[Jen Psaki’s recent commentary](https://youtube.com/shorts/K6kQNhLM7_g?is=1eKToHj2mTikbeiA) — warning that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) behaves like a bully who keeps taking your lunch money if you appease him — captures a deeper constitutional truth. When a president uses regulatory pressure to punish coverage he dislikes, every newsroom in America becomes vulnerable. The [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/)’s posture toward [ABC](https://www.abc.com/) is not just a dispute with one network; it is a warning to all broadcasters and publishers.

Television networks, whether over‑the‑air or cable‑only, know how easily license reviews can become political weapons. Radio operators across AM, FM, and XM understand the danger of selective enforcement. Print journalism — from local papers to national magazines — has long recognized how administrative intimidation can chill reporting. Even outlets often perceived as friendly to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), including [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/), [OAN](https://www.oann.com/), and [Newsmax](https://www.newsmax.com/), should not assume immunity. Once retaliation becomes normalized, loyalty offers no protection.

The [First Amendment](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-1/) is not a courtesy extended by government; it is a constraint placed upon it. When officials use regulatory tools to coerce editorial behavior, they cross a constitutional boundary that protects all Americans, regardless of political affiliation.

As the nation approaches its 250th year, Benjamin Franklin’s warning still applies: “We must all hang together, or we shall all hang separately.”[^101] Media institutions must respond to this moment with unified resolve. A single outlet cannot withstand government intimidation alone — but a united press can.

[^101]: [Call To Aaction: We Must Hang Together Or We Shall Hang Seperately — The American Revolution / HistoryCentral](https://www.historycentral.com/Revolt/stories/Hang.html)

Defending the [First Amendment](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-1/) is not optional. It is the responsibility of every newsroom that relies on it.
