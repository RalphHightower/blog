## [Team Trump airs taxpayer-financed propagandistic ad, despite legal limits](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-propaganda-ad-commercial-midterms-taxpayer-public-money)

_It is illegal to use public funds for “propaganda purposes.” Worried about the midterms, the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) apparently decided not to care._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Sept. 24, 2026, 2:46 PM EDT

It was about a month ago when [The Atlantic reported](https://www.theatlantic.com/politics/2026/08/trump-midterms-ads-maga/688409/) that [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had directed his political operation to create a series of election-season television advertisements about how great he is. It was [a dubious idea for all sorts of reasons](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-campaign-ads-maga-inc-super-pac-midterm-elections), but it was clear at the time where the money would come from: The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s focus was on how to spend his super PAC’s \$400 million political war chest, not public funds.

A month later, however, many Americans are already seeing [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)-centric election ads on TV, except they’re the ones who are footing the bill for those commercials. [Politico reported](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/24/trump-administration-midterm-tv-ads-01091499):

> The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) launched a new television advertisement featuring [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) calling out socialism, communism and Marxism, appearing to use government resources to go on air in the weeks before the midterm election.

The ad began airing Wednesday night, according to the ad tracking service [AdImpact](https://adimpact.com/https://adimpact.com/), and has aired on programs on [Faux News](https://www.foxnews.com/) and [Newsmax](https://www.newsmax.com/) along with connected TV platforms.

The ads explicitly tell viewers in a disclaimer that the commercials were “paid for by the U.S. government,” although it did not elaborate on details such as which part of the government.

The degree to which [the 30-second ad](https://bsky.app/profile/ariehkovler.com/post/3mwam5nlg3k2x) looks like a campaign commercial is hard to overstate. It’s filled with images and video clips of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and his campaign slogans and concludes with a [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) ally praising the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s “toughness.”

The video serves no purpose other than to celebrate and glorify [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/). (The ad’s soundtrack is the song “Love Me” by JMSN, emphasizing the lyric, “I’ll make them love me,” which is every bit as creepy and on the nose as it sounds.)

At this point, I could write a few paragraphs fact-checking the ad and noting how counterproductive it is for [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) to have a wildly unpopular incumbent [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) putting the spotlight on himself as early voting begins in earnest.

But while those details are relevant, let’s instead focus on what matters most: This appears to be a clear instance in which the federal government is illegally using taxpayer money on brazen political propaganda.

As [The Washington Post reported](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/24/ad-boosting-trump-paid-by-taxpayers-airing-la-chicago/), it is illegal under federal law to use funds for “publicity or propaganda purposes.” The [Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/) has said the prohibition applies to communications that are “purely partisan” and “designed to aid a political party or candidates,” which would seem to apply to this video, given the content and the context.

[Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Jamie Raskin (D-MD8)](https://raskin.house.gov/), the ranking member on the [House Judiciary Committee](https://judiciary.house.gov/), [argued via social media](https://x.com/RepRaskin/status/2103114920933904703) that the ad could amount to, among other things, “felony criminal theft and conversion of government property for political campaign purposes.”

If recent history is any guide, congressional [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) will shrug with indifference at this latest abuse of taxpayer money, but should [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) win a majority in either chamber, it’s a safe bet this will be among the controversies to generate real scrutiny — including from [Raskin](https://raskin.house.gov/), who’s positioned to chair the [House Judiciary Committee](https://judiciary.house.gov/).
