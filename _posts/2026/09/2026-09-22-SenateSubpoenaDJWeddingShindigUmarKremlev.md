## [Republican senator seeks subpoena for Donald Trump Jr. over wedding party controversy](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/curtis-trump-jr-wedding-russian-putin-grassley-republicans-subpoena-scandal)

_The fact that [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [John Curtis](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) of [Utah](https://www.utah.gov/) is asking the right questions is a start, but it’s hardly enough._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Sept. 22, 2026, 11:36 AM EDT

Nearly all congressional [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)s have been content to look the other way on the controversy surrounding the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch who paid for [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s wedding party. [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [John Curtis (T-UT)](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) , however, has taken the matter quite seriously.

“A [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch paid for a lavish wedding after-party for [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), which to me just stinks. It’s corruption, I don’t like it,” [Curtis said](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6099233-curtis-calls-trump-jr-wedding-corruption/?tbref=hp) said in a video posted to social media late last week. The [Utah](https://www.utah.gov/) [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) added, “You’ll hear more from me later on. This is a big deal.”

Evidently, he meant it. The senator’s office issued [a press statement](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/press-releases/curtis-calls-for-investigation-into-presidential-families-business-dealings/) on Tuesday morning that read in part:

> U.S. [Senator](https://www.senate.gov/) [John Curtis (T-UT)](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) sent a letter to [Senate Judiciary Committee](http://www.judiciary.senate.gov/) [Chairman](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/about/chair) [Chuck Grassley (T-IA)](https://www.grassley.senate.gov/) and [Ranking Member](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/about/ranking) [Dick Durbin (D-IL)](https://www.durbin.senate.gov/) calling for an investigation into the use of [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) family relationships for private financial benefit, preferential treatment, or access by domestic and foreign interests.

> As part of that investigation, the letter urges the [Committee](http://www.judiciary.senate.gov/) to subpoena [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) and [Hunter Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Biden?wprov=sfla1) to testify regarding their past business dealings, relationships with foreign individuals and entities, gifts or other benefits they have received, and any instances in which their relationship to the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) was invoked or understood to provide value.

“A toaster is a wedding gift,” [Curtis](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) added in an online statement. “A private-island party paid for by a [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/)-connected oligarch is something else. [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)s nearly wore out the subpoena machine investigating [Hunter Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Biden?wprov=sfla1)’s foreign relationships. We should not unplug it now. ‘Trust us’ was not enough then, and it is not enough now.”

[The senator’s letter](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/09.21.2026-Curtis-Letter-to-Senate-Judiciary-re_-Don-Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)-Hunter-Biden-1.pdf) to the [Judiciary Committee](http://www.judiciary.senate.gov/)’s leaders will almost certainly go ignored — [Grassley](https://www.grassley.senate.gov/) and his [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) brethren have gone to absurd lengths to ignore a long list of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)-related scandals — unless [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)s reclaim a majority in the chamber. What’s more, it remains to be seen whether [Curtis](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) will follow through once the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) weighs in and tries to bring the [Utah](https://www.utah.gov/)n to heel.

But the fact that a sitting [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) senator from a red state wants the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s son to be subpoenaed is both notable in its own right and a development that brings fresh attention to the underlying controversy.

And what a controversy it is. [ProPublica reported](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) last week that [Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch close to [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/), had “secretly bankrolled” part of [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s recent wedding, footing the bill for “hundreds of thousands of dollars of wedding expenses” and even helping to plan the gathering.

“It’s not clear why [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) helped pay for [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s wedding,” [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) added. “The men appear to have met only recently.”

It was [a tough story to shrug off](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/donald-trump-jr-wedding-party-russian-oligarch-putin-garcia) for reasons that should have been obvious: A [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/)-aligned [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch paid for the wedding party of the sitting American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s oldest son, and it was an open question as to why that happened and what the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) might want or expect in return.

The fact that one congressional [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) is asking the right questions is a start, but it’s hardly enough.

As for the groom’s father, [Donald Trump Sr.](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [addressed the story](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-says-don-jr-repaid-russian-oligarch-who-spent-hundreds-of-thousands-on-wedding-celebrations) for the first time on Friday, insisting that the arrangement was “totally allowed.”

As part of the same exchange, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said of his oldest son, “As I understand it, he paid him back.” He then hedged a bit on the timing, adding, “I understand that [Don](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s paying them back.”

There are not yet any details as to whether that has happened, whether it will happen, how much [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) intends to repay the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch, or why he would even bother if all of this was “totally allowed.”
