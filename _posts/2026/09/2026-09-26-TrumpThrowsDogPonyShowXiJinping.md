## [Trump pulls out all the stops for China’s Xi](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-xi-meeting-china-tea-state-dinner)

_With few deliverables expected, the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) is counting on regular summitry to keep the relationship stable._

By [Felicia Schwartz](https://www.ms.now/author/felicia-schwartz) and [Emily Hung](https://www.ms.now/author/emily-hung)

Sept. 24, 2026, 5:00 AM EDT

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is [treating [
Chinese President Xi Jinping](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-china-trade-xi-jinping-state-dinner-iran) to a three-day [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) extravaganza featuring blaring trumpets, stealth bomber and fighter jet flyovers, and the [U.S. Marine Band](https://www.marines.mil/) playing both the [American](https://www.usa.gov/national-anthem) and [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) national anthems.

Also planned to honor the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) leader: an opulent state dinner, a private tea meeting and hundreds of U.S. military personnel from all branches marching and carrying flags from both countries

The pageantry is the point.

“This is less a summit and more a spectacle,” [Kurt Campbell](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kurt-campbell/), the [Biden administration](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/)’s top Asia policy expert, said of the gathering.

American officials have made clear in conversations with lawmakers and allies that deliverables will be few, according to a U.S. official and a Western diplomat. The most important outcome of 2 1/2 days of fanfare is that the world’s two largest economies are talking to each other and keeping those communication channels open, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) officials said.

“No matter what our disagreements and differences may be with the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) government, this is not some small country. This is the second largest economy in the world, and a pretty substantial military,” [Secretary of](https://www.state.gov/biographies/marco-rubio/) [State](https://www.state.gov/) [Marco Rubio](https://www.state.gov/biographies/marco-rubio/) said. “It’s responsible statecraft for our leaders to speak and meet with one another.”

Lawmakers on the right and left have criticized [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) for offering [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) such a lavish welcome. [Rubio](https://www.state.gov/biographies/marco-rubio/) on Wednesday rejected such criticism as “outrageous.”

“The relationship between the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) and [China](https://www.gov.cn/) will define the 21st century,” he said hours before [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) met [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) at [Andrews Air Force Base](https://www.jba.af.mil/). “The idea that we would not interact with them at the highest levels is irresponsible. It’s outrageous. We have to.”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has spent months preparing for [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm)’s highly anticipated visit and has repeatedly touted his “great relationship” with the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) president. In June, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) ordered restoration of the [North Portico](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) columns and security enhancements to its doors, as well as round-the-clock construction of the helipad on the [South Lawn](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), taking it upon himself to survey progression of the now complete projects by foot and air.

Former senior U.S. officials, experts and analysts said the summit isn’t likely to produce many, if any, substantial results, though the two leaders are expected to discuss a wide range of topics, including [Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/), the [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) war, artificial intelligence, trade and [China](https://www.gov.cn/)’s holding of political prisoners.

“This is basically two geopolitical strongmen circling each other on the playground, trying to determine what their next move is,” said [Campbell](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kurt-campbell/), who is now the chair and co-founder of the [Asia Group](https://theasiagroup.com/). “The current dynamic in the competition has a little bit of the feel of a race. Who is going to get there first? Who is going to reduce their vulnerability and, as a result, accumulate a new source of leverage?”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) was received with fanfare when he traveled to [China](https://www.gov.cn/) earlier this year. But [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is going much bigger. The [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) sees [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm)’s visit as a chance to “lock in” some of the things the powers agreed to in Beijing in February, such as investment deals and market access,” a U.S. official said.

Even if the summit yields few concrete deliverables, some in the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) see regular meetings between the leaders as part of a process that will buy Washington time to catch up with [China](https://www.gov.cn/) in the race to secure critical minerals, the U.S. official said. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) are expected to meet again at the [APEC](https://www.apec.org/) summit in [Shenzhen](https://www.sz.gov.cn/en/), [China](https://www.gov.cn/), in November and at the [G20](https://g20.org/) summit in [Miami](https://www.miami.gov/) in December.

The spectacle began Wednesday with [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s decision to buck six decades of diplomatic tradition and greet [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) at [Andrews Air Force Base](https://www.jba.af.mil/) — the first time a [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has received a world leader there since 1962. It continues Thursday, with a grand arrival ceremony at the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), including a flyover with a [B-2 Spirit](https://www.northropgrumman.com/what-we-do/aircraft/b-2-stealth-bomber) stealth bomber used to strike [Iran](https://irangov.ir/)’s underground nuclear facilities. At the [Great Hall of the People](http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/englishnpc/GreatHall/node_3072.htm) in [Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/), [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) was welcomed by flag-waving schoolchildren singing and shouting “welcome” in Mandarin.

Later on Thursday, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is set to host a state dinner in [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm)’s honor, with an array of American CEOs in attendance: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/)’s [Jeff Bezos](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/officers-and-directors/default.aspx), [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/) and [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/)’s [Elon Musk](https://ir.spacex.com/leadership/default.aspx), [Google](https://about.google/)’s [Sundar Pichai](https://abc.xyz/investor/board-and-governance/), [Dell](https://www.dell.com/en-us)’s [Michael Dell](https://www.dell.com/en-us/learn/our-company/lp/who-we-are/michael-dell), [Nvidia](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/)’s [Jensen Huang](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/bios/jensen-huang), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/)’s [Sam Altman](https://blog.samaltman.com/), [Citigroup](https://www.citigroup.com/global)’s [Jane Fraser](https://www.citigroup.com/global/about-us/leadership/jane-fraser) and [Apple](https://www.apple.com/)’s [Executive Chair Tim Cook](https://www.apple.com/leadership/tim-cook/).

Ahead of the dinner, the U.S. and [China](https://www.gov.cn/) agreed to extend a trade truce that had been set to expire on Nov. 10. [Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/) [Secretary Scott Bessent](https://home.treasury.gov/about/general-information/officials/scott-bessent) announced late Wednesday afternoon after an unannounced meeting with [China](https://www.gov.cn/)’s vice premier that the “economic detente between the two countries” will be extended through Jan. 10.

Under the current arrangement reached in [Busan](https://www.busan.go.kr/), [South Korea](https://www.mois.go.kr/), in October 2025, [Washington](https://dc.gov/) cut tariffs and eased export controls in exchange for [Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/) pausing rare earth restrictions and resuming soybean purchases.

But as [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) grows more powerful, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has little leverage to prod the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) leader to change his behavior. It’s the personal relationship between the leaders of the world’s two largest economies that will be most on display. Analysts said [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) may not place as much value on it as [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) does.

“[Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) values access and predictability, but he is much less sentimental about rapport,” said [Craig Singleton](https://www.fdd.org/team/craig-singleton/), senior director of the [Foundation for Defense of Democracies](https://www.fdd.org/)’ [China](https://www.gov.cn/) program. “For [Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/), a warm relationship with [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is useful if it produces space, it delays pressure, it keeps the bilateral relationship manageable and predictable.”

“That’s why I think the relationship can look warm at the top, while the systems underneath remain intensely competitive,” continued [Singleton](https://www.fdd.org/team/craig-singleton/), a former U.S. diplomat. “[Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) does not need friendship from [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/). He needs room to maneuver, and so that’s what the summit’s going to produce for him.”
