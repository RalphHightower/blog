## [Trump’s fawning over Xi won’t advance U.S. interests](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-xi-china-summit)

_The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s garish show for the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) leader is no substitute for the hard work needed to make the American people safer and more prosperous._

By [Jonathan Fritz](https://www.ms.now/author/jonathan-fritz-2)

Sept. 25, 2026, 5:45 PM EDT

From the perspective of advancing U.S. interests on issues that directly matter to the American people, [Xi Jinping](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm)’s visit to Washington scored somewhere between low and incomplete. But even as we await the final grades on specific deals hashed out during the summit, the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) is likely headed for a solid F on geopolitical messaging that humiliated the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) — and resulted in no strategic gains for the nation or its allies.

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) rolled out far more than a very long red carpet for the visiting leader of the [People’s Republic of China](https://www.gov.cn/). [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and the first lady met [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) and his wife, [Peng Liyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peng_Liyuan?wprov=sfla1), on the tarmac. (Before [Trump greeted Russian leader Vladimir Putin on the tarmac](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WcghV74xmas) in [Alaska](https://www.alaska.gov/) in 2025, no U.S. [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) had welcomed a foreign leader in that fashion since [John F. Kennedy](https://www.jfklibrary.org/).) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) also provided full official honors, ordered a military jet flyover and gushed about his “great friendship” with the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) leader.

This treatment stands in stark contrast to the way the visiting leaders of America’s most important [Indo-Pacific allies](https://www.pacom.mil/) have been received during [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s second term.

> This treatment stands in stark contrast to the way the visiting leaders of America’s most important [Indo-Pacific allies](https://www.pacom.mil/) have been received during [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s second term.

[Australian](https://www.directory.gov.au/) [Prime Minister](https://www.pm.gov.au/) [Anthony Albanese](https://www.pm.gov.au/about-prime-minister) was met only by the [Australian ambassador]() when he came to [Washington](https://dc.gov/) in fall 2025. [Japanese](https://www.japan.go.jp/) [Prime Minister](https://japan.kantei.go.jp/) [Sanae Takaichi](https://www.sanae.gr.jp/) — who has faced heavy economic and diplomatic pressure from [China](https://www.gov.cn/) ever since telling her parliament that a [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) attack on [Talwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/) might constitute an existential threat for [Japan](https://www.japan.go.jp/) — arrived at the airport earlier in the week with no bells or whistles at all. Neither [Presiden](thttps://english.president.gov.tw/) [Lai Ching-te](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/644) of [Talwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/) nor his [vice president](https://english.president.gov.tw/Page/645) has been allowed passage through the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) since [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s second inauguration.

And while [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) made public that he pressed [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to adopt a harder line on [Talwan](https://www.taiwan.gov.tw/), we do not know whether [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) pushed back, as he should have, in support of the island democracy.

Allied worries over this jarringly disparate treatment were likely exacerbated by [Secretary of](https://www.state.gov/biographies/marco-rubio/) [State](https://www.state.gov/) [Marco Rubio](https://www.state.gov/biographies/marco-rubio/)’s announcement on the day of [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm)’s arrival that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) planned to invite [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) to the [Group of 20 summit](https://g20.org/) in [Miami](https://www.miami.gov/) this December. This is the same [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) who [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm) has called his “friend of steel” and who continues a brutal war in [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/).

Furthermore, these grand gestures to harsh adversaries of the free world took place while [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) was fighting in court to kick three news outlets, including [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/), out of the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) press pool because he didn’t like how they covered his [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/). That was the backdrop for his private joke to [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm), who famously ordered [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) media outlets to obey the [Communist Party](https://www.12371.cn/). “[China](https://www.gov.cn/) had the friendliest press corps,” the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) of the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) quipped to the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) dictator.

As for actual diplomatic substance, the summit was underwhelming.

> That was the backdrop for his private joke to [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm), who famously ordered [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) media outlets to obey the [Communist Party](https://www.12371.cn/). “[China](https://www.gov.cn/) had the friendliest press corps,” the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) of the [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) quipped to the [Chinese](https://www.gov.cn/) dictator.

Ahead of [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm)’s visit, [Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/) offered predictable sweeteners, like buying more soybeans and banning a new group of chemicals used to make fentanyl. Agricultural sales provide temporary relief for farmers struggling with high and rising costs caused by [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s war of choice with [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), and tighter chemical rules are a helpful step toward addressing America’s ongoing opioid crisis. However, these one-off gestures don’t represent deep progress toward either reliable market access for U.S. exports to [China](https://www.gov.cn/) or stopping the flow of these chemicals globally. Without meaningful action, drug supply chains will simply shift to other countries.

The extension of the trade-war truce for a couple more months was also welcome, though it remains unclear how [Washington](https://dc.gov/) will ensure that [Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/) keeps its word on fully restoring the flow of critical minerals to American and allied manufacturers. On other pressing issues, from managing the risks of rogue artificial intelligence to securing the release of wrongfully detained Americans, no visible progress was made, and the signs — like [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s social media post about leaving AI “exactly where it is” — are not encouraging.

What [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm)’s visit has revealed is that the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) seemingly prioritizes pomp and circumstance over substantive success.

[Washington](https://dc.gov/) should be negotiating from a position of strength. The [United States](https://www.usa.gov/) needs to work closely with our Indo-Pacific partners and allies to push back with firm resolve against [Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/)’s economic and security bullying, even while keeping open channels for pragmatic, respectful diplomacy. That’s the only way to be taken seriously by an unsentimental Leninist like [Xi](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2023-03/10/content_5745919.htm), who likely views [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s welcome as a signal that the U.S. [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) is playing a weak hand.

A garish show is no substitute for the hard work needed to advance U.S. national interests in ways that make the American people safer and more prosperous. The [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) should be held accountable for choosing political theater over real strategy.

[Jonathan Fritz](https://www.ms.now/author/jonathan-fritz-2) is a senior fellow for [China](https://www.gov.cn/) policy at the Center for American Progress. Previously, he served for 32 years in the U.S. [State Department](https://www.state.gov/) as a diplomat, most recently as the principal deputy assistant secretary of state for East Asian and China Affairs.
