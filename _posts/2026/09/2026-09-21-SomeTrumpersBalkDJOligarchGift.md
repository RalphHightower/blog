## [Some GOP senators balk at Russian oligarch paying for Trump Jr.’s wedding party](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/donald-trump-jr-wedding-party-russia-oligarch-republicans-fbi)

_“A [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch paid for a lavish wedding after-party for [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), which to me just stinks,” [Utah](https://www.utah.gov/)’s [John Curtis](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) said. “It’s corruption.”_

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Sept. 21, 2026, 11:43 AM EDT

As last week got underway, [ProPublica reported](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) that that [Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch close to [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/), had “secretly bankrolled” part of [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s recent wedding, footing the bill for “hundreds of thousands of dollars of wedding expenses” and even helping to plan the gathering.

“It’s not clear why [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) helped pay for [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s wedding,” [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) added. “The men appear to have met only recently.”

It was [a tough story to shrug off](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/donald-trump-jr-wedding-party-russian-oligarch-putin-garcia). A [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/)-aligned [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch paid for the wedding party of the sitting American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s oldest son, and it was an open question as to why that happened and what the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) might want or expect in return. Such questions were among the reasons [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org) on the [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/) immediately [launched an investigation](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/donald-trump-jr-wedding-party-russian-oligarch-putin-garcia).

But what about [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/)? Were they prepared to defend this?

During a [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) hearing, [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/) [Director Kash Patel](https://www.fbi.gov/about/leadership-and-structure/director-patel) [claimed last week](https://www.ms.now/liveblog/fbi-director-kash-patel-senate-hearing-todd-blanche-live-updates#live-blog-1365274) that he had never heard of the controversy. Around the same time, during a [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) press briefing, [Attorney General](https://www.justice.gov/) [Todd Blanche](https://www.justice.gov/ag/staff-profile/meet-attorney-general) [assured reporters](https://bsky.app/profile/thebulwark.com/post/3mvldkjowht2z) that the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) “doesn’t know” [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), suggesting that [Blanche](https://www.justice.gov/ag/staff-profile/meet-attorney-general) and [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had at least one conversation about the burgeoning controversy, though the [attorney general](https://www.justice.gov/) claimed he had no additional information.

Hours later, [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Bernie Moreno (T-OH)](https://www.moreno.senate.gov/) refused to discuss the matter during a [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/) interview, [except to say](https://bsky.app/profile/atrupar.com/post/3mvlybsydju2n), “Look, you compare [Don Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) to [Hunter Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Biden?wprov=sfla1), and you have one that’s a complete drug-addicted degenerate compared to a great human being.” (One assumes he meant that [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) was the “great human being.”)

But at least some people in [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) circles have expressed a willingness to acknowledge the reality of the story. [The Hill reported](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6099233-curtis-calls-trump-jr-wedding-corruption/?tbref=hp):

> _[Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [John Curtis (T-UT)](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) on Friday ripped [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s son [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) for letting a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch with ties to the [Kremlin](http://kremlin.ru/) pay hundreds of thousands of dollars to finance his wedding after-party in the Bahamas this year._

> _[Curtis](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) called the generous gift “corruption” and pledged to say more about it in coming days._

“A [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch paid for a lavish wedding after-party for [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), which to me just stinks. It’s corruption, I don’t like it,” [Curtis](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/) said in a video posted to social media. The [Utah](https://www.utah.gov/) [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) added, “You’ll hear more from me later on. This is a big deal.”

Two days later, [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Bill Cassidy (T-LA)](https://www.cassidy.senate.gov/) conceded during an appearance on [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/)’ [“Face the Nation”](https://www.cbsnews.com/face-the-nation) that the circumstances surrounding the wedding controversy [look “really bad.”](https://bsky.app/profile/atrupar.com/post/3mvxhdldvxd2h)

As for the groom’s father, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [addressed the story](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-says-don-jr-repaid-russian-oligarch-who-spent-hundreds-of-thousands-on-wedding-celebrations) for the first time on Friday, insisting that the arrangement was “totally allowed.”

As part of the same exchange, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said of his oldest son, “As I understand it, he paid him back.” He then hedged a bit on the timing, adding, “I understand that [Don](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s paying them back.”

There are not yet any details as to whether that has happened, whether it will happen, how much [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) intends to repay the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch, or why he would even bother if all of this was “totally allowed.”

A day before the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s comments, [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org)ic [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Jim Himes (D-CT4)](https://himes.house.gov/), the ranking member on the [House Intelligence Committee](https://intelligence.house.gov/), wrote to [Patel](https://www.fbi.gov/about/leadership-and-structure/director-patel) to [demand scrutiny](https://www.ms.now/news/rep-jim-himes-demands-fbi-investigate-russian-oligarchs-funding-of-trump-jr-wedding-events) of what transpired. “If accurate,” [Himes](https://himes.house.gov/) wrote, the reporting “presents significant counterintelligence concerns that must be thoroughly investigated by the [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/).”

[Patel](https://www.fbi.gov/about/leadership-and-structure/director-patel) has not yet responded. Watch this space.
