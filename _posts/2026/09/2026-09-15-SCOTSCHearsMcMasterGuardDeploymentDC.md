## SC Supreme Court Hears Challenge To McMaster’s National Guard Deployment

Sep 15, 2026  [#SouthCarolina](https://www.sc.gov/) [#SupremeCourt](https://www.sccourts.org/courts/supreme-court/) [#NationalGuard](https://www.scguard.ng.mil/)
The [South Carolina Supreme Court](https://www.sccourts.org/courts/supreme-court/) hears oral arguments at [Clemson University](https://www.clemson.edu/) in a challenge to [Governor](https://governor.sc.gov/) [Henry McMaster](https://governor.sc.gov/governors-biography/)’s deployment of [South Carolina National Guard](https://www.scguard.ng.mil/) members to [Washington, D.C.](https://dc.gov/)

In Case No. 2026-000032, the [South Carolina Public Interest Foundation](https://www.scpif.org/) and James Weninger contend [McMaster](https://governor.sc.gov/governors-biography/)’ exceeded his legal authority when he authorized the deployment.

The defendants are [McMaster](https://governor.sc.gov/governors-biography/)’, in his official capacity as [governor](https://governor.sc.gov/), and [Major General Robin B. Stilwell](https://scmd.sc.gov/about), in his official capacity as adjutant general of the [South Carolina National Guard](https://www.scguard.ng.mil/).

Attorneys representing the plaintiffs include lawyers from the [ACLU of South Carolina](https://www.aclusc.org/), The Carpenter Law Firm and [Protect Democracy](https://protectdemocracy.org/). The governor and adjutant general are represented by their respective legal offices.

[South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) [Senate President Thomas Alexander](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/member.php?code=0013636362) and [House Speaker Murrell Smith](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/member.php?code=1703408887) filed an amicus brief in the case. Several environmental, conservation and voting-rights organizations also participated as amici curiae.

Read more [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) legal and political coverage at [FITSNews](https://www.fitsnews.com/).

Case: [South Carolina Public Interest Foundation](https://www.scpif.org/) and James Weninger v. [Henry McMaster](https://governor.sc.gov/governors-biography/) and [Major General Robin B. Stilwell](https://scmd.sc.gov/about)
Case No.: 2026-000032
Location: [Clemson University](https://www.clemson.edu/)

### [Gov. Henry McMaster Authorizes Deployment of National Guard to Washington, D.C. / S.C. Governor Henry McMaster](https://governor.sc.gov/news/2025-08/gov-henry-mcmaster-authorizes-deployment-national-guard-washington-dc)

August 16, 2025
COLUMBIA, S.C. – [Governor](https://governor.sc.gov/) [Henry McMaster](https://governor.sc.gov/governors-biography/) today authorized the deployment of [200 South Carolina National Guardsmen](https://www.scguard.ng.mil/) to [Washington, D.C.](https://dc.gov/) The guardsmen will support federal law enforcement activities under [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald J. Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)'s executive order to [Restore Law and Order in the District of Columbia](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/08/restoring-law-and-order-in-the-district-of-columbia/).

The guardsmen are subject to immediate recall if a hurricane or other natural disaster requires their return to [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/).

“[South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/) is proud to stand with [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) as he works to restore law and order to our nation's capital and ensure safety for all who live, work, and visit there,” said [Governor](https://governor.sc.gov/) [Henry McMaster](https://governor.sc.gov/governors-biography/). “As our [National Guard](https://www.scguard.ng.mil/) works to support [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s mission, should a hurricane or natural disaster threaten our state, these men and women can and will be immediately recalled home to respond.”

The governor’s order comes at the request of the [Pentagon](https://www.defense.gov/)’s [National Guard Bureau](https://www.nationalguard.mil/). The deployment will be funded by the federal government under Title 32.
