## [‘Rushed, risky and haphazard’: Whistleblower account of USPS mail ballot process alarms Democratic lawmaker](https://www.ms.now/news/rushed-risky-and-haphazard-whistleblower-account-of-usps-mail-ballot-process-alarms-dem-lawmaker)

_An anonymous federal official says the internal process to carry out [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s executive order is untested and could “derail” the midterm elections._

By [Casey Gannon](https://www.ms.now/author/casey-gannon)
Sept. 1, 2026, 6:04 AM EDT

A whistleblower is claiming that the [US Postal Service](https://www.usps.com/)’s process to implement the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/)’s mail-in ballot restrictions is technically fraught, citing a rushed timeline and lack of testing on the system to get it done, according to a [letter](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28591229-2026-08-31-final-letter-disclosure/) sent to the postmaster general from [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Richard Blumenthal (D-CT)](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/).

The whistleblower is described in an attachment to [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/)’s letter as an anonymous federal official with direct knowledge of [USPS](https://www.usps.com/)’ new system for handling federal election mail ballots. They allege that the process the [Postal Service](https://www.usps.com/) is implementing to carry out [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s [executive order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/03/ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections/) is “rushed” and “risky and haphazard,” according to the letter, which has also been sent to the [Postal Service](https://www.usps.com/)’s [inspector general](6).

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s executive order directing the government agency to deliver mail-in ballots only to eligible voters on federal citizenship lists is being fiercely fought in multiple courts. Most recently, a federal judge in [Massachusetts](https://www.mass.gov/) [temporarily paused the implementation](https://www.ms.now/news/judge-blocks-trumps-effort-to-restrict-mail-in-voting-again) of the [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) provision in [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s executive order. On Monday, the judge clarified that [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) was allowed to continue developing the system while the implementation of it remains paused.

[Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) details allegations that the **USPS Federal Ballot Mail Portal** — designed to screen ballots submitted by state election officials before being sent to voters — is technically flawed and has been put on an “impossible” timeline to finish before the midterm elections.

Additionally, [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) says that without the needed testing, such as debugging the software and customer acceptance testing, there is risk of “catastrophic failure” that has the potential to “derail the midterm elections.”

“The whistleblower’s allegations make clear that [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) lacks the technical or operational capability needed to effectively implement the [EO](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/03/ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections/)’s provisions in a way that safeguards every citizen’s right to vote in the upcoming midterm elections,” [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) wrote.

“As a result of this rushed process, [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) has been unable to conduct tests of the Portal to ensure its proper functioning, troubleshoot problems, or distribute instructions on use to state election officials,” [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) says in his letter.

In a [In a statement issued Tuesday](https://about.usps.com/newsroom/statements/090126-statement-on-development-of-us-federal-ballot-mail-portal.htm), the [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) said it is “finalizing” the portal and that election officials will be provided tools to help them understand and familiarize themselves with the system.

“The portal is just one part of a broader, year-round effort by the [Postal Service](https://www.usps.com/) to ensure that election mail and ballots are handled and delivered securely, reliably, and efficiently. [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) regularly works with election officials, mail service providers, and the broader mailing community to provide guidance on election-mail procedures, operational standards, and best practices,” according to the statement.

[USPS](https://www.usps.com/) added that it is reviewing the concerns from [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) and the whistleblower and that it welcomes “appropriate oversight.”

“Regardless of political party or perspective, we share a common goal: ensuring that Americans can have confidence that their election mail will be handled securely and delivered reliably,” the statement read.

[Democrats](https://www.democrats.org%) on the [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/) weighed in Tuesday on the whistleblower accusations, saying the mail-in ballot situation is “more serious than previously known.”

“([President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is creating a new tracking system at the [US Postal Service](https://www.usps.com/) that is untested, dangerous, and threatens to totally disrupt ballot delivery for millions of American citizens. This is an unconstitutional and dangerous power grab and must be permanently and immediately blocked,” [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Robert Garcia (D-CA42)](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/) said in a statement.

The [whistleblower account](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28591229-2026-08-31-final-letter-disclosure/), provided to [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) by [Whistleblower Aid](https://whistlebloweraid.org/), claims that the technology needed to build and complete the portal could take a year or more. Instead, the account says, [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) is rushing to finish the portal less than six months after [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) signed his executive order, with a target launch date of Sept. 1.

The first mail ballots that could test this new system may be from [North Carolina](https://www.nc.gov/), where mail ballots are required to be sent out to voters by Sept. 4. Officials there have said they have received [“virtually no information”](https://www.ms.now/news/democratic-states-take-another-swing-at-blocking-trumps-effort-to-restrict-mail-in-voting) on how [USPS](https://www.usps.com/)’ new rules are being implemented.

[Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) has asked [USPS](https://www.usps.com/) to respond to his letter by Sept. 8, telling reporters he believes the agency is “sabotaging” mail-in voting for the upcoming midterm elections.

“The main takeaway for me is that the [Postal Service](https://www.usps.com/) has designed a system to disenfranchise millions of Americans,” [Blumenthal](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/) told reporters.
