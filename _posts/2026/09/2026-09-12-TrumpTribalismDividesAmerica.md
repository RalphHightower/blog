title: "Trump’s Tribalism Divides America"
---

During an August 2024 [Meet the Press](https://www.nbcnews.com/meet-the-press) edition, [Senator](https://www.senate.gov/) [Lindsey Graham (T‑SC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Graham?wprov=sfla1) berated Republicans who endorsed [Kamala Harris](https://kamalaharris.com/). That moment crystallized the divide for me: Country Over Party versus Party Over Country.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has hijacked the Republican Party and perverted it into the [Trumpian Party](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/). There are no true Republicans left in [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/); they’ve drunk the [Jim Jones Flavor‑Aid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Jones?wprov=sfla1).

Only a few voices still speak with clarity above the noise — people like [Michael Steele](https://www.ms.now/author/michael-steele) and [John Kasich](https://www.ms.now/author/john-kasich).

[Trumpism](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) relies on tribalism to divide Americans, using coded language and constant vilification. Since 2025, I’ve said that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is not President of the United States — he is President of the Divided States of America. Nothing illustrates that better than granting [FEMA](https://www.fema.giv/) aid to red states while denying aid to blue states.

Here in [South Carolina](https://www.sc.gov/), [Trumpism](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has permeated the state level.

Our [Attorney General](https://www.scag.gov/), [Alan Wilson](https://www.scag.gov/about-the-office/meet-the-attorney-general/), has become one of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s most ardent water boys. At every court level where [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s unconstitutional executive orders are adjudicated, [Wilson](https://www.scag.gov/about-the-office/meet-the-attorney-general/) — along with members of the [Trump‑aligned Attorneys General Association](https://republicanags.com/) — files amicus briefs defending him.

The good news: [Wilson](https://www.scag.gov/about-the-office/meet-the-attorney-general/) will no longer be [Attorney General](https://www.scag.gov/).
The bad news: he’s running for [governor](https://governor.sc.gov/). And if past trends hold, he’ll likely win with around 56% of the vote.

I hope he chooses to serve all [South Carolinians](https://www.sc.gov/). But I’m not holding my breath.
