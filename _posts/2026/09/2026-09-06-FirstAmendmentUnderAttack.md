## A Future That Demands a Unified Front
### A Call to Broadcasters, Publishers, and Every Guardian of the First Amendment

The last few years have revealed a pattern that can no longer be ignored. Broadcasters are under pressure. Publishers are being sued. Editorial boards are being leaned on. From [ABC](https://www.abc.com/)’s license review to lawsuits against the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/), [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/), and the [Des Moines Register](https://subscribe.desmoinesregister.com/), the message is unmistakable:

**No single outlet is safe when political power is used to intimidate the press.**

And that means the future of American journalism — radio, television, digital, and print — depends on whether these institutions stand alone or stand together.

---

### The Future If They Stand Alone

If each broadcaster or publisher fights its own battle in isolation, the pressure will continue to escalate.
- ABC today.
- AM/FM radio tomorrow.
- Local newspapers next.
- Digital outlets after that.

A fragmented press is a vulnerable press.
A vulnerable press is a chilled press.
And a chilled press is no longer free.

---

### The Future If They Stand Together

A unified front — broadcasters, publishers, radio networks, digital platforms, editorial boards — sends a message louder than any lawsuit or regulatory filing:

**The First Amendment is not negotiable.**

When the entire media ecosystem stands shoulder‑to‑shoulder, it becomes clear that:
- No administration can intimidate the entire press corps.
- No regulator can silence every broadcaster.
- No lawsuit can frighten every publisher.

Unity transforms pressure into resistance.
Resistance transforms fear into resolve.
Resolve protects the First Amendment.

---

### Why Unity Matters Now

Because the pattern is widening.
Because the stakes are rising.
Because the pressure is no longer limited to one network or one newspaper.

History shows something important:
When the press stands together, political actors back down.
Not because they suddenly agree — but because they realize the cost of escalation is too high.

This is not about partisanship.
This is not about candidates.
This is not about predicting outcomes.

It’s about the **structure** of democracy itself.

---

### A Call to Action

**The future of American journalism depends on unity. Broadcasters and publishers must stand together — not as competitors, but as defenders of the First Amendment. A unified press is a protected press. A fragmented press is a vulnerable one. If the entire media landscape stands up for constitutional freedom, intimidation collapses under its own weight.**

📡 # Major Conglomerates in Broadcasting, Radio, and Publishing

Understanding who the major players are in American media is essential for recognizing the scale of influence — and the scale of responsibility — these organizations carry. These are the companies whose unified support would send an unmistakable message that the First Amendment is sacred.

---

## 📡 Broadcast Television Conglomerates

- **[Nexstar Media Group](https://www.nexstar.tv/)** — Largest local TV owner in the U.S., with more than 200 stations and ownership of [The CW](https://www.cwtv.com/) network.
- **[Sinclair Broadcast Group](https://sbgi.net/)** — Roughly 190 stations nationwide, known for centralized “must‑run” content.
- **[Gray Television](https://graymedia.com/)** — Around 180 stations, strong presence in mid‑sized markets.
- **[Hearst Television](https://www.hearst.com/television)** — Approximately 30 stations with highly regarded newsrooms.
- **[Tegna](https://www.tegna.com/)** — About 60 stations, formerly part of Gannett.
- **[Paramount Global](https://www.paramount.com/)** — Owns [CBS](https://www.cbs.com/) network and its owned‑and‑operated stations.
- **[Disney](https://www.disney.com/)** — Owns [ABC](https://abc.com/) network and eight owned‑and‑operated stations.
- **[Fox Corporation](https://www.foxcorporation.com/)** — Owns the [Fox](https://www.fox.com/) network and 29 owned‑and‑operated stations.
- **[NBCUniversal](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/)** — Owns [NBC](https://www.nbc.com/) network and 12 owned‑and‑operated stations.

---

## 📻 Radio Conglomerates (AM/FM)

- **[iHeartMedia](https://www.iheartmedia.com/)** — Roughly 850 stations, the largest radio owner in the United States.
- **[Audacy, Inc.](https://audacyinc.com/)** — Around 230 stations, strong in major urban markets.
- **[Cumulus Media](https://www.cumulusmedia.com/)** — About 400 stations, major presence in talk radio.
- **[Salem Media Group](https://salemmedia.com/)** — Religious and conservative talk programming across numerous stations.
- **[Beasley Media Group](https://bbgi.com/)** — Approximately 60 stations in regional clusters.
- **[Cox Media Group](https://www.cmg.com/)** — Operates both radio and television stations.
- **[NPR Member Stations](https://www.npr.org/)** — A nationwide network of licensed public radio broadcasters.

---

## 📰 Publishing Conglomerates

- **[The New York Times Company](https://www.nytimes.com/)** — Publisher of [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/), [Wirecutter](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter), and international bureaus.
- **[News Corp](https://newscorp.com/)** — Owns The [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/), [New York Post](https://nypost.com/), and [Dow Jones](https://www.dowjones.com/).
- **[USA Today Co., Inc.](https://www.usatodayco.com/)** — Largest newspaper owner in the U.S., including [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/) and more than 200 local papers.
- **[McClatchy Media Company](https://mcclatchy.com/)** — Publisher of [The Sacramento Bee](https://www.sacbee.com/), [Miami Herald](https://www.miamiherald.com/), [Charlotte Observer](https://www.charlotteobserver.com/), and others.
- **[Hearst Communications](https://www.hearst.com/)** — Owns the [San Francisco Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/), [Houston Chronicle](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/), and numerous magazines.
- **[Advance Publications](https://www.advance.com/)** — Parent company of [The Oregonian](https://www.oregonlive.com/), [Cleveland Plain Dealer](https://www.plaindealer.com/), and [Condé Nast](https://www.condenast.com/).
- **[The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/)** — Owned by Jeff Bezos.
- **[Lee Enterprises](https://www.lee.net/)** — Roughly 75 newspapers across mid‑sized markets.
- **[Tribune Publishing](https://www.tribpub.com/)** — Publisher of the [Chicago Tribune](https://www.chicagotribune.com/), [Baltimore Sun](https://www.baltimoresun.com/), and other legacy papers.

---

These are the institutions that shape American journalism. Their voices, together, would form a unified front capable of defending the [First Amendment](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-1/) against any attempt to intimidate or silence the press.
