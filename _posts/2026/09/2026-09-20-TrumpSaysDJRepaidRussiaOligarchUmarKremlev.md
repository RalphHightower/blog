## [Trump says Don Jr. repaid Russian oligarch who spent hundreds of thousands on wedding celebrations](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-says-don-jr-repaid-russian-oligarch-who-spent-hundreds-of-thousands-on-wedding-celebrations)

_[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) defended the arrangement as “totally allowed” but said [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) told him he would reimburse [Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) for bankrolling the celebrations._

By [Ebony Davis](https://www.ms.now/author/ebony-davis)

Sept. 18, 2026, 9:59 PM EDT

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said Friday that his son, [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), repaid a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) businessman with ties to [President Vladimir [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) who spent [hundreds of thousands of dollars](https://www.ms.now/opinion/donald-trump-jr-wedding-gift-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev) on his wedding celebrations.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said [the arrangement](https://www.ms.now/news/bettina-trump-wedding-putin-trump-jr-umar-kremlev) was “totally allowed” when asked whether it was appropriate for his son to accept the gift from [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1).

“As I understand it, he paid him back,” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) told reporters in the [Oval Office](https://www.whitehouse.gov/). Moments later, however, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) suggested the repayment might still be underway, saying, “I understand that [Don](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s paying them back.”

It remains unclear whether the payment has been completed and, if so, how much. [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) has not independently verified the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s claim. The [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/), [citing an unnamed person](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/18/trump-says-son-has-paid-back-russian-wedding-party/) close to [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), reported that he had repaid [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1).

[ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) [reported Monday](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) that [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) spent a hefty amount toward [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s May wedding celebrations in the [Bahamas](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/), including the rental of a private island and a fireworks display.

[Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) and his wife, [Bettina Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/), [previously confirmed](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdRVuhhGxFy/?hl=en&img_index=1) that [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) hosted two nights of celebrations after their wedding, describing it as an “extraordinarily generous wedding gift” from a friend. Their statement did not mention plans to reimburse him.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said Friday that he had asked his son about the arrangement after learning about it. The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [previously said](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/15/house-dems-demand-answers-to-trump-jr-s-ties-with-russian-oligarch-01076986) he had “no idea who [Umar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) is” and emphasized that [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) had paid for an after-party rather than the wedding ceremony itself.

The arrangement has drawn scrutiny from congressional [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/)sl. [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Robert Garcia (D-CA42)](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/), the top [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/) on the [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/), opened an investigation this week, seeking records about [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s relationship with [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) and the payments.

[Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Jim Himes (D-CT4)](https://himes.house.gov/), the top [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/) on the [House Intelligence Committee](https://intelligence.house.gov/), also asked [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/) [Director Kash Patel](https://www.fbi.gov/about/leadership-and-structure/director-patel) on Thursday to examine whether the arrangement poses a counterintelligence risk.

[Ebony Davis](https://www.ms.now/author/ebony-davis) is a breaking news reporter for [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) based in Washington, D.C. She previously worked at CNN as a campaign reporter covering elections and politics.
