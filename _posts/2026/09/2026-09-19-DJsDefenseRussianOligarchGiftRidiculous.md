## [Donald Trump Jr.’s defense of a Russian oligarch’s ‘gift’ is ridiculous](https://www.ms.now/opinion/donald-trump-jr-wedding-gift-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev)

A gift is more than just a gift when you’re the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s son.

By [Zeeshan Aleem](https://www.ms.now/author/zeeshan-aleem)

Sept. 16, 2026, 6:00 AM EDT

[Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) hasn’t taken kindly to the recent scrutiny of his [lavish wedding party](https://www.ms.now/news/bettina-trump-wedding-putin-trump-jr-umar-kremlev), following the revelation that it was partially bankrolled by a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru) oligarch with deep ties to the [Kremlin](http://kremlin.ru/). In a joint statement with his [wife](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/), [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) argued that “friendship doesn’t require a political motive” and “sometimes a wedding gift is simply a wedding gift.” His defense might make an ounce of sense if he was not the son of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/).

Investigative media outlet [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/), citing records it reviewed and interviews with three people familiar with the event, revealed on Monday that [Trump Jr.’s three-day party held across two private islands was funded in part by Umar Kremlev](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) was funded in part by [Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), a “[Russia](http://kremlin.ru)n oligarch close to [President Vladimir Putin](http://kremlin.ru/).” [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) heads the [International Boxing Association](https://www.iba.sport/), an organization that has been financed by the [Russia](http://kremlin.ru)n state-owned energy company [Gazprom](http://gazprom.com/). [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) reported that [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) “footed the bill for hundreds of thousands of dollars of wedding expenses,” including paying to rent one of the islands and the fireworks show. (A spokesperson for [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) did not dispute the wedding payments from [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) and told [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) that “[Umar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) is a personal friend of [⁷Don](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/).”)

Without even getting into the political and geopolitical concerns, this arrangement is, on its face, peculiar for a few reasons. [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) said on his podcast that the party was “really small, like, just really close friends,” which makes it all the more striking that [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) helped pay for the celebration and attended. [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) says [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) “speaks limited English,” and [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1)’s office said that he and [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) met “a couple of years ago.” [President Donald Trump](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/15/donald-trump-jr-wedding-investigation-kremlev) said he’s “never heard of” [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1).

> _Given that [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) is the son of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), questions of politics and government ethics cannot be set aside._

Furthermore, why would a new friend offer such an extraordinary contribution — hundreds of thousands of dollars to cover the cost of a private island? That level of expense is something that would typically be covered by either the bride and the groom or their families. Even a party of [Gatsbyesque](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64317) decadence would’ve been easily manageable for [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), who is estimated to have a net worth of [hundreds of millions of dollars](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kylemullins/2025/12/08/how-donald-trump-jrs-fortune-jumped-six-fold-in-a-year/). And of course his father is a [billionaire](https://www.forbes.com/sites/danalexander/article/the-definitive-networth-of-donaldtrump/).

But given that [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) is the son of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), questions of politics and government ethics cannot be set aside. And while there’s no indication that any laws were violated, it certainly violates common sense principles of ethical governance that would guard both against a quid pro quo with the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) — or the appearance of one.

Is it possible that [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) could receive a gift without the expectation of something in return? Sure. But it’s impossible to rule out. That’s because both the giver and the recipient of an eye-poppingly expensive gift are closely connected to their respective governments.

[Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), it should be noted, already bears a shameful history of trying to collaborate with [Russian](http://kremlin.ru) contacts to influence elections. During the 2016 campaign, [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) was approached in an email by one of his father’s [Russia](http://kremlin.ru)n business contacts with ties to the [Kremlin](http://kremlin.ru/), who said he could provide dirt on [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) candidate [Hillary Clinton](https://www.hillaryclinton.com/) that “would be very useful to your father.” [Trump Jr. replied](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/07/11/us/politics/trump-russia-email-clinton.html), “If it’s what you say I love it.” And as [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) recounts, “[Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s June 2016 meeting with a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru) attorney in [Trump Tower](https://www.trumptowerny.com/) became a major focus of the [Robert Mueller](https://www.fbi.gov/history/directors/robert-s-mueller-iii) investigation,” though [Mueller](https://www.fbi.gov/history/directors/robert-s-mueller-iii) concluded there was insufficient evidence to convict [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) of a crime. (In 2017, [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) told [Faux News](https://www.foxnews.com/) host [Sean Hannity](https://www.foxnews.com/person/h/sean-hannity), “In retrospect, I probably would have done things a little differently.”)

Perhaps [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1)’s exorbitant gift to [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) was earnest generosity from one new friend to another. Or perhaps it was a nudge to [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) to advocate on [Russia](http://kremlin.ru)’s behalf with the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) on [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en) or sanctions against [Russia](http://kremlin.ru) or other policy issues. It’s impossible to know. But if [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) cared at all about the possibility of sullying the reputation of the federal government as potentially for sale, he would have declined the gift. He has long demonstrated he has no such qualms.

[Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) and his brother, [Eric Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/erictrump/), have raked in cash with a number of ventures that serve as vehicles for potential corruption, including [a cryptocurrency business](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/20/crypto-bank-world-liberty-trust-company-trump) and [investments in the defense sector.](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/interactive/2026/07/13/trumps-sons-invest-heavily-defense-fathers-administration-pours-money/). (The two have insisted multiple times they are not involved in government decision making.) This hardly registers as a scandal during an [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) when the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [used his office to enrich himself](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2025/08/18/the-number) in an entirely unprecedented manner. (The [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has insisted that [Trump and his family](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has never engaged in conflicts of interest.) That doesn’t make it any less devastating for our democracy.

[Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Robert Garcia, (D-CA42)](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/), the top [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/) on the [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/), [has sent a letter](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/15/donald-trump-jr-wedding-investigation-kremlev) [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [chief of staff Susie Wiles](https://www.linkedin.com/in/susiewiles/) stating that “[Oversight](https://oversight.house.gov/) [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) are investigating the numerous ways in which the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) family have sought to enrich themselves on the basis of their proximity to [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)” and requesting documents and communications that could shed light on connections between the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) and [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1). He sent a separate letter to [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) with similar asks. It’s the right first step, and [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) ought to dig in further should they gain more power in [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) after the midterm elections.
