# Freedom of Speech: A Warning Shot to Every Corner of American Media

2026‑09‑04 3:46 PM
[Trump admin fights ABC lawsuit as watchdogs worry Disney will settle with FCC — Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/trump-admin-fights-abc-lawsuit-as-watchdogs-worry-disney-will-settle-with-fcc/)

The [FCC]()’s early license review of [ABC](https://abc.com/) — paired with public statements criticizing the network’s content — represents a frightening and chilling moment for freedom of speech in the United States. This is not an isolated dispute. It concerns every broadcasting company, every network, every station owner, and every media outlet across TV, AM, FM, and publishing.

If one broadcaster can be pressured, all can be pressured.

This includes:
- National networks like [NBC](https://www.nbc.com/), [CBS](https://www.cbs.com/), [Fox Broadcasting Company, LLC](https://www.fox.com/), and [ABC](https://abc.com/)
- Station conglomerates such as [Gray Media, Inc.](https://graymedia.com/), [Nexstar Media Group, Inc.](https://www.nexstar.tv/), [Sinclair, Inc.](https://sbgi.net/), [Hearst Communications Inc.](https://www.hearst.com/), [Tegna Inc.](https://www.tegna.com/), and others.
- AM and FM radio groups including [iHeartMedia, Inc.](https://www.iheartmedia.com/), [Audacy, Inc.](https://audacyinc.com/), [Cumulus Media, Inc.](https://www.cumulusmedia.com/), and [National Public Radio (NPR)](https://www.npr.org/) member stations
- Publishing institutions from the [The New York Times (NYT)](https://www.nytimes.com/) and [The Wall Street Journal (WSJ)](https://www.wsj.com/) to [USA Today Co., Inc.](https://www.usatodayco.com/), [McClatchy Media Company](https://mcclatchy.com/), [Hearst Communications Inc.](https://www.hearst.com/), and the [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/).

A unified front is needed to demonstrate that the [First Amendment](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-1/) is sacred. When broadcasters and publishers stand together — not as competitors, but as guardians of constitutional freedom — they send a message that intimidation cannot silence a free press.

This moment demands solidarity.
This moment demands resolve.
This moment demands a collective defense of speech itself.

Sep 22, 2025  FAVERSHAM
We couldn't find any lobster costumes, and a whirling freestyle in the middle of a school week was not gonna happen. So - with some extra input from a few media sources to cover instrumental sections - this is our parody rendition of ["Under the Sea"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Sea?wprov=sfla1), one of our old [Disney](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/) favourites for its sheer joy and energy. It was composed by [Alan Menken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Menken?wprov=sfla1) (with cracking lyrics by [Howard Ashman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Ashman?wprov=sfla1)) for the 1989 animated ["The Little Mermaid"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Mermaid_%281989_film%29?wprov=sfla1) and won an [Oscar](https://www.oscars.org/). Of course, the calypso lobster's forlorn plea for an alternative path falls on deaf ears. Which brings us to free speech.

It's been a scary time in lots of ways in the US, with the temperature cranked up again by shocking murders and reckless responses, and sadly now the [First Amendment](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-1/) (for decades a place of some bipartisan accord) seems to be creaking. As we were working on the arrangement, we learned that [Jimmy Kimmel](https://abc.com/show/9bfe2f4f-41ad-4492-a6dd-0b67db180543)'s show had been reinstated by [Disney](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/) - which is great news, having loved meeting him in 2020 albeit under bizarre pandemic circumstances - though we gather that much is still unclear about how the matter is going to play out. Here's hoping that enough uproar is generated that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and co. think again about making their predictable/alarming move on limiting freedom of expression.

We're assuming folk will have enough pointers not to need links, but will keep an eye on comments and can fill in any details!

P.S. Please note: for anyone whose kids watch, Ben forget to put special characters in the swear word at the death of the song, so heads-up on that.
