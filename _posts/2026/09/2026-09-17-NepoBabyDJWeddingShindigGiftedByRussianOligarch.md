## [Trump Jr.'s wife, Bettina, confirms Putin ally paid for wedding party](https://www.ms.now/news/bettina-trump-wedding-putin-trump-jr-umar-kremlev)

_The [Palm Beach](https://townofpalmbeach.com/) socialite said it was unfortunate the arrangement was being cast as “political or sinister.”_

By [Erum Salam](https://www.ms.now/author/erum-salam)

Sept. 14, 2026, 2:45 PM EDT

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s new daughter-in-law, [Bettina](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/), unapologetically confirmed a report that a political ally of [Russian President](http://kremlin.ru/) [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) paid for a lavish weekend following her wedding to [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.ms.now/news/don-jr-1789-capital-investigation).

[ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) [first reported](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) Monday that the couple’s three-day wedding celebration in the [Bahamas](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/) in May, described by the outlet as “[Monte Carlo](https://www.monte-carlo.mc/en/#google_vignette) meets frat party,” was funded by [Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1).

“Apparently our wedding is making headlines again, so a little clarity seems in order,” [Bettina Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/) wrote in a statement shared on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdRVuhhGxFy/?hl=en&img_index=1) on Monday. In her post, she shared other pictures from the event, adding that “Generosity doesn’t automatically come with an agenda.” [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/) last year estimated [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) had amassed a fortune of [several hundred million dollars](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kylemullins/2025/12/08/how-donald-trump-jrs-fortune-jumped-six-fold-in-a-year/), driven in large part by his holdings in cryptocurrencies.

“When our original wedding plans changed, we simply decided to get married before it and arrived at an already planned weekend as newlyweds,” [Bettina Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/) wrote. “Our dear friend [Umar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) very generously hosted two incredible nights of celebrations for us AFTER our wedding.”

[Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), according to documents reviewed by [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/), paid for hundreds of thousands of dollars of wedding expenses, including dancers, a five-tier funfetti cake flown in from [Florida](https://www.myflorida.gov/), fireworks and the rental of the private islands. The payments came via an entity affiliated with the [Dubai](https://www.dm.gov.ae/)-based [International Boxing Association](https://www.iba.sport/), which is headed by [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) and [financially backed](https://www.insidethegames.biz/articles/iba-to-renew-gazprom-sponsorship) by [Gazprom](http://gazprom.com/), a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) state-owned energy corporation.

According to [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/), [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1)’s spokesperson “said the boxing organization itself did not incur expenses for the wedding but did not comment on the payments from the [Dubai](https://www.dm.gov.ae/) entity.”

[Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1)’s press office did tell [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) that he has a “friendly relationship” with [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), whom he first met “a couple of years ago,” adding that “Mr. [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) has never discussed political matters with any of his American friends and acquaintances.” The [IBA](https://www.iba.sport/) did not immediately respond to [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/)’s request for comment about the payments from its affiliated entity.

Also in attendance at [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s wedding was his sister [Ivanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivanka_Trump?wprov=sfla1) and her husband, [Jared Kushner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Kushner?wprov=sfla1), who, despite having no formal position in the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/), has played a prominent role in U.S. negotiations with [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) over its ongoing war with [Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en). The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [skipped his son’s wedding](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-to-skip-eldest-sons-wedding-because-i-have-a-thing-called-iran), saying that “this is not good timing for me. I have a thing called [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) and other things.”

Special counsel [Robert Mueller](https://www.fbi.gov/history/directors/robert-s-mueller-iii) also investigated [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) for his possible connection to [Russian interference](https://www.ms.now/news/former-cia-director-john-brennan-in-court-over-records-related-to-investigations-in-which-he-is-the-target) in the [2016 election](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/federalelections2020.pdf) after he met with a [Kremlin](http://kremlin.ru/)-linked lawyer in [Trump Tower](https://www.trump.com/) in June that year. Ultimately, [Mueller](https://www.fbi.gov/history/directors/robert-s-mueller-iii) [found no evidence](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/mueller-report-no-evidence-trump-knew-about-trump-tower-meeting-n995816) the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) campaign colluded with [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) while drawing no conclusion about whether [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) Sr. obstructed justice.

[Bettina Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/) said of the report on [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1)’s largesse that it is “unfortunate that something so personal and happy can be recast as something political or sinister.”

“And sometimes a wedding gift is simply a wedding gift,” she wrote, adding that the couple is not “embarrassed or apologetic” for accepting the gift.
