## [Nicolle Wallace reacts to Putin ally bankrolling Trump Jr.’s wedding](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-jr-wedding-putin-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev)

_[Wallace](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house) shared “stunning” new reporting that found that a close ally of [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) bankrolled [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s lavish wedding celebration._

[MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/)’s [Nicolle Wallace](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house) said she had to “pick my jaw up from the table” after reading what she called “stunning” new reporting from [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin), which found that a close ally of [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) [bankrolled Donald Trump Jr.’s](https://www.ms.now/news/bettina-trump-wedding-putin-trump-jr-umar-kremlev) lavish May wedding celebration in the [Bahamas](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/).

[Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), who is [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) of the [International Boxing Association](https://www.iba.sport/), reportedly spent hundreds of thousands renting a private island for the event. He also organized a fireworks show for the couple’s guests.

The celebration took place just days after [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) traveled with [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) to [China](https://www.gov.cn/) as part of a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) delegation, according to [Chinese state media](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-05-21/IBA-boss-Kremlev-sees-strong-future-for-boxing-in-China-1NkaOKUBrEc/index.html?). The businessman has been sanctioned by the [Ukrainian government](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en) due to his ties to the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) [president](http://kremlin.ru/).

In a [post on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdRVuhhGxFy/?hl=en&img_index=1), [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s new wife, [Bettina Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/), defended letting [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) pay for the two-day affair, calling it “an extraordinarily generous wedding gift from a friend.”

On Monday’s [“Deadline: White House,” Wallace](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house) asked how one of [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/)’s “cadre of oligarchs” apparently became a member of [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s “inner circle.” She also questioned why the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s son, who, [according to Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kylemullins/2025/12/08/how-donald-trump-jrs-fortune-jumped-six-fold-in-a-year/), boasts a net worth of around \$300 million, would need [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) to foot the bill.

“What is the need for money?” she asked, adding that she thought the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s son was “making money hand over fist.”

According to [Wallace](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house), an affinity for [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) appears to run the [Trump family](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).

“Getting along with the murderous dictator of [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) is an odd point of public pride for [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/),” she said, pointing to the [many times](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/how-trumps-rhetoric-about-zelenskyy-and-putin-has-evolved-in-his-own-words) the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [has boasted](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/takeaways-warm-words-contrast-with-cold-reality-no-deal-trump-putin-summit-2025-08-16/) about his “very good” and “fantastic” personal relationship with [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/).

The [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) host also noted that during the 2016 presidential campaign, [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/) met with a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) lawyer who claimed to have damaging information on [Hillary Clinton](https://www.hillaryclinton.com/). In an email responding to the offer for dirt on his father’s opponent, he responded: “If it’s what you say I love it especially later in the summer.”

A decade later, [Wallace](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house) said the new reporting reveals that the connections between the [Trumps](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) just “keep on coming.”
