## [Democrats start looking for answers on Donald Trump Jr.’s Russian-paid wedding party](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/donald-trump-jr-wedding-party-russian-oligarch-putin-garcia)

_It’s an open question as to why [Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) paid for and helped plan the festivities, and what he might want or expect in return._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Sept. 15, 2026, 2:55 PM EDT

The list of controversies surrounding [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) that congressional [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) are eager to investigate was already staggering, but the latest addition to the list is of particular interest. [Axios reported](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/15/donald-trump-jr-wedding-investigation-kremlev):

> _[[Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Robert Garcia (D-CA42)](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/), the top [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/) on the [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/) is investigating a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch’s reported financial involvement in a celebration related to [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s wedding, [Axios](https://www.axios.com/) has learned._

[Garcia](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/) is poised to be [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ lead investigator if [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) retake the [House](https://www.house.gov/). This probe offers a hint of the direction he’ll go in that role.

“A [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch with close ties to [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) spent hundreds of thousands of dollars bankrolling [Donald Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s wedding. It’s baffling that the son of a [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) would even think about allowing a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch to bankroll his wedding. Is this why [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) skipped his son’s wedding? [Oversight](https://oversight.house.gov/) [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) are investigating and demanding answers immediately,” the ranking member [said in a press statement](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/news/press-releases/ranking-member-robert-garcia-demands-answers-from-trump-jr-after-russian-oligarch-bankrolls-lavish-wedding).

[Garcia](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/) has already [sent separate letters](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/news/press-releases/ranking-member-robert-garcia-demands-answers-from-trump-jr-after-russian-oligarch-bankrolls-lavish-wedding) to [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [chief of staff Susie Wiles](https://www.linkedin.com/in/susiewiles/) and [Trump) Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), requesting extensive information on a voluntary basis. A subpoena would require [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) support, which isn’t going to happen — though the circumstances appear likely to change in the coming months should [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) retake the [House](https://www.house.gov/) majority, reclaim subpoena power and restore [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ oversight role.

The underlying story clearly deserves scrutiny. As this week got underway, [ProPublica reported](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) it was [Umar Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), a [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch close to [Vladimir Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/), who “secretly bankrolled” part of [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/)’s wedding. From the article:

[Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) footed the bill for hundreds of thousands of dollars of wedding expenses, according to records reviewed by [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/) and interviews with three people familiar with the event. The oligarch paid to rent out one of the private islands, where a reception was held and where guests slept.

He also covered other big-ticket items, like the fireworks show, and his team helped plan the event.

It’s important to emphasize that the guest list was fairly small (about 50 people were reportedly in attendance) and according to the groom, the wedding was limited to “really close” friends and family.

It also included a “conspicuous” group of [Russians](http://kremlin.ru/), including [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1), whom the other guests apparently did not know (for example, [Eric Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/erictrump/)’s spokesperson told [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/), “[Eric](https://www.linkedin.com/in/erictrump/) has absolutely no clue who this person is, nor has never heard his name”).

In terms of politics in [Moscow](https://www.mos.ru/), [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) is a rather prominent figure. [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/)’s report noted, for example, that in the days leading up to the wedding, he and [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) were in [China](https://www.gov.cn/) together, which came on the heels of [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/) bestowing him with a state honor, the [Order of Friendship](http://award.gov.ru/).

What’s more, one of [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1)’s companies was chosen to operate the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) national lottery; [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) was rewarded with ownership of [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/)’s largest car dealership company; and [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) is the head of the [International Boxing Association (IBA)](https://www.iba.sport/), “a scandal-plagued sports group that has been financed by the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) state-owned energy giant [Gazprom](http://gazprom.com/).”

As [MS NOW reported](https://www.ms.now/news/bettina-trump-wedding-putin-trump-jr-umar-kremlev), the newlyweds issued a statement arguing that [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) didn’t pay for the wedding itself, but rather, the party that came shortly after the wedding.

I’m not sure how that distinction helps.

As for why Americans should care about a [Putin](http://putin.kremlin.ru/)-liked [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) oligarch paying for the wedding party of the sitting American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s oldest son, it’s an open question as to why, exactly, [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1) paid for and helped plan the festivities and what he might want or expect in return. (Seemingly in response to such open questions, the bride, [Bettina Trump](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bettina-anderson-54abba60/), wrote on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/), “[S]ometimes a wedding gift is simply a wedding gift.”)

The [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) and the [Russian](http://kremlin.ru/) government did not respond to [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/)’s requests for comment. [Kremlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Kremlev?wprov=sfla1)’s press office said he has “a friendly relationship” with [Trump Jr.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/donald-trump-jr-4454b862/), adding that he “has never discussed political matters with any of his American friends and acquaintances.”

It’s a safe bet we haven’t heard the last of this one. Watch this space.
