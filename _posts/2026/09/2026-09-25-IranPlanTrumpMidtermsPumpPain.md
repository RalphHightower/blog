## [‘Pain for Trump’: Iran is trying to escalate the war before the midterms](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-iran-war-midterms-pain)

_[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s actions look increasingly indecisive as [Tehran](https://tehran.ir/) focuses on driving up oil prices._

By [David Rohde](https://www.ms.now/author/david-rohde) and [Felicia Schwartz](https://www.ms.now/author/felicia-schwartz)

Sept. 22, 2026, 5:00 AM EDT

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has hesitated in recent days to expand the war against [Iran](https://irangov.ir/), even as [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/)-backed proxies in [Yemen](https://pmo-ye.net/) have driven up oil prices by escalating attacks against [Saudi Arabia](https://my.gov.sa/) and threatening tanker traffic in the [Red Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Sea?wprov=sfla1).

At the same time, [Iran](https://irangov.ir/)’s strategy is becoming increasingly clear: find new ways to disrupt the international oil supply before the midterm elections to create a political pain point for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).

But that hasn’t convinced [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to take action.

When [Saudi](https://my.gov.sa/) officials asked [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to join [Saudi](https://my.gov.sa/) attacks on [Iran](https://irangov.ir/)-backed [Houthi](https://ansarollah.com.ye/) militia in Eastern [Yemen](https://pmo-ye.net/), [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) declined, according to a person familiar with the phone call who was granted anonymity to describe the sensitive conversation.

For now, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is committed to maintaining a unilateral ceasefire with the [Houthi](https://ansarollah.com.ye/)s, though the group knows [Washington](https://dc.gov/)’s “red lines,” said a U.S. official who was granted anonymity to discuss sensitive diplomacy.

Ted Singer, a former senior [CIA](https://www.cia.gov/) official who oversaw the agency’s operations in the Middle East, said the [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) regime’s goal is to maximize the political damage to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).

Singer said [Iran](https://irangov.ir/)’s focus between now and November is singular: “pain for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).” 

[Tehran](https://tehran.ir/) is clearly suffering economically from a U.S. blockade that has halted [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) oil exports, said Singer, who served in seven countries across the Middle East. But they are gambling that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) will be weakened by the results of the midterms, he said, and the [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) — if they take control of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) — could pass a war powers resolution limiting [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s military options.

The [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) government is probably hurting themselves economically, Singer said, but “they are betting that bad election results will bring more pressure on [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).”

Singer said that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s threat this weekend to [“blow the entire nation up”](https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/trump-iran-war-blow-up-polling-b3053158.html) with air strikes will not necessarily topple the [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) regime. He said the deployment of U.S. ground troops inside [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) or an uprising by [Iranians](https://irangov.ir/) are needed to achieve that goal. 

“Maybe after the midterms, he’s willing to put boots on the ground,” Singer said.

[Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) [President](https://president.ir/en) [Masoud Pezeshkian](https://drpezeshkian.ir/) is expected to arrive at the [U.N.](https://www.un.org/) [General Assembly](https://www.un.org/en/ga/) shortly after [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) returns to [[
Washington](https://dc.gov/).

The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) took the unusual step to grant visas to [Pezeshkian](https://drpezeshkian.ir/) and other senior [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) officials even though [Washington](https://dc.gov/) and [Tehran](https://tehran.ir/) have been fighting a war for more than six months.

While a meeting between the two leaders is unlikely, in an interview with [Faux News](https:/www.foxnews.com/) on Sunday, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said he is “open” to meeting [Pezeshkian](https://drpezeshkian.ir/). There are currently no plans for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to meet the [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), said a U.S. official who was granted anonymity to describe [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s schedule.

[Qatar](https://hukoomi.gov.qa/) and [Pakistan](http://pakistan.gov.pk/) are continuing to try to mediate an end to the fighting, with the U.S. and [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) submitting proposals over the weekend in response to a fresh push to resume diplomatic talks, said a Middle East diplomat who was granted anonymity to describe the closed-door negotiations.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) came close to meeting an [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) at the [United Nations](https://www.un.org/) in 2019, when tensions were high following his decision to withdraw from the [Iranian](https://irangov.ir/) nuclear deal and wage a “maximum pressure” sanctions campaign against [Tehran](https://tehran.ir/), according to two former [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) officials.

“[Iran](https://irangov.ir/) will be a central topic” for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and other members of the U.S. delegation, said a senior U.S. official who was granted anonymity to discuss the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/)’s thinking.

[Gregory Brew](https://www.eurasiagroup.net/people/gbrew), a senior [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) and oil analyst at the [Eurasia Group](https://www.eurasiagroup.net/) told [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) that it’s clear [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s decision-making over how far to go against [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) has waffled.

 “[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has nothing but bad options, and as a result, he’s become more indecisive,’ [Brew](https://www.eurasiagroup.net/people/gbrew) said. 

“Escalation would do little to address his growing economic and political problems, while [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) and its allies have shown they are tough to intimidate — and have potent tools to retaliate,” he said.

[Brew](https://www.eurasiagroup.net/people/gbrew) said [Iran](https://irangov.ir/)’s negotiating position is unchanged. [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) is asking [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to agree to the lapsed June memorandum of understanding that established a ceasefire. [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) also wants the U.S. to recognize [Iran](https://irangov.ir/)’s claims that it controls the [Strait of Hormuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz?wprov=sfla1), a power it did not have before [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) chose to attack [Iran](https://irangov.ir/) seven months ago.

The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) has rejected those terms.

“Having launched this war in February,” [Brew](https://www.eurasiagroup.net/people/gbrew) said, “he seems to have no clear idea of how to escape it.”
