### BOMBSHELL new report on Trump's alleged Epstein ties TERRIFIES Republicans one week before midterms

Oct 27, 2026  [#EpsteinFiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1) [#Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) [#Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)
[Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) on the [House Judiciary Committee](https://judiciary.house.gov/) have released a new [50-page report](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-10.hjc-dems-staff-report-epstein-and-trump.pdf) detailing [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)'s alleged ties to [Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1), claiming to lay out "the case against [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) emerging in the [Epstein Files](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1) and [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/)'s desperate efforts to cover it up." [Nicolle Wallace](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house) is joined by Ranking Member [Jamie Raskin (D-MD8)](https://raskin.house.gov/) for conversation on [Deadline: White House](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house).

[Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Jamie Raskin (D-MD8)](https://raskin.house.gov/) ranking member of the [House Judiciary Committee](https://judiciary.house.gov/), urged every American to read what he called an “extraordinary” [new report](https://www.ms.now/news/house-judiciary-democrats-report-trump-epstein-files) on [Donald Trump](https://www.ms.now/white-house)’s ties to [Jeffrey Epstein](https://www.ms.now/news/epstein-survivors-political-ad-2026-kansas-senate-marshall-mississippi-hyde-smith). [Raskin](https://raskin.house.gov/) told [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/)’s [Nicolle Wallace](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house) that he and his fellow [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) were demanding an independent special counsel to investigate the pair’s decadeslong relationship.
[Deadline: White House](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house)
The [50-page report,](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-10.hjc-dems-staff-report-epstein-and-trump.pdf), released Monday by [House Judiciary Committee](https://judiciary.house.gov/) [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/), drew on interviews with the committee, survivor accounts, [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/) records and documents released under the [Epstein Files Transparency Act](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/epstein-files-transparency-discharge-petition-johnson-congress). [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) allege in the report that a “growing body of evidence demonstrates that [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) was an active participant in [Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1)’s abuse of young women,” and they accuse the [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) and the [FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/) of “doing everything they can to keep the American people in the dark about it.”

In response to the report, a [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) spokesperson said, “None of these allegations are true,” and said [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had been “fully exonerated of anything related to [Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1).” And in [a statement to MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/news/house-judiciary-democrats-report-trump-epstein-files), a [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/) spokesperson called the report “nothing more than a last-ditch effort by [House Judiciary](https://judiciary.house.gov/) [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) to mislead the public.”

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and [Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1)’s relationship, spanning from the 1980s to the early 2000s, is [well-documented](https://www.ms.now/top-stories/latest/epstein-birthday-book-trump-drawing-house-oversight-rcna229846). [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has said their friendship ended around 2004 and that he knew nothing about [Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1)’s crimes. He has denied that he engaged in any sexual misconduct with any woman connected to [Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1).

[Raskin](https://raskin.house.gov/) said the [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/)’s refusal to thoroughly investigate [Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1)’s “enablers” and possible co-conspirators “forced [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/)’” hand. “The idea that you run a billion-dollar global human trafficking ring, and only two people are guilty of crimes, and one of them happens to be deceased,” was hard to believe, he said.

The [Maryland](https://www.maryland.gov/) [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/) also accused the [Justice Department](https://www.justice.gov/) and [Attorney General Todd Blanche](https://www.ms.now/news/former-doj-official-blasts-todd-blanches-attack-on-minnesota-judges-are-you-kidding-me) of failing to cooperate with other investigations into [Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein?wprov=sfla1) and his associates. “With foreign governments that are asking for information from the [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/), [Todd Blanche](https://www.justice.gov/ag/staff-profile/meet-attorney-general) is not helping,” [Raskin](https://raskin.house.gov/) said. “Same with states that are engaged in doing state-level investigations and prosecutions related to potential crimes that were committed in their states.”

“We’re demanding an independent special counsel to take this over from the [Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/), with the hopes that the truth will come out and justice will be done for hundreds and hundreds of victims,” [Raskin](https://raskin.house.gov/) said.

He urged viewers to “read the report,” saying, “The detail is extraordinary. But we’re not even trying to make the case here. We’re not prosecutors. But we’re saying the [Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/) has completely abdicated their responsibility.”

“And why?” [Raskin](https://raskin.house.gov/) asked. “We cannot understand any reason other than they’re trying to protect [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).”

You can watch [Raskin](https://raskin.house.gov/)’s full interview in the clip at the top of the page.
