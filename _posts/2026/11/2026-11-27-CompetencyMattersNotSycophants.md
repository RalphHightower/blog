### [Sean P Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy)

[Sean P Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) serves as the 20th United States [Secretary](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) of [Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/).  From [Hayward](https://cityofhaywardwi.gov/), [Wisconsin](https://www.wisconsin.gov/), [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) earned a bachelor's degree in marketing from [St. Mary's College](https://www.smcm.edu/) of [Maryland](https://www.maryland.gov/) and a Juris Doctor from [William Mitchell College of Law](https://mitchellhamline.edu/) in 1999. Before entering politics, he gained national recognition as a professional lumberjack athlete, competing in [world championships](https://lumberjackworldchampionships.com/), and appeared on [MTV](https://www.mtv.com/)'s ["The Real World: Boston”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Real_World%3A_Boston?wprov=sfla1)" and ["Road Rules: All Stars,”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_Rules%3A_All_Stars?wprov=sfla1) where he met the love of his life, [Rachel Campos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Campos-Duffy?wprov=sfla1).

[Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy)'s legal career began as a special prosecutor in [Ashland County](https://ashlandcountywi.gov/), [Wisconsin](https://www.wisconsin.gov/), where he was later appointed district attorney in 2002, serving until 2010. Elected to the [U.S. House of Representatives](https://www.house.gov/) in 2010, he represented [Wisconsin](https://www.wisconsin.gov/)'s Northwoods in the [7th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin%27s_7th_congressional_district?wprov=sfla1) until 2019, focusing on financial services and economic policy as a member of the [House Financial Services Committee](https://financialservices.house.gov/). His passion was bringing prosperity to rural America. As the lead sponsor of the [Puerto Rico Oversight, Management, and Economic Stability Act (PROMESA)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-114publ187/html/PLAW-114publ187.htm), [Sean Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) addressed the national ramifications of [Puerto Rico](https://www.pr.gov/)'s unprecedented debt crisis and ensured that public services for residents could continue.  After resigning from [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) to prioritize his family amid health challenges with his ninth child, he co-hosted "The Bottom Line" on [Faux Business Network](https://www.foxbusiness.com/) starting in 2023.

As [Secretary, Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) has prioritized infrastructure investments, efficiency, safety, and the speedy delivery of projects. Since July 9, 2025, he has additionally served as acting administrator of [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/). A devoted Catholic and family man, [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) is married to [Rachel Campos-Duffy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Campos-Duffy?wprov=sfla1), a fellow television personality, and they have nine children.

Born in [North Carolina](https://www.nc.gov/), [Secretary](https://www.ed.gov/about/ed-organization/meet-secretary-of-education/linda-e-mcmahon) [McMahon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lindamcmahon/) graduated from [East Carolina University](https://www.ecu.edu/?ref=ecu.edu) in 1969 with a bachelor’s degree in French. Her college aspirations to become a schoolteacher reflect her lifelong interest in education, which she later pursued through educational [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) and advocacy. She served on the [Connecticut State Board of Education](https://portal.ct.gov/sde/board/state-board-of-education), advocating for policies to empower teachers and help students excel, and on the [Board of Trustees](https://www.sacredheart.edu/about-shu/leadership/) at [Sacred Heart University](https://www.sacredheart.edu/) in [Fairfield](https://fairfieldct.gov/), [Connecticut](https://portal.ct.gov/).

[Secretary](https://www.ed.gov/about/ed-organization/meet-secretary-of-education/linda-e-mcmahon) [McMahon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lindamcmahon/) pursued a career in business as the CEO of [World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE)](https://www.wwe.com/), a global company she helped grow from a regional operation into a publicly traded, multibillion-dollar enterprise. Thanks to her leadership, [WWE](https://www.wwe.com/) created thousands of American jobs and sparked [McMahon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lindamcmahon/)’s passion for Career and Technical Education (CTE)^[201]. Throughout her career in business and advocacy, she has firmly held that skills-based career preparation is the backbone of the American economy and the path to the American Dream for every citizen.

##  [Sean Duffy’s FAA failures show experience still matters](https://www.ms.now/opinion/sean-duffy-faa-failures-experience)

_The [transportation secretary](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) has failed to adequately explain to Americans either why last week’s [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) shutdown happened or how it will be fixed._

By [Hayes Brown](https://www.ms.now/author/hayes-brown)

Nov. 17, 2026, 6:00 AM EST

Many Americans would love to hear two things from the federal government after a major, disruptive incident like [last week’s nationwide ground stop](https://www.ms.now/news/faa-ground-stop-all-flights-nationwide): Here’s what went wrong and here’s how we’re going to fix it.

[Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/) [Secretary Sean Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) [five major Sunday morning political shows](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/icymi-secretary-duffys-full-sunday-circuit-outlines-ground-stop-response-and-the-path-forward/) this week — and struggled to deliver either. [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) couldn’t effectively provide either a cause or solution to the issues at the [Federal Aviation Administration](https://www.faa.gov/), laying bare his lack of experience with the issues his own department is supposed to manage.

It’s part and parcel of the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s broader dismissal of pretty much anyone who actually knows what they’re doing in favor of sycophants and people [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) thinks look good on TV.

On [NBC’s “Meet the Press”](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administrationl/transportation-secretary-apologize-nationwide-ground-stop-airports-rcna609397) on Sunday, [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) refused to apologize for the more than four-hour-long incident that led to days of disruptions. “It happened on my watch but we have to be clear: I am moving at an incredible speed that no one has done at the [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) or in decades, and I’m proud of the work we’re doing, the attention we’re bringing to this issue,” he told host [Kristen Welker](). He likewise [likewise told CNN’s Dana Bash](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/15/transportation-secretary-sean-duffy-faa-flight-stoppag): “I take responsibility for what was given to me by prior [administrations](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)sl.”

> It’s part and parcel of the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s broader dismissal of pretty much anyone who actually knows what they’re doing in favor of sycophants and people [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) thinks look good on TV.

It’s true that the [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/)’s outdated systems have been [in desperate need of modernization](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/17/us/politics/air-traffic-control-faa-newark-biden.html) for decades now, but [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), [as is his wont](https://www.ms.now/opinion/trump-hegseth-pirro-blame), went further in dodging blame. [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s knee-jerk reaction to cast blame on the [Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) whenever a federal government agency fails on his watch seems to come from a place of insecurity. People expect competent leaders to know how to fix their mistake — or at least get the ball rolling. The alternative is to be seen as unable or unwilling to clean up your own mess and for an [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) that values optics and narrative over everything, that simply won’t do. The fault then must lay elsewhere.

“The [Biden administration](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/)
 was an [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) disaster. Everything they touched turned bad,” he wrote on Truth Social, adding, “Our [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) is doing a great job, and is ready to go.” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) ignored the fact that he was [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) for four years before [Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/) and didn’t fix what needed fixing either. He has also been back in the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) for almost two years, displaying a continued lack of urgency on [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) modernization, even during a spike in near-misses and other incidents at airports around the country.

Journalist James Fallows recently reported in [The New York Times Magazine](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/12/magazine/air-travel-safety-faa-aviation.html?unlocked_article_code=1.P1E.mrGL.GRz8R7BBMojw&smid=nytcore-ios-share) that, since last year, the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has shed several of the layers of redundancy that make air travel safe, including by disbanding several expert advisory panels. Even seemingly minor acts, like the [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) changing the international identifier of the newly renamed [Donald J. Trump International Airport](https://flydjt.org/) in [Florida](https://www.myflorida.gov/), add another layer of potential failure to an already precarious system. And there’s been plenty of people who know better who would happily tell [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) the same.

[Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) is one of many [Cabinet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/the-cabinet/) members including [Defense](https://www.defense.gov/) [Secretary Pete Hegseth](https://www.defense.gov/About/Biographies/Biography/Article/4040890/hon-pete-hegseth/), [Education](https://www.ed.gov/) [Secretary Linda McMahon](https://www.ed.gov/about/ed-organization/meet-secretary-of-education/linda-e-mcmahon) and [Health and Human Services](http://www.hhs.gov/) [Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](https://www.hhs.gov/about/leadership/robert-kennedy.html) who lack expertise in the complex issues in their portfolios and [undermine](https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/21/health/kennedy-cdc-vaccine-guidance), [fire](https://theintercept.com/2025/04/25/hegseth-defense-policy-board-pentagon-tucker-carlson/) or otherwise [denigrate the work](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/education/linda-mcmahon-department-education-plans-d71e690f) of staffers and other advisers who have built up institutional knowledge.

But just why an upgrade from copper wiring to fiber-optic cable at one remote facility could overwhelm the nation’s air traffic control systems, as [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) claimed Saturday, may be more on him than he’s been willing to admit. [MS NOW has reported](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-sean-duffy-air-traffic-nationwide-ground-stop) that according to sources, including a senior [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) official, it may have been the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s efforts to update the system too quickly that prompted the issue with radar systems nationwide. The goal of finishing the upgrade during [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s term has clashed with the difficult process of converting delicate, critical systems, as last week’s outage has shown.

The [Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107992) also said in a report published in September that the [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) was “buying delivery speed at the risk of cost uncertainty and potential disruptions.” [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) had no real answer when confronted with that report by host Margaret Brennan on [CBS’ “Face the Nation.”](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/sean-duffy-transportation-secretary-face-the-nation-transcript-11-15-2026/)

> We’re left with [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) both struggling to suggest either a fully credible diagnosis or prognosis.

It’s also odd that despite these rush jobs, the [Department of Transportation has reportedly spent only \$1 billion of the \$12.5 billion in funds for upgrades](https://democrats-transportation.house.gov/news/press-releases/ranking-members-larsen-carson-statement-on-last-nights-nationwide-ground-stop) the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)-controlled [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) approved last year. A deeper series of investments in long-term fixes may have been possible if [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) was more personally invested in the matter and better informed to effectively steer the modernization project.

Instead, we’re left with [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) both struggling to suggest either a fully credible diagnosis or prognosis. His criticism of the [Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) likewise comes across as petulant whining. Maybe if [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) had any sort of previous knowledge about [the \$1 trillion industry he oversees](https://www.iata.org/en/iata-repository/pressroom/fact-sheets/industry-statistics), he could be forgiven for choosing to do away with expertise in the department. Without that background, it seems irresponsible at best, dangerous at worst, and has made the odds of a disaster on the runway or in the skies much more likely.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s political appeal lies largely in grievance politics, but it’s somewhat forgotten that when he first launched his [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) campaign in 2015, his pitch was that the country needed a hypercompetent, quick-on-his-feet man of action like [the mostly fictitious version of himself](https://www.asc.upenn.edu/news-events/news/politics-entertainment-media-how-apprentice-helped-trump-2016) he played on [“The Apprentice.”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Apprentice_%28American_TV_series%29?wprov=sfla1) After six years as [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), it’s plain to see that management — especially crisis management — is just not his strength.
