## [Trump nominee for Congress’ watchdog agency faces block](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/tillis-ueland-gao-government-accountability-office-nomination-hold)

_If retiring [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Thom Tillis](https://www.tillis.senate.gov/) sticks to his guns, a [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) plan to neuter the [Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/) will fail._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Nov. 20, 2026, 11:29 AM EST

Thursday was [Eric Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/)’s opportunity to convince members of the [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee](http://www.hsgac.senate.gov/) that he deserves to be confirmed to lead the [Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/) as part of [a rushed and truncated hearing](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/ueland-gao-senate-confirmation-government-accountability-office).

Soon after, there was fresh evidence that he was not persuasive. [Politico reported](https://www.politico.com/live-updates/2026/11/19/congress/tillis-to-block-ueland-01177685):

> [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Thom Tillis (D-NC)](https://www.tillis.senate.gov/) plans to block the speedy confirmation of [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) aide [Eric Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) to head the [Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/) by opposing his inclusion in a catchall nomination package [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) plan to approve next month.

> The [North Carolina](https://www.nc.gov/) [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)’s opposition substantially diminishes the chances [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) will be confirmed as comptroller general in the waning days of [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) control of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/).

The plan on the part of [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) leaders has been to include [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) in an enormous package of presidential nominees, who could be confirmed as a bloc during the lame-duck session (before [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) lose their majority in the new year).

[Tillis](https://www.tillis.senate.gov/), who did not seek re-election this year, might very well end up voting for the package before exiting Capitol Hill, but he doesn’t want [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) to be a part of it.

“I’ve already issued a presumptive hold if it comes out of committee,” the [North Carolinian](https://www.nc.gov/) told [Politico](https://www.politico.com/). “I won’t allow him to go through en bloc.”

As [HuffPost explained](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/2-senators-block-trump-nominee-who-said-capitol-wasnt-attacked-on-jan-6-2021_n_6affb1ffe4b0db044548efb0?origin=home-latest-news-unit), a [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) hold doesn’t necessarily kill a nomination. But with just weeks left in the lame-duck [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) and [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) facing bipartisan opposition, his confirmation odds (which weren’t great to begin with) are suddenly worse.

For the public, the end of [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/)’s nomination would be excellent news. [Russell Vought,](https://www.ms.now/top-stories/latest/trump-russell-vought-project-2025-office-management-budget-rcna181469) the [Project 2025](https://www.ms.now/video-podcast/project-2025-in-action-2511774275665) architect who leads the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) budget office, would prefer that the [Congress’ independent watchdog agency not even exist](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-gao-ueland-white-house-omb-vought) not even exist because it stands in the way of his [legally dubious power grabs](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/white-houses-latest-spending-power-grab-slammed-clear-violation-law-rcna228509), and with this in mind, [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [nominated Euland, Vought’s deputy](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-gao-ueland-white-house-omb-vought) at the [Office of Management and Budget](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/), to lead the [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) — the very entity tasked with pushing back against [OMB](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/) abuses.

I can appreciate why “fox guarding the henhouse” might seem like an overused cliché, but it definitely applies here: The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) nominated a top [OMB](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/) official to lead a watchdog agency responsible for policing the [OMB](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/).

The [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) might seem like any other obscure agency, but it has played an important role as [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ nonpartisan watchdog for generations. Its sole purpose matters a great deal, conducting research, investigations and audits, which in turn serve as the basis for congressional oversight of the executive branch. It is a key instrument in the federal toolbox to combat corruption and federal abuses.

If [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) and [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) successfully neuter it in the lame-duck session by confirming [Vought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Vought?wprov=sfla1)’s right-hand man to a 15-year term, it likely means even less access to reliable, nonpartisan investigations, more corruption and additional abuses.

If [Tillis](https://www.tillis.senate.gov/) sticks to his guns, [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) and his allies won’t get that chance. Watch this space.
