## [Fed’s Lisa Cook to skip dubious hearing before Trump’s ‘committee of inquiry’](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/lisa-cook-federal-reserve-trump-white-house-committee-inquiry)

_After the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s crusade against the [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) governor was blocked, he settled on a radical new course. [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) isn’t playing along._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Nov. 17, 2026, 4:10 PM EST

It was 15 months ago when [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/), exercising a power the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) didn’t appear to have, said he would [fire Federal Reserve governor Lisa Cook](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trumps-offensive-feds-lisa-cook-five-alarm-fire-rcna227188), effective immediately. His motivations were hardly subtle: The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) wanted to seize greater control over the [Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) in part by ousting a [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) appointee and replacing her with a choice of his own.

To date, those efforts have come up short. As recently as June, the [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/) [blocked the White House’s efforts](https://www.ms.now/deadline-white-house/deadline-legal-blog/supreme-court-federal-trade-commission-rebecca-slaughter-humphreys) to remove [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) from office immediately, allowing her to remain on the job while the underlying case continued to be adjudicated.

Days later, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [indicated to CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/cnbc-transcript-president-donald-trump-speaks-with-cnbcs-joe-kernen-today-.html) that he would continue his efforts to remove [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm). He was evidently serious, and last month, the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [established a “committee of inquiry”](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/09/donald-trump-lisa-cook-fed-investigation-01113667) to investigate whether [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) made “false statements in connection with one or more mortgage instruments.”

The underlying allegations remain [awfully difficult to take seriously](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/mortgage-fraud-allegations-generate-even-political-headaches-team-trum-rcna231929), but the committee’s hearing was nevertheless scheduled for Dec 14. As [The Financial Times reported](https://www.ft.com/content/ac8fe697-a48d-4b0a-b556-46ae5463a6e4?accessToken=zwAAAaHrU6v7kdOsj-aXpI1LCtO1VkauVGOm5A.MEUCIQCPlXCg4hYzZsPGlTbkvM7zde0bIPRB5uhNBlBw3Xe0aAIgFC0S6mN_dBbfDE3aoB7Cj8B9N21HnhNtSc4g5MCTsZw&sharetype=gift&token=0db9b5dc-116f-4c0f-ab6f-cf30bcef6833&syn-25a6b1a6=1), however, [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) will not be participating in the circumspect process. From the article:

> [Abbe Lowell](https://www.lowellandassociates.com/aboutabbe), [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm)’s counsel, said in a letter addressed to the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) that [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm), who the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has attempted to fire over unproven accusations of mortgage fraud, had “no legal obligation to participate in an unfair hearing” and had a duty to the US central bank “to decline to participate in any proceeding that would undermine the institution’s unique status and independence.” […]

> [Lowell](https://www.lowellandassociates.com/aboutabbe) said in the letter that the proceedings were “a facade designed to obscure a pre-determined decision to fire [Governor Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm).”

Under the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s plan, [Cook](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/cook.htm) would be subjected to questions from a variety of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) loyalists, including [Kevin Hassett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Hassett?wprov=sfla1), the director of the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [National Economic Council](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/nec/) — an arrangement that reinforces the unavoidable impression the whole process would be little more than a kangaroo court.

[Lowell](https://www.lowellandassociates.com/aboutabbe) said his client is prepared to appear before a panel if the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) appoints “unbiased” officials to examine the allegations, but the lawyer added that efforts to engage with [Team Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) have not produced a “productive path forward.”

It’s worth appreciating just how outlandish these circumstances are. The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) set out to fire a [Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/) member without cause; he faced institutional and legal resistance; and now he’s exploring new ways to advance his crusade, regardless of the evidence or possible concerns about propriety, with an unusual and wildly unnecessary inquiry overseen by members of his own team who are eager to make him happy.

There were already concerns about [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s radical attempts to undermine the [Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/)’s independence. Those concerns continue to get worse — and reach unprecedented heights.
