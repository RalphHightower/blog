## [Trump’s first crypto dinner was indefensible. The second dinner adds insult to injury.](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-crypto-dinner-meme-coin-access-cftc-senate-democrats)

_“Those of us who want to see this [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) succeed can only do so much. You can lead a horse to water, but you can’t make it drink,” one former official said._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Nov. 23, 2026, 11:06 AM EST

When [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) unveiled a meme coin a few days before his second inauguration, the ethical mess was obvious. The [Campaign Legal Center](https://campaignlegal.org/)’s [Adav Noti](https://campaignlegal.org/staff/adav-noti) [explained at the time](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/18/us/politics/trump-meme-coin-crypto.html), “It is literally cashing in on the presidency — creating a financial instrument so people can transfer money to the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s family in connection with his office. It is beyond unprecedented.”

But when the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) and his partners [launched a contest of sorts](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-extends-rare-white-house-opportunity-buy-meme-coin-rcna202855) a few months later, it took the story to a new level. Those interested in investing in [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s meme coin — and by extension giving the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) money — were told they would have a chance to win special access to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) at a special private dinner.

[Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Chris Murphy (D-CT)](https://www.murphy.senate.gov/) [said of the scheme](https://x.com/ChrisMurphyCT/status/1915138398714753437), “This isn’t [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) just being [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/). The [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) coin scam is the most brazenly corrupt thing a [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has ever done. Not close.”

The gambit [proved predictably lucrative](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/problems-white-houses-defense-trumps-scandalous-crypto-dinner-rcna208749) in what [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/)’s [Chris Hayes described](https://bsky.app/profile/allinwithchris.bsky.social/post/3lpsgr657lc2y) as “the most brazen act of corruption by a [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) in our lifetimes, probably in a century, possibly ever.”

And then, he did it again. [The Wall Street Journal reported](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/trump-rewards-top-owners-of-his-declining-coin-with-exclusive-dinner-f610e583?mod=hp_lead_pos11):

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) rewarded top owners of his declining meme coin with [an exclusive dinner](https://gettrumpmemes.com/gala-dinner) Sunday [near Washington](https://www.trumpnationaldc.com/) featuring himself and a number of celebrities. […]

At the [dinner](https://gettrumpmemes.com/gala-dinner), the top 29 holders of his coin were expected to get access to a VIP reception with [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/). The top four were slated to obtain 18-karat gold [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) watches, provided they hold at least one million [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) coins — worth roughly $850,000.

According to the [dinner’s website](https://gettrumpmemes.com/gala-dinner), guests wouldn’t be able to privately meet with the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), though it’s unclear just how much access the meme coin owners were able to receive.

It’s worth emphasizing that the trajectory charting the value of the [\$TRUMP](https://gettrumpmemes.com/) meme coin resembles the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)’s approval rating — which is to say, its price has largely collapsed — which made tickets to Sunday’s exclusive dinner [far more affordable](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/11/18/invites-trump-memecoin-gala-dinner-are-surprisingly-cheap/) than the event in May 2025.

But the underlying concerns about corruption remain the same.

[MS NOW spoke to](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-meme-coin-crypto-gala) to a former [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) official, granted anonymity to speak candidly, who conceded that hosting a dinner intended to inflate his personal wealth gave [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) fresh political ammunition to further their successful midterm messaging that linked [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to corruption.

“Those of us who want to see this [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) succeed can only do so much. You can lead a horse to water, but you can’t make it drink,” the former official said.

In terms of possible investigations, [The New York Times reported](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/20/us/politics/cftc-cryptocurrency-trump-memecoin.html) that career officials at the [Commodity Futures Trading Commission](https://www.cftc.gov/) saw the developments as worthy of scrutiny — not surprisingly, they backed off, allowing [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to further enrich himself — though it’s likely that congressional [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) will be far less inclined to look the other way.

As [NBC News reported](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/trump-hosts-exclusive-dinner-investors-declining-meme-coin-rcna610757), nine [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) sent a letter to the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) last month demanding answers about the meme coin dinner, writing, “This [dinner](https://gettrumpmemes.com/gala-dinner) and scoring mechanism appear carefully constructed to increase investment in [\$TRUMP](https://gettrumpmemes.com/), allowing the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) to abuse the power of his office to personally enrich himself, as he has throughout this term.”

The [senators](https://www.senate.gov/) added, “[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s continued monetization of his name and likeness once again raises serious ethical concerns, necessitating this request for a robust inquiry into the [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s financial entanglements and personal profiteering while costs for the American people continue to climb.”

At the time, the [senators](https://www.senate.gov/) were in the minority, and their inquiries were ignored. In the new year, they’ll be in the majority, and future outreach will probably be in the form of a subpoena. Watch this space.
