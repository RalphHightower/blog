## [Trump finally addresses nationwide air traffic fiasco by blaming Biden](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-sean-duffy-air-traffic-nationwide-ground-stop)

_And [Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/) [Secretary Sean Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) refused to apologize “for what was passed off to us.”_

By [Erum Salam](https://www.ms.now/author/erum-salam)

Nov. 15, 2026, 12:50 PM EST

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) deflected blame Sunday for the weekend’s debilitating [nationwide ground stop](https://www.ms.now/news/faa-ground-stop-all-flights-nationwide), and [Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/) [Secretary Sean Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) declined to apologize.

In his first public message about the air travel meltdown, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) blamed his predecessor and praised the [Federal Aviation Administration](https://www.faa.gov/) under his leadership. He did not directly address what happened, or offer consolation to the hundreds of thousands of passengers left stranded.

“The [Biden Administration](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/) was an [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) disaster. Everything they touched turned bad,” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) wrote on social media, asking for more money from [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) to modernize the air traffic control system.

“Our [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) is doing a great job, and is ready to go,” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) added.

[Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) fanned out across network Sunday news shows to defend himself and praise the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/). Appearing on [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/)’s [“Meet the Press,”](https://www.nbcnews.com/meet-the-press-full-episodes) the transportation secretary said he’s “moving at an incredible speed” to upgrade an antiquated system.

“I’m proud of the work we’re doing, the attention we’re bringing to this issue,” [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) [said](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/transportation-secretary-apologize-nationwide-ground-stop-airports-rcna609397). “But I can’t apologize for what was passed off to us, and I can’t blame [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)s or [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)s because it’s all of them.”

The [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/)’s nationwide ground stop Friday paralyzed air travel across the country, canceling and delaying thousands of flights, upending travel plans and piquing concerns about the security — or lack thereof — of the nation’s infrastructure. The coast-to-coast shutdown — the first of its kind since the Sept. 11 attacks — was lifted after more than four hours, but major disruptions [persisted Sunday](https://uk.flightaware.com/live/cancelled/today).

[Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)ic lawmakers vowed to hold the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) accountable for its handling of the air space closure.

“With holiday travel around the corner, we must avoid chaos that leads to mass, unexpected travel delays,” [Reps.](https://www.house.gov/) [Rick Larsen (D-WA2)](https://larsen.house.gov/), ranking member on the [House](https://www.house.gov/) [Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure](https://transportation.house.gov/), and [André Carson (D-IN7)](https://carson.house.gov/), ranking member on the [aviation subcommittee](https://transportation.house.gov/subcommittees/subcommittee/?ID=107417), said in a statement Saturday.

The [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)s pointed out that the [GOP](https://www.gop.com/)-controlled [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) already approved \$12.5 billion for the [Department of Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/) to make necessary upgrades. They said the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) had spent just \$1 billion, leaving \$11 billion unspent.

“Explain to [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/), again, why they need more money?” [Larsen](https://larsen.house.gov/) and [Carson](https://carson.house.gov/) said.

The committee is expected to be briefed by [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) and [DOT](https://www.transportation.gov/) staff on Monday.

Officials on Sunday still had not pinpointed a root cause of the problem, but [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) said he believed the communications outage was caused by work at a remote [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/) facility to replace old copper lines with fiber-optic cables.

Asked if the disruptions could be linked to a potential cyber attack, [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) [told](https://www.ms.now/news/faa-sean-duffy-nationwide-ground-stop-flights-canceled) reporters Saturday “we don’t believe so.”

[Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [James Walkinshaw (D-VA11)](https://walkinshaw.house.gov/), a member of the [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/), which has vowed to aggressively investigate the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) when [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)s take control in January, said Sunday that [Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) needs to answer questions under oath before [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/). “Because I watched him in that press conference Friday night, and he was not being truthful with the American people,” he said on MS NOW’s “The Weekend.”

The congressman accused federal transportation officials of carelessly rushing upgrades. “Because the [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) wants it to be done before [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) leaves office,” [Walkinshaw](https://walkinshaw.house.gov/) said, questioning why the work was being conducted “on a busy travel day, as opposed to in the middle of the night where a disruption wouldn’t have been so damaging because they’re rushing.”

[Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/)-elect [Scott Colom](https://www.ms.now/news/news-analysis/scott-colom-mississippi-analysis) of [Mississippi](https://www.ms.gov/) was [caught up](https://x.com/scott_colom/status/2121831918131904712) in the chaos at the [Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport](https://www.atl.com/) Saturday night. He said his [Delta](https://www.delta.com/) flight “has been continuously delayed because they can’t find another pilot.”

“This is a hot mess for Americans. One of them just said, ‘pick another flight…pick another transportation secretary,’” he said, referencing his viral campaign ad poking fun at his opponent, outgoing [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Cindy Hyde-Smith (T-MS)](https://www.hydesmith.senate.gov/), for telling constituents they could pick another protein than red meat amid the rising cost of food.
