## [The Senate GOP wants to let Trump blind Congress’ watchdog](https://www.ms.now/opinion/senate-gop-gao-director-rush)

_[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) getting his handpicked [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) director confirmed would ensure that every spending bill [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) pass is treated as a suggestion._

By [Hayes Brown](https://www.ms.now/author/hayes-brown)

Nov. 16, 2026, 6:00 AM EST

[Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Rand Paul (T-KY)](https://www.paul.senate.gov/) has hit fast-forward on the search for the next leader of the [Government Accountability Office](https://www.ms.now/opinion/elon-musk-doge-report-gao-damage), which aids [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) in overseeing the executive branch. Confirming a new [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) director as the [GOP](https://www.gop.com/)’s majority wanes would be a tremendous gift to [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/). [Fights over federal spending](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/13/business/democrats-eye-strict-new-limits-on-white-house-budget-powers.html) will surely dominate the next two years and rushing through a pliant watchdog willing to turn a blind eye to [Trump’s blatant seizure of funds](https://www.ms.now/opinion/russell-vought-impeach-congress-spending) would only further enable his illegality.

The most recent comptroller general, [Gene Dodaro](https://www.gao.gov/about/what-gao-does/history/gene-dodaro/gene-dodaro-biography), retired last year when his 15-year term ended. [Orice Williams Brown](https://www.gao.gov/about/comptroller-general/biography) has served as the acting head of the agency since then with no apparent rush from the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) to fill the role. Under normal circumstances, [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/) would have convened [a 10-member bipartisan panel of lawmakers](https://www.gao.gov/about/comptroller-general) and spent the past year compiling a list of at least three names to present to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) as potential replacements.

> Confirming a new [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) director as the [GOP](https://www.gop.com/)’s majority wanes would be a tremendous gift to [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/).

But that hasn’t happened this time. Four [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) lawmakers — [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Minority Leader](https://www.senate.gov/about/parties-leadership/majority-minority-leaders.htm) [Chuck Schumer (D-NY)](https://www.schumer.senate.gov/) and [House](https://www.house.gov/) [Minority Leader](https://democraticleader.house.gov/) [Hakeem Jeffries (D-NY8)](https://jeffries.house.gov/), [Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee](http://www.hsgac.senate.gov/) ranking member [Gary Peters (D-MI)](https://www.peters.senate.gov/) and [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/) ranking member [Robert Garcia (D-CA42)](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/) — [issued a joint statement](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-schumer-jeffries-and-garcia-statement-on-sham-nomination-process-for-next-gao-leader/), calling [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/)’s gambit a “sham nomination process.”

“Counter to the last time a [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) [Comptroller](https://www.gao.gov/about/comptroller-general) was selected in 2010 in a yearlong, bipartisan process, [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) leaders have never convened the Commission for meetings, and the Commission has not interviewed a single candidate for this critical role, despite repeated requests to begin the process over the last two years,” the statement read, adding, “[Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/)’s office requested submissions of candidates to serve as the next [Comptroller](https://www.gao.gov/about/comptroller-general) [General](https://www.gao.gov/about/comptroller-general) of [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) with a deadline of less than 24 hours.”

The other members of the commission heading the search alongside [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/) and the four [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) are [Speaker](https://speaker.house.gov/) [Mike Johnson (T-LA4)](https://mikejohnson.house.gov/); [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [President Pro Tempore](https://www.senate.gov/about/officers-staff/president-pro-tempore.htm) [Chuck Grassley (T-IA)](https://www.grassley.senate.gov/); and [House Oversight Committee](https://oversight.house.gov/) [Chair](https://oversight.house.gov/chairman-james-comer/) [James Comer (T-KY1)](https://comer.house.gov/) Past searches for comptroller general have yielded bipartisan recommendations, but with six seats on the panel, any decision the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)s make can be sent to [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) over [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/)’ objections.

Incredibly, [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/) has already [included a placeholder nominee on the agenda](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/hearings/nominsations/) on the agenda for a confirmation hearing set for Thursday alongside nine other key nominations [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) hope to rush through the lame duck. [The Washington Sun reported last month](https://www.washingtonsun.com/whitehouse/trump-floats-russ-vought-deputy-lead-congress-watchdog-gao-ueland) that the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) would prefer [Eric Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/)’s name atop the recommendations list. [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) currently serves as deputy director at the [Office of Management and Budget](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/). His boss, [Russell Vought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Vought?wprov=sfla), has been the [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/)’s enemy No. 1 for withholding money that [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) has ordered to be spent.

The [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) has already ruled multiple times since last January that [the Trump administration has acted illegally](https://www.ms.now/opinion/susan-collins-midterms-trump-spending-cuts) with its impoundments and other unilateral spending cuts. Most recently, [Vought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Vought?wprov=sfla) and [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) executed a move known as a “pocket rescission,” which [the GAO has also expressly said is illegal](https://www.politico.com/f/?id=000001a0-ee05-dfdd-afef-ee1f85250000), clawing back roughly \$1 billion in otherwise approved spending. [Vought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Vought?wprov=sfla) has repeatedly denounced the [1974 Impoundment Control Act](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/COMPS-10356/uslm/COMPS-10356.xml)’s legal authority and ignored the [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/)’s nonbinding findings.

The [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) does have a way to force the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s compliance by [taking the administration to court](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/09/26/supreme-court-foreign-aid-impoundment-ruling-00583052) — but acting comptroller [Brown](https://www.gao.gov/about/comptroller-general/biography) has yet to pull the trigger. Her hesitation has even led some [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) lawmakers to want her to act, unlike [her former boss, Dodaro, who had hesitated](https://talkingpointsmemo.com/news/one-agency-has-been-calling-out-trumps-illegal-impoundment-that-may-soon-change) in his final year on the job. “They are empowered to sue,” [Sen.](https://www.senate.gov/) [Susan Collins (T-ME)](https://www.collins.senate.gov/), the outgoing [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Appropriations Committee](http://www.appropriations.senate.gov/) [chair](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/about/chair), [told reporters in October](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/09/gao-trump-comptroller-general-funding-01113223). “I think the biggest problem is that we have an acting director of the [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/), who understandably is wary of suing the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) without direct congressional approval.”

With that kind of authority, it’s no wonder the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) wouldn’t want to miss the chance to install a loyalist like [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/). A [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/)ic aide to the [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Homeland Security Committee](http://www.hsgac.senate.gov/) told [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/agency-oversight/2026/11/senate-gop-set-up-lame-duck-effort-to-appoint-new-comptroller-general/) that the “[GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) received ethics paperwork for [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) [last week], prior to [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/)’s request for candidate submissions.” In other words, the fix is in and any names [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) would have presented would have been instantly thrown in the garbage.

According to the same [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) aide, [Schumer](https://www.schumer.senate.gov/), [Jeffries](https://jeffries.house.gov/), [Peters](https://www.peters.senate.gov/) and [Garcia](https://robertgarcia.house.gov/) refused to comply with [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/)’s rush job:

> The aide said no names were submitted, because that’s “not how the process works.”

> “The submission of names is the culmination of the process,” the aide said, adding that [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/)’s office has ignored repeated requests from [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) staff to initiate the commission process over the last two years.

The incoming [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) majority would be able to restart the search process [Paul](https://www.paul.senate.gov/) attempted to let atrophy. A proper list of nominees, which could even include a [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) choice, could then be sent to the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/). [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) would still be able to name [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) as his nominee — but there’s no chance that [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) would confirm him.

> With that kind of authority, it’s no wonder the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) wouldn’t want to miss the chance to install a loyalist like [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/).

As things stand, given [the narrow vote approving](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1191/vote_119_1_00256.htma) him to become [Vought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Vought?wprov=sfla)’s deputy, there may not even be enough votes in the lame-duck session to hurry him into the role. [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)s should see the folly in letting a man who would gladly see [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ power of the purse usurped be given such a position — but that feels like a bridge too far. [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) must then ensure that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is not allowed to “install a fox in the henhouse” at [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/), as the sidelined commission’s [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) put it in their statement last week, and block any attempt to advance the [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/) director alongside other nominees, especially if [Ueland](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-ueland-0a13506/) is [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s pick.

Whatever other methods of stalling, delaying or otherwise denying a vote on this crucial position before January will be necessary to ensure the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) doesn’t see the next [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ spending bills merely as suggestions.
