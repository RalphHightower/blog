## [DHS’ Mullin took government jet to LSU-Texas college football game](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/markwayne-mullin-flight-government-planes-cabinet-college-football-rubio-kennedy)

_During [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s first term, several [Cabinet secretaries](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/the-cabinet/) faced controversies for their use of government airplanes. Years later, it’s happening again._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Nov. 17, 2026, 1:08 PM EST

On Friday, for only the second time since the [Sept. 11 terrorist attacks](https://www.ms.now/news/sept-11-lessons-unlearned), American air travel came to a halt. There was a nearly five-hour ground stop that disrupted air travel across the country, generating thousands of canceled and delayed flights.

Shortly after the ground stop was implemented, [Homeland Security](https://www.dhs.gov/) [Secretary Markwayne Mullin](https://www.dhs.gov/markwayne-mullin) hosted a rather clumsy [nine-minute press briefing](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-press-briefing-markwayne-mullin-the-white-house-november-13-2026/) just outside the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), where he said very little — though he [did take care to remind a reporter](https://bsky.app/profile/atrupar.com/post/3n27ypzrmx62v) that the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) expected people to refer to artificial intelligence as “SI,” as if that were what really mattered at the time. That was soon followed by a longer press conference in which [Mullin](https://www.dhs.gov/markwayne-mullin)’s rhetoric [was so bizarre](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/duffy-mullin-trump-air-travel-ground-stop-faa-blame-game) that [Politico’s John F. Harris questioned](https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2026/11/14/air-traffic-control-debacle-trump-column-01166616) questioned whether he and [Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/) [Secretary Sean Duffy](https://www.transportation.gov/meet-secretary/us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy) had “elementary self-awareness.”

A day later, however, as thousands of people across the country struggled to recover from Friday’s breakdown, [Mullin](https://www.dhs.gov/markwayne-mullin) apparently found a way to enjoy himself. [The New York Post reported](https://nypost.com/2026/11/17/us-news/markwayne-mullin-took-his-government-plane-to-lsu-texas-game-as-thousands-were-stranded-in-faa-ground-stoppage/) that the homeland security secretary took a government plane to [Baton Rouge](https://www.brla.gov/), [Louisiana](https://www.louisiana.gov/), on Saturday to attend the [Texas](https://texaslonghorns.com/)-[LSU](7) college football game. From the article:

> It’s unclear whether [Mullin](https://www.dhs.gov/markwayne-mullin) has any specific ties to either [Texas](https://www.utexas.edu/) or [LSU](https://www.lsu.edu/), but he was spotted during the game in the private box of [Ben Bordelon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ben-bordelon/), the CEO of [Louisiana](https://www.louisiana.gov/)-based [Bollinger Shipping](https://www.bollingershipyards.com/), which has received billions of dollars in [DHS](https://www.dhs.gov/) contracts — including a recent \$2.2 billion deal to build six icebreakers.

While [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/) didn’t independently verify the reporting, [Mullin](https://www.dhs.gov/markwayne-mullin) himself appeared on [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/) on Tuesday morning and confirmed all of the relevant details.

After noting that he was required to take government planes and emphasizing that the ground stop was lifted ahead of his flight, the secretary said he took the excursion because “we still got to live our lives.”

[Mullin](https://www.dhs.gov/markwayne-mullin) also acknowledged that he did, in fact, attend the game while sitting with an executive who has done business with the [Department of Homeland Security](https://www.dhs.gov/), though he added that he did not previously know [Bordelon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ben-bordelon/) and “there was never business spoke about” on Saturday.

Time will tell whether the story generates additional scrutiny, though it’s tough not to notice the familiarity of the circumstances. It was just last week, for example, when we learned that [Secretary of](https://www.state.gov/biographies/marco-rubio/) [State](https://www.state.gov/) [Marco Rubio](https://www.state.gov/biographies/marco-rubio/) [keeps using government planes](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/rubio-military-jets-college-football-travel-florida) to attend college football games (his son is on the [University of Florida](https://www.ufl.edu/) [team](https://floridagators.com/sports/football/), though he rarely plays), which came on the heels of related revelations about [Health](https://www.hhs.gov/) [Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](https://www.hhs.gov/about/leadership/robert-kennedy.html), who ignored ethics restrictions and hit the campaign trail ahead of Election Day, and who used [costly military aircraft](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/kennedy-hhs-military-planes-aircraft-rfk-jr-campaign-flights-midterm-elections) for at least some of his travels.

During [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s first term as president, a surprising number of [Cabinet secretaries](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/the-cabinet) were caught up in related controversies, including then-[Health](https://www.hhs.gov/) [Secretary Tom Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20170214003824/https://www.hhs.gov/about/leadership/secretary/thomas-e-price-md/index.html), who was forced to resign just seven months into his tenure after revelations that he relied on taxpayer-financed private flights with unnerving frequency.

A year later, the public learned that then-[Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/) Secretary [Steven Mnuchin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/steven-mnuchin-bb805675/) had [relied on military flights](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/treasurys-mnuchin-racks-sky-high-costs-taxpayer-funded-travel-msna1079481) for reasons the [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) struggled to explain. In addition, then-[Interior](https://www.doi.gov/) Secretary [Ryan Zinke](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryan-zinke-b3b34416/) faced [a plane-related controversy](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/another-trump-cabinet-secretary-under-investigation-msna1025826).

Now, during [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s second term, everything is up in the air again.
