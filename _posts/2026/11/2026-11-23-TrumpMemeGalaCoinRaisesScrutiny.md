## [Trump’s meme coin gala raises eyebrows — even in MAGA circles](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-meme-coin-crypto-gala)

_Fresh off a resounding election rebuke, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) is offering top [\$TRUMP](https://gettrumpmemes.com/) holders special access._

By [Jake Traylor](https://www.ms.now/author/jake-traylor)

Nov. 22, 2026, 6:00 AM EST

[President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) is set to host another gala Sunday night to reward investors in his [crypto meme coin venture](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-meme-coin-crypto-access), an affair likely to attract fresh attention from [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) poised to investigate the ways in which the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) has enriched himself during his second term.

But the third-of-its-kind event, hosted at [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s [Northern Virginia golf club](https://www.trumpnationaldc.com/), isn’t exactly a hot ticket. Nor is it popular among some in the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s inner circle.

Much like his political stock, the value of [the president’s digital token](https://www.ms.now/news/news-analysis/trump-economy-billionaire-bubble) has deteriorated.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s first meme coin dinner, in April 2025, saw investors spending some \$148 million in a contest to dine with the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), according to the cryptocurrency intelligence firm [Inca Digital.](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/buyers-trump-meme-coin-pay-millions-win-dinner-with-president-trump-2025-05-12/). Sunday’s dinner, which invites the top 185 investors in the [\$TRUMP](https://gettrumpmemes.com/) coin, showed closing numbers of only \$8.6 million, according to a [Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/interest-trumps-meme-coin-dinner-fades-trump-sinks-record-lows-2026-11-16/).

The dramatic drop-off is partly a reflection of the fact that the coin itself has drastically declined in value. When it launched in January 2025, the token was trading at \$74. Its value tumbled, but after the announcement of the first [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) meme coin dinner that April, the coin surged from roughly \$7 to \$14.

There was no significant uptick following the announcement of [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s third gala, with the coin now trading at a low of approximately 85 cents.[^TRUMP]

[^TRUMP]: @RalphHightower That valuation is in penny stock range

It’s also a move that even some allies in [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s orbit, in conversations with [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/), described as a self-inflicted wound — hosting a dinner intended to inflate his personal wealth, giving [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) fresh ammo to further their successful midterm messaging that linked [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to corruption.

“At this point, it’s pretty clear the [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) doesn’t see this as a vulnerability, even after the message voters sent on Election Day,” said a former [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) official granted anonymity to speak candidly.

The [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) did not immediately respond to a request for comment about criticism from the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s own loyalists that the gala is a politically tone-deaf idea that provides more fodder to [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/) who seized control of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/), largely on an anti-corruption platform.

“Those of us who want to see this [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) succeed can only do so much. You can lead a horse to water, but you can’t make it drink,” the former official said.

[Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s net worth rose by roughly \$3 billion in one year following his return to office, according to [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/). The [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) made the bulk of that fortune from cryptocurrency, with his meme coin earning over $600 million, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)’s 2025 financial disclosures show.

Sunday night’s event, dubbed [“The Most Exclusive Dinner in the World”](https://gettrumpmemes.com/gala-dinner) by the [\$TRUMP coin website](https://gettrumpmemes.com/), has invited scrutiny from government watchdogs and lawmakers from both parties, including [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) allies.

Leading public ethics monitors, including [Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington](https://www.citizensforethics.org/), [Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/), [Indivisible](https://indivisible.org/) and [Common Cause](https://www.commoncause.org/), issued a joint statement last week calling on [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to cancel the dinner and on [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) to “require [presidents](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) to fully divest from their crypto businesses, bar their adult children from using their proximity to the presidency to advance crypto ventures, and let state attorneys general enforce those rules in court.”

In a recent interview with [“60 Minutes,”](https://www.cbsnews.com/60-minutes/) [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) denied wrongdoing in profiting from the presidency.

“I devote almost no time to my net worth. I don’t care,” [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) said.

The [Democratic Party](https://www.democrats.org/), which swept both chambers of [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/) in the midterm election’s colossal rebuke of the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), highlighted the meme coin dinner in a Friday [press release](https://democrats.org/corruption-watch-trump-hosts-exclusive-dinner-for-buyers-of-his-meme-coin-to-line-his-pockets-as-americans-struggle-to-make-ends-meet/), calling it a way for [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) to “line his pockets as Americans struggle to make ends meet.”

The former [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) official called the release “effective messaging.”

Jake Traylor is a [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) reporter for [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/).
