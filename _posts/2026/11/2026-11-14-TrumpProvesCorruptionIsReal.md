## [Why Trump’s defense against corruption allegations is plainly self-defeating](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-corruption-democrats-investigation-president-salary-profitting)

_The proof the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) came up with to show his lack of corruption is actually evidence pointing toward his apparent corruption._

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Nov. 12, 2026, 9:48 AM EST

To a degree unseen since Watergate, [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)ic officials and candidates emphasized government corruption as a central issue in the midterm elections. It proved effective: Polls showed voters [prioritizing corruption](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/corruption-politics-midterm-elections-democrats-republicans-trump) in ways that likely caught [Republican](https://www.gop.com/)s off guard.

There’s no great mystery as to why: [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s corruption was so uniquely brazen that it helped the issue break through for the electorate. As writer [Brian Beutler summarized](https://www.offmessage.net/p/donald-trump-republican-corruption-crime-spree-iran-war-insider-trading-white-house-ballroom-bribery-crypto-cryptocurrency-artificial-intelligence-ai-teapot-dome-watergate-richard-nixon-george-w-bush) a few weeks before Election Day, in reference to the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), “He isn’t just more corrupt than most politicians; corruption is his raison d’être. This isn’t just ‘government corruption’ to be reformed away. It’s a [GOP](https://www.gop.com/) crime spree.”

After [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)s were able to capitalize on the backlash to widespread corruption, the party took the obvious next step: It started making plans to use [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ oversight authority to investigate years’ worth of wrongdoing.

This, by all appearances, has sparked [widespread panic](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/blumenthal-ballroom-reitz-prosecutor-white-house-justice-department-wartime-footing) in [the White House](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/white-house-scarf-garcia-oversight-investigations-letter-threat) and throughout the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [administration](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/) — according to whistleblowers, at least one [Cabinet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/the-cabinet/) agency has [begun destroying documents](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/interior-whistleblowers-oversight-house-natural-resources-committee), in the hopes of keeping them from lawmakers — and the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) himself appears to be increasingly anxious about the potential for sunlight on activities he has long preferred to keep in the shadows.

To that end, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) spent the first weekend after the midterms accusing the incoming [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) majority of being [“extortionists” who deserve “imprisonment,”](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-blanche-democrats-oversight-investigations-justice-department) and [ordering his Justice Department](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trump-blanche-democrats-oversight-investigations-justice-department) to “look into” the [Democrats](https://www.democrats.org/)’ “totally illegal” plans to conduct routine oversight.

The [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) used his social media platform on Thursday morning to publish a series of related missives, each of which was rooted in the idea that it’s his [Democrat](https://www.democrats.org/)ic opponents who are actually corrupt. One online item, published at 5:50 a.m. ET, [included this gem](https://www.trumpstruth.org/statuses/43554):

> I, perhaps foolishly because the Fake News refuses to write about it, including in my [First Term](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/), don’t even take a Salary, worth Millions of Dollars. I am the only [President](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) to have done so! In any event, THE DEMOCRATS ARE CORRUPT AND EVIL, and I am the exact opposite! Fight hard, [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/), or you won’t have a Country left!

In the hours that followed, the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) continued to [publish](https://www.trumpstruth.org/statuses/43555) a [series](https://www.trumpstruth.org/statuses/43558) of related [posts](https://www.trumpstruth.org/statuses/43559), all of which insisted that those concerned about corruption need not bother looking at him.

It’s unlikely that anyone will be persuaded by such nonsense, though I was struck by the nature of his defense: To hear [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) tell it, he can’t be corrupt because he doesn’t accept his [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) salary.

He’s going to need a better talking point.

Right off the bat, let’s note that the base annual salary for an American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) is \$400,000, which is not “Millions of Dollars.” It’s also worth mentioning that [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) keeps telling people he’s the only [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) to ever decline his official salary, but that’s still not true: [Herbert Hoover](https://www.philanthropyroundtable.org/hall-of-fame/herbert-hoover/) and [John F. Kennedy](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1962/11/14/JFK-Donates-Full-Salary-To-Charity/3714224962880/) also donated their [presidential](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) salaries.

But even if we put these details aside, what [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) doesn’t seem to fully appreciate is the fact that he doesn’t need the \$400,000 a year because he’s already profiting off the presidency.

As a candidate for the nation’s highest office, [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) [vowed to reject](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trum-defends-massive-crypto-profits-white-house) “the politics of personal profit” and to stand up to those who had gone to the nation’s capital and “lined their pockets.” But as a [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/), [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) has practically sent his pants out to the tailors to get bigger pockets.

In the first year of his second term, the [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [pulled in at least \$2.2 billion](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/trum-defends-massive-crypto-profits-white-house), including [more than \$1.4 billion](https://www.ms.now/news/trump-financial-disclosure-crypto-income) in cryptocurrency-related income, according to his latest annual financial disclosure.

No other [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) in American history has [ever seen such financial gains](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/07/01/trumps-wealth-grew-scale-without-modern-presidential-precedent/) while in office, and as a recent analysis from [The New York Times explained](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/02/world/europe/trump-world-leaders-corruption-wealth.html), “[Mr. Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s profits are a haul once unimaginable for any leader of a liberal democracy, particularly a sitting American [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/). No modern Western leader has ever publicly disclosed such big windfalls while in office. The [Trump family](https://www.trump.com/)’s earnings, experts said, have moved him into an echelon of enrichment more associated with strongmen in [Russia](http://kremlin.ru/) and [Turkey](https://global.tbmm.gov.tr/).”

In other words, the proof the [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) came up with to show his lack of corruption is actually evidence pointing toward his apparent corruption.
