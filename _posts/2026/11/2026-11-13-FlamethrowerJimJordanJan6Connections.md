## [As Jim Jordan prepares to lead House Republicans, his Jan. 6 record is relevant anew](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/jordan-house-republicans-minority-leader-january-six)

_[Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/), [Liz Cheney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Cheney?wprov=sfla1) said, “knew more about what [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had planned for [January 6](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0W4zBOS45K_NAfZ-MFphiK1y73LVa05q&si=K4nlayVIqO7Huxq9) than any other member of the [House of Representatives](https://www.house.gov/).”_

By [Steve Benen](https://www.ms.now/author/steve-benen)

Nov. 11, 2026, 12:41 PM EST

In an era in which political rhetoric can get quite heated, it’s still unusual to hear prominent American leaders use words like “terrorist” to criticize other officials. But as recently as 2021, former [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [House](https://www.house.gov/) [Speaker](https://speaker.house.gov/) [John Boehner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Boehner?wprov=sfla1) nevertheless described [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [Rep.](https://www.house.gov/) [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) as a political “[terrorist](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/asked-about-political-terrorists-gop-boehner-names-names-n1263811).”

[Boehner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Boehner?wprov=sfla1) [added](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/john-boehner-on-how-the-rise-of-ideologues-harms-america/), in reference to his fellow [Ohioan](https://ohio.gov/), “I just never saw a guy who spent more time tearing things apart, never building anything, never putting anything together.”

There’s no shortage of reasons why I’ve long described [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) as [Congress](https://www.congress.gov/)’ worst member. From his [Ohio State scandal](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/former-ohio-state-wrestlers-balk-jim-jordans-bid-speaker-rcna119891) to his conspiracy theories, from his [failed and pointless investigations](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/jim-jordan-house-minority-leader-republicans-congress) to the fact that he’s [quite literally never passed a bill](https://www.congress.gov/member/jim-jordan/J000289?q=%7B%22sponsorship%22%3A%22sponsored%22%2C%22congress%22%3A%22all%22%2C%22bill-status%22%3A%22passed-one%22%7D) despite having been on Capitol Hill for nearly 20 years, from his total indifference toward governing to his obsessive [appearances on Faux News](https://www.mediamatters.org/fox-news/fox-news-caucus-new-house-gop-leader-jim-jordan-has-made-least-716-weekday-appearances-fox), [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) is a uniquely odious political figure.

He was nevertheless easily elected by [House](https://www.house.gov/) [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) [to serve as the next House minority leader](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/jim-jordan-house-minority-leader-republicans-congress).

To appreciate why this is such a dramatic mistake, consider [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/)’s record related to [Jan. 6](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0W4zBOS45K_NAfZ-MFphiK1y73LVa05q&si=K4nlayVIqO7Huxq9).

Around this time five years ago, [The New York Times reported](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/12/15/us/politics/trump-meadows-republicans-congress-jan-6.html) that the far-right congressman attended crisis meetings at [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s campaign headquarters as early as Nov. 9, 2020, just two days after [Joe Biden](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/) became president-elect. [The Times](https://www.nytimes.com/) [separately reported](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/07/21/us/politics/jim-banks-jim-jordan.html) that [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) also participated in [a meeting at the White House](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2020/12/21/us/joe-biden-trump#trump-meets-with-house-republicans-to-discuss-a-far-fetched-effort-to-overturn-the-election-results) in late December 2020, at which he plotted with the defeated [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) [president](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) on how best to challenge the election results.

Soon after, on Jan. 5, [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) [forwarded a message](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/jim-jordan-concedes-he-sent-controversial-jan-5-text-meadows-n1286090) to then-[White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/) chief of staff [Mark Meadows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Meadows?wprov=sfla1), describing a scheme in which then-Vice President [Mike Pence](https://advancingamericanfreedom.com/about/vice-president-mike-pence) could help reject election results [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) didn’t like.

Former [House](https://www.house.gov/) [Republican](https://www.gop.com/) Conference Chair [Liz Cheney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Cheney?wprov=sfla1) [summarized matters succinctly](https://news.yahoo.com/liz-cheney-warns-jim-jordan-140732464.html): “[Jim Jordan (T-OH4)](https://jordan.house.gov/) knew more about what [Donald Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/) had planned for [Jan. 6](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0W4zBOS45K_NAfZ-MFphiK1y73LVa05q&si=K4nlayVIqO7Huxq9) than any other member of the [House of Representatives](https://www.house.gov/).”

With this in mind, the bipartisan [Jan. 6 Committee](https://www.govinfo.gov/committee/house-january6th?path=/browsecommittee/chamber/house/committee/january6th/collection/CRPT), which [Cheney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Cheney?wprov=sfla1) helped lead, subpoenaed [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) to provide additional information. After [initially insisting](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/jan-6-jim-jordan-abandons-nothing-hide-position-rcna30619) that he had “nothing to hide,” the [GOP](https://www.gop.com) congressman ultimately [refused to comply](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/despite-defying-his-own-lawful-subpoena-rep-jim-jordan-has-issued-at-least-91-subpoenas-to-others/) with the subpoena.

A year later, [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) [very nearly became](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/jim-jordans-speaker-bid-gone-shouldnt-forgotten-rcna121651) the speaker of the [House](https://www.house.gov/), and as that process unfolded, [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/)’s [Ron Brownstein](https://www.theatlantic.com/author/ronald-brownstein/) wrote [a memorable piece](https://www.theatlantic.com/politics/archive/2023/10/us-house-democracy-threat-republican-speaker-race/675679/) (published before [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/)’s bid collapsed) that raised a point that continues to ring true:

> [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/)’s rise … provides more evidence that for the first time since the [Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War?wprov=sfla1), the dominant faction in one of America’s two major parties is no longer committed to the principles of democracy as the U.S. has known them. That means the nation now faces the possibility of sustained threats to the tradition of free and fair elections, with [Trump](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/)’s own antidemocratic tendencies not only tolerated but amplified by his allies across the party.

[Jena Griswold](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jenagriswold/), [Colorado](https://www.colorado.gov/)’s [Democratic](https://www.democrats.org/) [secretary of state](https://www.sos.state.co.us/) who was elected as [state attorney general](https://coag.gov/) last week, told [Brownstein](https://www.theatlantic.com/author/ronald-brownstein/) that “‘undermining the election’ is too soft a language” to describe [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/)’s activities in 2020.

“He was involved in every step to try to destroy American democracy and the peaceful transfer of the presidency,” she said.

If [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) were to become speaker, [Griswold](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jenagriswold/) added, “you could no longer count on the speaker of the [House](https://www.house.gov/) to defend the [United States Constitution](https://constitution.congress.gov/).”

A responsible, healthy political party would have laughed uproariously at the very idea of elevating [Jordan](https://jordan.house.gov/) to a powerful leadership position. The fact that 106 [Republicans](https://www.gop.com/) supported his bid to become [House](https://www.house.gov/) minority leader anyway is genuinely scandalous.
